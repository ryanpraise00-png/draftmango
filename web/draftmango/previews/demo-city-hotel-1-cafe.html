Lume · Ground Floor

Cooked over oak, served from seven till late.

A long concrete bar, an open-fire kitchen and tall windows onto the street. Breakfast for the house, lunch and dinner for the whole city.

Rui Barata · Head Chef

The fire is lit at six every morning.

Rui cooked along the coast for ten years before Lume. Everything here passes over oak embers, from the breakfast eggs to the sea bream, and the bread comes out of the same oven at dawn.

Private Dining & Meetings

A long table by the fire, or a room upstairs.
