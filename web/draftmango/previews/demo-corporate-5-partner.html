Victoria Ashford

Twenty years of portfolio and succession decisions for boards in chemicals, industrials and family-owned groups.

Profile

She asks the question the board has been avoiding.

Victoria joined Draftmango Strategy Partners in 2006, two years after it opened, and led the firm's first portfolio mandate that autumn. She has since led more than 140 mandates, most of them for boards deciding what to own and who should run it.

She sits as a standing adviser to nine boards, and is the partner chairs call when a succession and a deal arrive in the same year. Before Draftmango she spent eight years in corporate development at an industrial group.

MBA, Columbia Business School. BA in Economics, Georgetown University.
