The Formula

No magic, three numbers.

A starting point that has held up across 6,800 Valley homes. Shade, roof angle and your rate plan move it up or down.

  1. ÷ 18

    System size

    About 1 kW of panels for every $18 of monthly bill, on a Phoenix roof facing south or west.

  2. 85%

    Of the bill saved

    The rest is the utility’s fixed charge and the nights you draw from the grid.

  3. 7–9 years

    Payback

    For a cash purchase. After that, the panels work for you for another sixteen years of warranty and more.
