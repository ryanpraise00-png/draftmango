A new building, forty feet from an open emergency room.

Mercy Ridge Health needed 48,000 sq ft of imaging and outpatient surgery on the only free corner of its campus, beside an emergency department that sees 190 patients a day. Ambulances could not be rerouted, and the hospital’s oxygen and power had to stay live through every tie-in.

We joined at schematic design. Jennifer Cole’s team priced the building at every stage, and our superintendents marked up the set before the details were drawn: $2.4 million came out of the design before ground broke, most of it in the façade and the structure.

On site, the crane swung only over our own fence, deliveries ran at night, and eleven utility tie-ins were planned with the clinical staff weeks ahead. Not one was unplanned. We signed an $86 million guaranteed maximum price and returned $1.1 million of it.
