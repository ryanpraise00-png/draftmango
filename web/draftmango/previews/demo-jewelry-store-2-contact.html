Shipping, Returns & Care

The small print, in plain words

Still unsure? Write to hello@draftmangoatelier.com and a person who works at the bench will answer.

Delivery is free and insured on every US order, signed for on arrival. Pieces in stock leave us in one or two business days and arrive two to four days later; made-to-order pieces and engagement rings take four weeks at the bench. Overnight is $35. We ship to Canada for $45, insured, with duties shown at checkout.

Yes, within 30 days of delivery, insured both ways with a free label from your account. Your refund reaches you within five business days of the piece arriving back. Engraved and made-to-order pieces cannot be refunded, but we will resize or remake them.

Cleaning, polishing, claw checks and re-plating of white gold, free, for as long as you own the piece, and resizing up to two sizes. Drop it at King Street or post it to us insured; it comes back in about a week.

It happens to half of all proposals. Request a label and we resize it free, usually within a week. Eternity bands are the exception: their stones go all the way round, so we remake them in the new size at cost.

Every piece goes out in our sand box with a clay ribbon. Add a gift note at checkout and we write it by hand; gift receipts never show the price.
