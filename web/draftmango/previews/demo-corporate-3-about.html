Our story

A bookkeeping desk on Congress Avenue.

Draftmango opened in 2006 with three clients and one promise: the books would close on time, every month. The clients grew, and kept asking the same question — could you also run payroll? our laptops? the state filings?

So we built five teams under one roof and kept the rule that made the first clients stay: one named lead who knows your business and answers the phone.

  • Employee-owned since 2019
  • Every specialist in-house
  • Clients in 46 states
  • No offshore handoffs
