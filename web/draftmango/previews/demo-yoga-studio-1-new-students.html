Your First Visit

Four steps, no surprises.

Most people are nervous the first time. By the second class it is just Tuesday.

  1. 01

    Before you come

    Book any Foundations or All Levels class online. Eat lightly two hours before and bring water.

  2. 02

    Arrive ten minutes early

    We show you the rooms, the cubbies and where the props live. Doors close five minutes after start.

  3. 03

    In the room

    Take the spot nearest the window, rest whenever you like, and tell the teacher about any injury.

  4. 04

    After class

    Tea by the window, a question for the teacher, and your next class booked if you liked it.
