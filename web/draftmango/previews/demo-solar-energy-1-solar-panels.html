The System

More than the panels.

What sits under the array and on the garage wall decides how much you make and how easily you see it.

  • Under Every Panel

    Microinverters

    Each panel works on its own, so a shaded corner or a dusty module never drags down the rest. On simple, sunny roofs we may use one string inverter instead.

  • Production in your pocket

    Live and yearly output per panel in the app; we watch it too and call if something drops.
