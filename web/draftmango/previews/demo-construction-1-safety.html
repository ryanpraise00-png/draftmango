Eight habits. On every site, every shift.

The same rules for our crews and every subcontractor on our jobs. They are in the subcontract, and they are walked every day.

  • Stop-Work AuthorityAnyone on site, apprentice or president, can stop the work. No one is questioned for it.
  • Daily Pre-Task PlansEvery crew walks the day’s hazards before it picks up a tool.
  • OSHA 30 and 10OSHA 30 for every superintendent; OSHA 10 for every field employee in their first 90 days.
  • 100% Tie-OffAbove six feet, everyone is tied off. No exceptions, no “just a minute”.
  • Near-Miss ReportsAnonymous if you want, and answered by the safety team within 24 hours.
  • Heat PlansWater, shade and rest set by the heat index, from May to September.
  • WellnessFatigue limits on long pours and a confidential counseling line for every family.
  • Safety WeekEvery May, every site stops for a morning of training and a cookout.
Rule 04Above 6 ft

Tied off, or not up there.

Falls are the first cause of death in our industry. On our jobs, anchor points are designed into the steel before it is detailed, and a rescue plan is posted before anyone climbs.

  • Anchor points drawn into the steel shop drawings
  • Harnesses inspected and tagged every month
  • A written rescue plan for every elevated task
