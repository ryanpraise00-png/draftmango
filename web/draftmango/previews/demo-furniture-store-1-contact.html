Good to Know

Delivery, the trial and the warranty.

Delivery 100-Night Trial Warranty & Care

Delivery

Two people carry it to the room you choose, unwrap it, build it and take every bit of packaging away. It's free on orders over $1,500 and $149 under that, anywhere in the contiguous US.

Four to ten weeks depending on the fabric — each product page shows the time for the fabric you pick. Lamps, tables and decor marked Ready to Ship leave within two weeks.

Every product page lists the narrowest doorway it needs. Send us a photo of a tricky staircase and we'll check before you order — our sofas carry on end through a 30-inch door.

100-Night Trial

Live with any sofa, sectional or bed for 100 nights. If it isn't right, call us: we collect it free and refund you in full — custom fabrics included. Returned pieces go to our Second Home program.

Lighting, tables and decor have 30 nights for a free return, collected from your door or dropped at any showroom.

Warranty & Care

Frames and springs for ten years, cushions and fabric for two. If something fails we repair it in the workshop or replace it, and we pay for the collection.

Vacuum weekly with the upholstery brush and blot spills right away with a damp cloth and a drop of mild soap. Cushion covers zip off for professional cleaning.
