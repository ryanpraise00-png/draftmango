How We Work

One plan, agreed in week one.

Every engagement starts with a written plan: the team, the dates, the fee and what we need from you.

  1. 01

    Scope

    A partner meeting to understand the year, the risks and the deadlines that matter to you.

  2. 02

    Plan

    A written plan with the named team, a fixed fee and the list of documents, sent within five days.

  3. 03

    Fieldwork

    Interim work before year end, a weekly status note, and issues raised the week we find them.

  4. 04

    Sign-off

    The opinion, the return or the report on the agreed date, then a debrief to plan next year.
