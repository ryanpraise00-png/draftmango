About Draftmango

Fourteen people who still answer the support inbox.

We build Draftmango in Austin for product teams like the ones we used to run: small, busy and buried in good ideas from customers.

See Open Roles

Our Story

It started with a spreadsheet only Lea understood.

Six years of customer requests in one tab, colour-coded by a system nobody else could read. Sam, two desks away, kept building features nobody could prove anyone wanted.

So we built the tool we kept wishing for: count every request once, show everyone what's next, and tell each person who asked when it ships. We still run Draftmango on Draftmango, and our own board decides most of what we build.

  1. 2024Two founders, one boardLea and Sam leave their jobs and ship a first version to twelve friendly teams.
  2. 2025An $8M seed roundLed by people who have run product teams themselves. The team grows to fourteen.
  3. 2026Public betaAnyone can sign up. 12,400 teams so far, and the board has never been busier.

Three rules we try not to break.

They came from our own mistakes as product people. They still settle most arguments.

  • 01

    Count, then decide

    Opinions are welcome. Before we argue about one, we look at who asked and how many of them there are.

  • 02

    Ship small, ship Thursday

    A change a week beats a launch a quarter. If it can't ship by Thursday, we cut it until it can.

  • 03

    Everyone answers support

    Founders and engineers included, two hours a week each. It is the fastest way to know what to build.
