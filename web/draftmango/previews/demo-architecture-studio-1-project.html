Saltbox House

A house for winter mornings on eleven acres of meadow and white pines above the Roeliff Jansen Kill.

01 / Project Sheet

Program Private house, four bedrooms
Place Columbia County, NY
Area 3,400 sq ft
Design 2021–22
Completed 2024
Team Clara Weiss, partner in charge · Mara Lind · Owen Hale
Structure Fieldwork Engineering
Builder Kinderhook Builders
Award Residential Design Honor, 2024

One long roof turned to the north wind, the rooms gathered under its low side.

The Brief

A family of four leaving the city for good asked for a house that would be warm and bright in January, with a room where everyone could be together and a corner for each of them to be alone.

The Response

A timber frame on a concrete plinth, wrapped in charred larch. The steep north slope sheds snow and wind; the shallow south slope opens the living room to the low winter sun and the pines.

  • Ground-source heat pump, no fossil fuel on site
  • Triple-glazed timber windows, Passive House principles
  • Standing-seam zinc roof, 52° north and 18° south
  • Nineteen months on site, 2022 to 2024
