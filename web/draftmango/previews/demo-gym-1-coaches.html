About Personal Training

The Standard

Nobody coaches here on day one.

  1. 01

    Certified

    CSCS or NASM before the first interview, plus CPR and first aid renewed every two years.

  2. 02

    Shadowed

    200 hours on the floor beside a lead coach before running a class of their own.

  3. 03

    Still Learning

    Two courses a year, paid by the club, and a Monday meeting to review every block.
