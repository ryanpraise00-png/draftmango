Our Story

One truck, one roof in Tempe.

Chris Walker started Draftmango Solar in 2012 after six years as an electrician, tired of finishing other companies’ rushed installs. The first system went on a neighbor’s ranch house in Kyrene; it still makes more than its design promised.

We grew by the same rule: the people who design your system are the people who install it, and the same office answers the phone when you call in year twelve. Batteries came in 2018, business rooftops and carports in 2014.
