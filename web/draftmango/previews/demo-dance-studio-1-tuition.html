What a season costs.

Tuition follows the hours a dancer takes each week, billed monthly in ten installments from September to June. No surprises, and the first class is free.

Priced by the hour.

Add up the classes a dancer takes each week; that sets the monthly tuition, whatever the mix of styles.

  • Ten installments, September to June, billed on the 1st
  • Winter break is already spread over the year
  • Change the mix at the end of any month

Studio Policies

The small print.

Give us 30 days' notice in writing, by email or at the front desk. Tuition stops after the following installment; the registration fee isn't refunded.
Tuition isn't reduced for missed classes, but any class can be made up within the same term, in a class of the same level. The front desk books it for you.
There are no classes from December 21 to January 3. The ten installments already account for the break, so every month costs the same.
Each routine has one costume, $65, billed in February. It's the dancer's to keep after the show.
A 10-class card is valid for six months from its first class, for any adult open class. Cards are personal and can't be shared.
