Topic 01

Working With Us

How we take on work and who leads it.

Executive teams and boards of mid-sized and large organisations, most often through the CEO, CFO or COO.
Yes. One of four partners leads every engagement from the first call to the handover, and sits in every steering meeting.
Most of our clients are elsewhere. We travel to you, and the team is on site whenever the work needs it.
Many clients do. A two- to four-week diagnostic is a common first step, at a fixed fee.
We take work where we can name the measures we expect to move. If we can't, we say so on the first call.

Topic 02

Fees & Contracts

What it costs, and what you sign.

Most work is a fixed fee per phase, agreed before it starts. You know the full cost of a phase before you commit to it.
For some programmes, yes: part of the fee depends on the measures in the signed baseline. We only propose it where those measures are within our reach.
Yes. The first call and any follow-up before a proposal cost nothing and commit you to nothing.
Yes, at the end of any phase, with two weeks' notice and no penalty. You pay for the work done.
Either. Ours fit on six pages, and we are happy to work under your framework agreement instead.

Topic 03

Engagements

How the work runs, and how it ends.

Usually within three weeks of a signed proposal, and sooner for a short diagnostic.
Small: a partner, a principal and two to four consultants, working beside your people rather than in a separate room.
Most run twelve to twenty weeks. We plan every engagement to hand over, not to extend.
Every programme starts with a baseline your board signs in week one. We report against it monthly, and again a year after we leave.
Your team runs what we built together. We check the numbers twelve months on, at no charge.

Topic 04

Data & Confidentiality

Where your information goes, and who sees it.

Gladly. Send it with your request, or ask for ours; both are fine.
In your systems wherever possible. Anything we hold sits in ISO 27001 certified storage in your region and is deleted at handover.
No. Client data is never used to train models, ours or anyone else's.
Only with your written permission. Most of our case studies are published without the client's name.
We may serve others in your sector, but never on the same question at the same time, and never with the same team.
