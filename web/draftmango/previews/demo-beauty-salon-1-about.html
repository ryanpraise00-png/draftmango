Our Story

A salon built around the mirror.

Ava Monroe opened Draftmango Salon in 2016 with two chairs, one color bowl and a rule: nobody picks up scissors or a brush until the client has finished talking. The rule stayed as the salon grew to nine chairs, a color bar, a four-station nail bar and a quiet lash room at the back.

We print every price on the menu, train every new artist ourselves and check every color by the window before you leave. It is not complicated. It is just rarely done.

Ava Monroe, founder
