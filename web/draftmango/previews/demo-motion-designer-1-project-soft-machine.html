01The Brief

Show the foam, not the shoe.

Mondra had a new midsole foam and a problem every running brand knows: on a phone screen, every shoe looks the same. They had seen my Squeeze Test, a Friday experiment from the Playground, and asked whether that feeling could sell a midsole.

We left the shoe out altogether. Three silicone cushions, one for each density in the foam, pressed between two thin chrome bars and let go again: twelve seconds that loop so cleanly nobody sees where they start. The sound is a single soft exhale, timed to the press.

  • ChallengeA material, not a product, in a feed where every shoe looks alike.
  • AnswerThree cushions, two bars and one press, looped without a seam.
  • ResultOn 180 store screens in 14 countries; gold for Best Loop at Loopwerk.
