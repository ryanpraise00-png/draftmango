A low island of sand, sea grape and limestone.

Pelican Cay is 1.2 miles from end to end, ringed by a reef on the Atlantic side and a calm lagoon on the other. Paths of raked sand join everything; most guests leave their shoes in the room.

The Map

Eight places, none more than twenty minutes on foot.

Twelve villas along the west beach, each with its own pool and a path through the sea grape to the sand.

The island's highest point, 40 feet up. A telescope, blankets and a guide on clear nights from 9 pm.

Eight treatment pavilions in the scrub, open to the breeze, each with an outdoor shower.

The beach grill, feet in the sand: fish off the morning boats, cooked over driftwood from noon.

Where the boat from Providenciales comes in, and where the sailing and snorkel trips leave from.

Ages 4 to 12, 9 am to 5 pm: rock-pool walks, sandcastle crews and a shaded lagoon beach of their own.

Adults only, on the quiet north side: an infinity edge over the reef and a bar that brings the drinks to you.

A spit of sand that appears at low tide. We set up the picnic; you walk out with the water at your ankles.

Care of the Island

We only have the one reef.

  • A coral nursery

    1,200 fragments growing on frames off the north shore, replanted on the reef each spring.

  • Power from the sun

    A solar field behind the dunes gives the island 60% of what it uses.

  • Our own water

    Drawn from the sea and made fresh on the island, in glass bottles in every room.

  • Reef-safe, plastic-free

    Mineral sunscreen in every room and no single-use plastic anywhere on the cay.

Through the Year

Warm water in every month.

December to April

Dry and breezy

Air 75–84 °F, water 78 °F. The sandbar shows at every low tide.

May and June

Still and clear

Air 80–88 °F, water 81 °F. The best weeks for the reef.

July and August

Long, hot days

Air 84–90 °F, water 84 °F. Family Summer, and an afternoon shower now and then.

September to November

The quiet season

Hurricane season on paper, our lowest rates, and the island to yourselves.
