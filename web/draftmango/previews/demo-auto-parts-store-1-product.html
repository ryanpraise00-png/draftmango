In the Kit

Three parts, matched to each other

The pads are cut for these rotors and the clips for your caliper bracket, so nothing in the kit needs filing, bending or a second trip.

  • 1

    Drilled & slotted rotors, a pair

    Cross-drilled and slotted to shed heat and gas on long descents; the zinc-coated hat and vanes stay rust-free through salty winters.

  • 2

    Ceramic pads, one axle

    A low-dust ceramic compound with chamfers, slots and rubber-steel shims already fitted, so the first stops are quiet.

  • 3

    Clips, pins and boots

    New abutment clips, slide pins and boots with high-temperature grease, the parts most people forget to replace.

Built for Front Range driving

Denver brakes work hard: I-70 descents, stop-and-go on Colfax, slush and mag chloride all winter. Stoppa drills and slots the rotors to run cooler under load, coats them against corrosion and pairs them with a ceramic pad that stays quiet and keeps the wheels clean.

Every piece carries its own warranty and can be returned on its own, unopened, within 90 days.
