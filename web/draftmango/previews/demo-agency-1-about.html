Why Draftmango exists

We started Draftmango round a kitchen table in Lisbon with one rule: the people who win the work are the people who make it.

Fourteen years on, the rule still holds. No account layer, no juniors behind the curtain, just the people you met in the first workshop.

Four(04) What we hold to promises

Not posters for the wall. The habits every project is run by, from the first call to the handover.

  1. 01

    Senior by default

    Everyone who pitches the work also makes it, every week until launch.

  2. 02

    One team, no hand-offs

    Strategy, design and code share one table, so nothing gets lost between them.

  3. 03

    Show, don’t deck

    We bring prototypes to meetings, not forty slides about them.

  4. 04

    Leave it better

    Every system we hand over is documented, tested and easy to grow.
