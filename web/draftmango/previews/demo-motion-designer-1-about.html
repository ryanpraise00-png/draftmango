03About

Hi, I'm Kaja. I make objects that don't exist feel like you could hold them.

An independent 3D and motion designer in Kreuzberg, Berlin. Product designer by training, animator by trade, and the only person who touches every frame of the work you see here.

02The Story

From Weimar to Kreuzberg, in eight chapters.

2013

Product design in Weimar

Four years of drawing objects, and rendering the ones nobody would ever make.

2015

Halbton Studio, Berlin

Junior 3D artist on product animation: kettles, chairs and a great many phones.

2018

Rauschen Motion

Look-development lead on launch films for consumer tech and fashion houses.

Ines Seidel

Edit & Colour · Berlin

05How I Work

Four promises, kept on every project.

One person on every frame

You brief me and you hear from me. No account managers, no hand-offs to juniors.

The object first

Materials and light are agreed before anything moves, so the film is built on solid ground.

Notes stay small

Rough cuts twice a week mean you are never surprised, and nothing needs undoing.

Files you can keep

Scene files and asset kits are yours on request, ready for your team or the next studio.

On the desk

  • A node-based 3D suite
  • A GPU path-tracing renderer
  • Fluid, cloth and soft-body simulation
  • A node compositor
  • An edit and colour suite
  • A pencil and a sketchbook
