First-half 2026 results: revenue up 6.2%.

Amsterdam, 30 July 2026. Revenue of €4.36bn, a stronger margin and the full-year outlook confirmed.

Highlights

  • Revenue of €4.36bn, up 6.2%; organic growth of 4.8%
  • EBITDA of €689m, up 8.7%; the margin up to 15.8%
  • Draftmango Energy's Noordzee Hybrid delivers first power, on time and on budget
  • Draftmango Health agrees to acquire Lumeda Diagnostics
  • Net debt / EBITDA down to 1.4x; full-year outlook confirmed

Draftmango Group, the Amsterdam-based holding company, today reports its results for the six months to 30 June 2026. Five of the six businesses grew their revenue and their margin; Draftmango Mobility held both steady through a year of fleet renewal.

Growth was led by Draftmango Energy, where the first turbines of the 420 MW Noordzee Hybrid reached the grid in March, and by Draftmango Health, whose diagnostics range grew by double digits in Germany and the Nordics. Draftmango Properties completed two office refurbishments in Amsterdam and Hamburg, both certified for low energy use.

Outlook

We confirm our outlook for 2026: revenue growth of 4 to 6%, an EBITDA margin of around 15.5% and net debt / EBITDA below 1.5x at the year end. The acquisition of Lumeda Diagnostics is expected to complete in the fourth quarter, subject to the usual approvals.

The Q3 Trading Update follows on 5 November 2026.

About Draftmango Group

Draftmango Group is a family-founded holding company, established in Amsterdam in 1952 and listed on Euronext Amsterdam since 1998 (NXG). It owns six businesses for the long term — Draftmango Properties, Industrial, Mobility, Energy, Health and Foods — with 31,200 people in 42 countries and revenue of €8.4bn in 2025.
