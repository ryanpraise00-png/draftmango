B

  • Bakuchiol

    1%
    • Renew

    A plant extract that behaves like a gentle vitamin A: smoother texture and softer lines, without the dryness, morning or night.

    Pairs well with
    Ceramides, squalane
    Go easy with
    Retinal on the same night
    Find it in
    Bakuchiol Face Oil

  • Centella asiatica

    2%
    • Calm

    A soothing herb extract that takes the heat out of redness and helps a stressed barrier recover.

    Pairs well with
    Niacinamide, ceramides
    Go easy with
    Nothing in particular
    Find it in
    Cloud Water Cream

  • Ceramides

    3%
    • Hydrate

    The lipids that hold skin cells together like mortar; topped up, they stop water escaping and the tightness that follows.

    Pairs well with
    Everything, retinal especially
    Go easy with
    Nothing in particular
    Find it in
    Ceramide Milk Toner Barrier Night Cream

  • Colloidal oat

    3%
    • Calm

    Finely milled oat that settles itchy, dry skin and leaves a soft film behind, the classic winter rescue.

    Pairs well with
    Shea, ceramides
    Go easy with
    Nothing in particular
    Find it in
    Oat Body Butter

F

  • Ferulic acid

    0.5%
    • Protect

    A plant antioxidant that keeps vitamin C and E stable and doubles their defence against daylight.

    Pairs well with
    Vitamin C, vitamin E
    Go easy with
    Nothing in particular
    Find it in
    C15 Bright Serum

G

  • Glycerin

    5%
    • Hydrate

    The quiet workhorse: draws water into the top layers of skin and keeps it there. In almost every good moisturiser.

    Pairs well with
    Everything
    Go easy with
    Nothing in particular
    Find it in
    Cloud Water Cream Soft Gel Cleanser

H

  • Hyaluronic acid

    1%
    • Hydrate

    Holds many times its weight in water; in two molecule sizes it plumps the surface and the layers just under it.

    Pairs well with
    Glycerin, ceramides
    Go easy with
    Very dry air without a cream on top
    Find it in
    Cloud Water Cream

L

  • Lactic acid

    5%
    • Brighten

    The gentlest of the exfoliating acids: loosens dull surface cells and hydrates as it works.

    Pairs well with
    Ceramides, SPF the next day
    Go easy with
    Retinal on the same night
    Find it in
    Salt Body Polish

    Vitamin C, retinal, zinc
    Go easy with
    Nothing in particular
    Find it in
    N10 Niacinamide Serum

    5%
    • Calm

    Ceramides, retinal
    Go easy with
    Nothing in particular
    Find it in
    Barrier Night Cream

  • Retinal

    0.1%
    • Renew

    Ceramides, niacinamide
    Go easy with
    Acids on the same night; start two nights a week
    Find it in
    Retinal Night Serum

  • Salicylic acid

    0.5%
    • Clarify

    Niacinamide
    Go easy with
    Other acids in the same routine
    Find it in
    Soft Gel Cleanser

    Pure
    • Hydrate

    Retinal, vitamin C
    Go easy with
    Nothing in particular
    Find it in
    Melt Cleansing Balm Retinal Night Serum
