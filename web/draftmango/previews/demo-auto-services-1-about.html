About Us

Owner-run on Larimer Street since 2011.

Three bays and one promise in 2011: tell people the truth about their car. Fifteen years later, eight bays and the same promise.

Our Story

A dealer technician who wanted to show people the parts.

Wes Kendrick spent four years in dealer workshops, watching customers approve work they could not see. In 2011 he rented three bays on Larimer Street and started texting photos with every quote.

It caught on. Today twelve technicians work eight bays and a dedicated EV bay, and every quote still comes with the pictures.
