Featured Project · Westshore Library

The whale wall, from a pencil line to fourteen metres.

Thirty children drew the sea they wanted in their reading room. I turned their whales, ladders and fish into one long picture, then painted it over nine days.

Read the Whole Story

  1. 01Drawn with the kids

    Evenings and Sundays, so the room stayed open every day of the job.

  3. 03Sealed to last

How Commissions Work

No surprises, just pictures.

Four steps, one price agreed up front, and a sketch you can hold before a drop of colour goes down.

  1. 1

    Brief & quote

    You tell me the story, the size and the date. I reply within two working days with ideas and a fixed price.

    2 days

  2. 2

    Three rough sketches

    Pencil roughs of three different ideas. You pick one, we sharpen it together, changes included.

    1 week

  3. 3

    Colour & texture

    The chosen sketch in full colour and grain, with one round of colour changes before it’s final.

    1–2 weeks

  4. 4

    Files, or the wall

    Print-ready files and layered originals, or, for a mural, me on the scaffold with a van of paint.

    On the date

Hello

I’m Tilda. I draw at a big table above a bike shop.

I grew up on the Clare coast, studied illustration in Dublin and spent four years drawing for a children’s publisher in London before coming home to the west. The studio is one bright room on Raven Lane, Galway, with a plan chest, too many plants and a window on the market.

I like jobs with a real audience: a child choosing a book, someone reaching for a tin, a family walking past a wall every morning.
