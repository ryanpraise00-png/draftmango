How We Work

Four steps. No surprises.

The same method on every engagement, scaled to the problem. You see the plan, the team and the fee before we start.

01 / 04

3 weeks

Diagnose

Interviews, site visits and your numbers, read by senior people. You get the three moves worth making first.

  • Baseline of margin, cash and capacity
  • Thirty interviews across the business
  • A ranked list of opportunities, sized

02 / 04

2–4 weeks

Design

We shape the plan with the managers who will run it, so the first week of delivery is not a sales job.

  • Target operating model and owners
  • Business case your board can sign
  • A 100-day plan with weekly milestones

03 / 04

8–16 weeks

Deliver

Our people work beside yours on the floor, in the close and in the weekly review, until the change holds.

  • A partner in your weekly review
  • Pilots first, then the rollout
  • Savings tracked against the case

Ongoing

Sustain

A light retainer keeps a partner on call and the numbers in front of you, long after the team has gone.

  • A check-in at six and twelve months
