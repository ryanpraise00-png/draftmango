Five steps to your own front door.

  1. Get your numbers

    A twenty-minute call: income, savings, outgoings and the areas you love.

  2. Decision in Principle

    A lender’s written yes in principle, so estate agents take your offer seriously.

  3. Offer accepted

    We apply the same day and order the valuation with the lender.

  4. Mortgage offer

    Usually two to four weeks. We chase, you pick a removal van.

  5. Exchange and completion

    Your solicitor sets the date. The keys are yours that afternoon.

Representative example

A mortgage of £200,000 over 25 years, initially on a fixed rate of 3.89% for 5 years and then the lender’s variable rate of 7.24% for the remaining 20 years, would require 60 monthly payments of £1,044.31 followed by 240 monthly payments of £1,393.66. The total amount payable would be £399,746 made up of the loan amount plus interest of £198,747 and a product fee of £999. The overall cost for comparison is 6.1% APRC representative.
