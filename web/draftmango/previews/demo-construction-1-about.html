Since 1987

Four carpenters and a pickup. Thirty-nine years later.

Draftmango Builders is a commercial general contractor and construction manager owned by the people who run it, with 260 employees in Charlotte, Raleigh and Greenville.

Our Story

We still price jobs like we have to build them.

My father, Walt Hayes, started Draftmango in 1987 with four carpenters, a pickup and a rule: never give an owner a number you would not sign yourself. Our first jobs were church additions and dentist offices around Charlotte.

I joined as a carpenter in 2006 and have run the company since 2019. We have grown into hospitals, schools and million-square-foot warehouses, but our estimators still walk sites with superintendents, and we still pour our own concrete.

— Robert Hayes, President
