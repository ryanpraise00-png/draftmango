    Our Story

    From the queue, for the queue.

    Our founders ran support teams at a delivery start-up. Every night the queue grew, and every morning it was the same twenty questions.

    So they built something that could answer those questions properly, and pass on everything else with care. Five years later, it answers for 3,400 teams.

    1. 2021Founded in San FranciscoThree support leads and an engineer, one shared desk.
    2. 2022The first 100 teamsChat and email, answering from help centers.
    3. 2024Actions and voiceRefunds, bookings and a phone line that picks up.
    4. 2025London opensEU hosting and 45 languages.
    5. 20263,400 teams68% of conversations resolved end to end.

    What we hold to.

    Four rules for the product, and for how we work with each other.

    • Nobody Waits

      An answer in seconds, or a person who already knows the story.

    • Say “I Don’t Know”

      A guess costs more trust than an honest handoff.

    • People Come First

      The agent takes the routine so your team can take the human.

    • Your Data Stays Yours

      Never used to train shared models. Never sold.
