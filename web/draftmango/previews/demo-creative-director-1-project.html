Solenne Optics · Campaign & Film · 2026

Hard Light

A summer eyewear campaign shot the way the sun actually hits sunglasses: at noon, on walls the colour of the frames.

Client
Solenne Optics, Lyon
My role
Creative direction, photography direction, film direction
Deliverables
12 stills, a 30″ film, 6″ cutdowns, out-of-home
Shot in
Mallorca, four days in June
Live
Europe and North America, spring 2026

The Brief

Sell a summer range without a beach.

Solenne had spent three summers on the same picture: a model on a lounger, sea behind, frames in the middle of the frame. Sales were flat, and the new range had warmer acetates than anything they had made before.

The brief asked for “more energy”. What the range needed was a reason to exist. Tortoiseshell, amber and burnt orange are the colours of a wall at noon, so we went looking for walls.

We stopped photographing sunglasses and started photographing the light they were made for.

The idea

Every frame was shot between 11:30 and 14:00, when the shadows are shortest and hardest. Each model was matched to a wall the colour of their frames, and the styling stayed in cream and sand so the acetate carried the colour.

The film follows one woman along a single orange wall for thirty seconds, her shadow walking with her. No cut, no music drop, one line of type at the end.

The Film

Thirty seconds, one wall, no cut.
