Who Writes This

Two of us, one very long road.

We’re Linnea and Jasper. She writes, he takes the photos, and since 2018 we’ve traded a flat in Portland, Maine for a van, a lot of ferries and the occasional sleeper train. Every route here is one we walked, drove or rode ourselves, with real costs and the mistakes left in.
