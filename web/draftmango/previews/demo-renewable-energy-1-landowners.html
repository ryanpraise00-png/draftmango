How It Works

From a handshake to harvest.

  1. 01A visit at your kitchen tableNo paperwork. We bring the maps; you tell us how you farm the ground.
  2. 02An option agreementIt pays from the day you sign and lets us study the site for three to five years.
  3. 03StudiesWind or sun, wildlife, soils, drainage and the grid — two to three years.
  4. 04A layout you sign offEvery pad, road and cable route drawn with you, on your field map.
  5. 05The buildTwelve to eighteen months, timed around planting and harvest; crop loss paid at market price.
  6. 06Thirty years of paymentsOne check every March; a land agent you can call by name.
  7. 07Your land, restoredPaid for by a decommissioning bond we post before construction starts.
