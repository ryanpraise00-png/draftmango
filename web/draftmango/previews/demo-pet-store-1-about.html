Our Story · Since 2014

A corner pet shop that grew up with its regulars.

Kate and Ben Garrow opened Draftmango Pets on Holloway Ave in 2014 and drove the first deliveries over themselves. Thirteen years on, the idea is the same for 48,000 pets: the right food, there before the bowl runs out.

What We Know

Food that fits the pet, not the shelf.

A senior lab needs fewer calories and more joint support than a puppy of the same breed. A house cat drinks more from moving water. A rabbit eats a pile of hay the size of its body every day. We stock by life stage, size and need, our vets read every label before it reaches a shelf, and Autoship times each delivery to the size of the bag.

How It Started

A vet nurse, a feed-store kid and a hatchback.

Kate spent eight years as a vet nurse in Nashville, answering the same three questions at the front desk: which food, how much, and why does he scratch. Ben grew up behind the counter of his parents' feed store in Lebanon, Tennessee, lifting forty-pound bags before school.

In 2014 they took a narrow shop on Holloway Ave, filled it with food they would give their own dog and wrote each regular's bag size in a notebook by the till. When a bag was about to run out, Ben drove a new one over after closing, with Hank, a terrier mix from the county shelter, riding along.

That notebook became Autoship. The shop is still on Holloway Ave, and Hank still inspects every delivery that comes through the back door.

Thirteen Years

From one notebook to five kinds of pets.

  1. 2014

    A narrow shop on Holloway Ave

    Forty kinds of food, a treat bar by the scoop and a water bowl by the door. Deliveries went out in a hatchback after six.

  2. 2016

    The notebook becomes Autoship

    Regulars asked to be reminded before the bag ran out. We made it automatic, took 5% off and kept the reminder, three days before it ships.

  3. 2018

    A grooming salon behind glass

    Theo Brannigan gave the first bath in the room at the back. Wren Hollins joined in 2021 for the cats and rabbits.

  4. 2019

    Trailmix, our first own bag

    Deboned chicken first, cooked in small batches by a family kitchen in Kentucky. Six more house brands followed, one kind of pet at a time.

  5. 2021

    The Lebanon warehouse

    Forty minutes east, in Ben's hometown: next-day delivery across the Southeast, and orders ready at the shop in two hours.

  6. 2023

    Vet Chat, with Dr. Nora Kimball

    Licensed vets by chat or video, 8 AM to 10 PM CT, seven days, free for every Autoship member.

  7. Today

    Dogs, cats, small pets, fish and birds

    48,000 pets on Autoship and a team of 140 between the shop, the salon, the warehouse and Vet Chat. The water bowl is still by the door.
