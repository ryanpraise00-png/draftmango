About the Collective

Forty designers, bought in person, worn by us first.

Draftmango Collective is a designer boutique for women and men on Greenwich Street and East Oak Street. We look for independent labels, see every collection on the rail, and carry only the pieces we would wear ourselves.

What we do

A good designer piece is made twice: once by the designer, and again by whoever chose it for you. We take the second part seriously. Four buying trips a season, every sample worn for a week, and a fit note on everything we sell.

Chapter One

A buyer, an art director and nine labels on two rails

Juliette Kroon spent ten years buying womenswear in Paris and New York. Pascal Oberlin art-directed a fashion quarterly. They met on a shoot in 2012, and kept arguing about the same thing: why the best small labels never made it onto a New York rail.

In October 2014 they opened in a former print works on Greenwich Street with nine designers and two rails. Pascal folded the first Issue by hand in the winter of 2016, a letter of new pieces mailed to a few hundred clients. We have printed one every season since.

How We Buy

Fewer pieces, every size, all of them tried

  1. 01

    See it in the showroom

    Four buying trips a season, to Paris, Milan, Copenhagen, London and New York. Nothing is ordered from a lookbook.

  2. 02

    Wear it for a week

    Every sample goes home with someone on the team. If it pulls, pills or bores us by Friday, it stays off the rail.

  3. 03

    Buy deep, not wide

    Fewer styles, every size from XS to XL, so the coat you want is there in yours.

  4. 04

    Write it down

    A fit note on every piece and a page for every label in the Issue, so you know who made it and how it wears.
