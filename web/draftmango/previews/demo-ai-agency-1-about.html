Principles

Four rules we do not bend.

They are in every contract we sign, in these words.

  1. Measure First

    We agree the number before we build. If it does not move, we say so and stop.

  2. A Person Decides

    Agents draft, check and route. Anything that costs money or trust waits for a human.

  3. Built in the Open

    You see the prompts, the tests and the logs. Nothing runs that you cannot inspect.

  4. Leave It Better Documented

    Every handover comes with a runbook, so your team can run the agent without us.
