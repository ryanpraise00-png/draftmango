Care 20 minutes, once a year Oct 4, 2027

Caring for solid oak: oil once a year, and that’s all.

Our oak leaves Denver with a hard-wax oil finish that feeds the wood instead of sealing it. It asks for very little: a damp cloth on the day, and twenty minutes with a jar of oil each autumn.

Signe RaabyQuality Bench Lead · 3 min read
  1. 01Wipe, don’t soak
  2. 02Lift a ring with heat
  3. 03Oil it each autumn
  4. 04Mind the sun and the radiator
01 · Every day 02 · Rings 03 · Oiling 04 · Placement Checklist

Solid oak is forgiving. It dents and scratches like any real wood, and that is part of why people keep it for decades: a scratch in an oiled top can be rubbed out, a lacquered one has to be stripped. Here is everything our bench does to the floor samples at the studio, and all you will ever need to do at home.

Step 01Wipe, don’t soak

A cloth wrung out in warm water with a drop of mild soap is all a table needs day to day. Wipe along the grain, then dry it with a second cloth. Skip sprays with silicone or ammonia; they build a film that the oil can’t get through later.

From the benchLinen napkins and stoneware are kind to oak. Hot pans and wet glass are not: use a board and coasters.

Step 02Lift a ring with heat

A pale ring from a wet glass is moisture trapped in the finish, not damage. Lay a cotton tea towel over it and press a warm iron, no steam, for ten seconds at a time. The ring fades as the water lifts. A dark ring has reached the wood: a light sand and a coat of oil sorts it out.

  • Work with the iron moving, never resting.
  • Check after every ten seconds; stop as soon as it fades.
  • Finish with a thin coat of oil on that spot.

Step 03Oil it each autumn

Once a year, when the heating comes on and the air dries out, give every top a coat of hard-wax oil. Clean it, let it dry, rub a thin layer in with a lint-free cloth along the grain, wait fifteen minutes and buff away everything the wood hasn’t taken. Leave it overnight before you put anything back.

Less is moreA teaspoon of oil covers a coffee table. Too much stays tacky; buff until the cloth slides.

Free with every oak piece. A 100 ml jar of our hard-wax oil and a lint-free cloth come in the box. Run out? The studio refills it for free.

Step 04Mind the sun and the radiator

Light oak deepens to honey in strong sun over a year or two; move lamps, vases and placemats now and then so it colours evenly. Keep pieces a hand’s width from radiators and vents. Denver air is dry, and oak likes to stay between 40 and 60% humidity.
