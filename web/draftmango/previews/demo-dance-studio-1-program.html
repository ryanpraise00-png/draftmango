The Program

Real technique, big fun.

Between seven and eleven, children can learn properly: posture, turnout, rhythm, how to listen to music and to a teacher. We keep the joy and add the craft.

Every dancer takes Ballet, the base of everything else. On top, they choose Jazz, Hip Hop or Tap, one class or two. Classes stay small, so the teacher sees every child every week.

  • Posture, alignment and turnout
  • Musicality and counting
  • First turns, leaps and rhythms
  • Performing on a real stage

Four Disciplines

Ballet first, then play.

What each class works on, and what to put in the dance bag.

  • Ballet

    Barre, centre and the first jumps; the base of every other style.

    Black leotard, pink tights and slippers, hair in a bun

  • Jazz

    Kicks, leaps and turns to music they already love.

    Fitted top, black jazz pants, tan jazz shoes

  • Hip Hop

    Grooves, footwork and a crew routine of their own.

    Comfortable clothes, clean sneakers for the studio

  • Tap

    Rhythm, timing and the sound of a whole class in step.

    Black tap Oxfords, any dancewear
