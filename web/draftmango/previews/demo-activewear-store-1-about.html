Since 2016 · Mission Bay, San Diego

Kit that passes the squat, the long run and the wash

Draftmango Active is a training, running and yoga store on Harbor Lane, run by a coach and a fabric developer who got tired of leggings that went see-through.

What We Do

We sell fewer pieces, and we wear every one of them first.

Leggings, bras, shorts and layers for training, running, yoga and the studio: our own line, cut in our own CoreKnit, beside three brands we run, lift and stretch in ourselves. Every style is worn for thirty sessions before it reaches a rail, and every pair of leggings is squat-checked before it ships.

How It Started

A coach, a fabric nerd and forty pairs of leggings

Claire Blaine coached college track for eight years and spent most of them apologizing for team kit: shorts that rode up, tights that went sheer in a lunge. Owen Bryant had spent ten years developing performance knits for other labels and knew exactly why.

In 2016 they rented 600 square feet at the far end of Harbor Lane, hung forty pairs of leggings and a coffee machine, and promised one thing: if it fails in a session, bring it back. A year later Claire started a Saturday run from the door. Six people came.

Claire Blaine & Owen BryantCo-founders · Claire runs the store, Owen runs product and fabric

How a Piece Gets Made

Thirty sessions before it ships

Nothing reaches a rail until the club has sweated in it. Most samples change three or four times on the way.

  1. 01

    Sketch with the club

    Every new piece starts with what runners and lifters ask for at the fit desk.

  2. 02

    Sample in CoreKnit

    Owen cuts the first pairs in our own fabric, then the pattern changes, a lot.

  3. 03

    Thirty sessions

    Theo and forty club members train, run, wash and repeat. They report everything.

  4. 04

    The squat check

    Every pair of leggings, at full depth, under bright studio light. Every one.

  5. 05

    Mended for a year

    A seam that gives in the first year is mended or replaced, free.
