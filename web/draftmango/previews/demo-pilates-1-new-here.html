The Machine

The reformer, in six parts.

It looks like a bed with ropes. It's a moving floor with adjustable resistance. Tap a part.

  1. FootbarWhere your feet go for footwork, and your hands for planks. It sits at three heights.
  2. SpringsFive of them, heavy to light. More springs is not always harder — it depends on the exercise.
  3. CarriageThe padded platform that slides on the rails. The springs pull it home.
  4. Shoulder BlocksThey keep you from sliding off the end when you press away.
  5. HeadrestUp for most of us, flat for some exercises. Your instructor will say.
  6. StrapsLoops on ropes for your hands or feet, running over the pulleys at the back.

What to bring.

  • Grip Socks

    Required on every apparatus. No socks? $14 at the desk.

  • Fitted Clothes

    Loose tops ride up when you're upside down, and zips catch the upholstery.

  • Water

    We have a filtered tap and towels. Bring a bottle or borrow a glass.

  • A Heads-Up

    Injuries, surgeries, a pregnancy — tell us in the form below or at the desk.
