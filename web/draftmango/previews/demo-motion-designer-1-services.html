02Services

Four ways to work together, one person on every frame.

Four to six projects a year, each one made from the first still to the final grade, with sound and edit by people I have worked with for years.

4–8 Weeks2 Rounds of NotesFixed Quotes

The four services

Typically 4–6 weeks

01

3D Product Film

Launch films that make a product feel heavy, soft or cold before anyone holds it. I build the product from your CAD files, or from a reference photo when the CAD is still changing, and light it like a real object on a real set.

  • A 0:30–0:45 film in 4K, graded
  • Three cut-downs for social and pre-roll
  • Six key stills at 6K for print
  • Sound design by Tomas Reiter

From$18,000

Recent Project · AureloHalo Drift
Typically 2–3 weeks

02

Brand Loops

Seamless loops for launches, screens in store and social. A good loop has no visible seam and no visible beginning: you watch it three times before you notice it repeats.

  • One 0:08–0:15 seamless loop
  • Cut for 16:9, 9:16 and 1:1
  • Two rounds of notes per stage
  • Files for screens in store on request

From$6,500

Recent Project · MondraSoft Machine
Typically 5–8 weeks

03

Motion Systems

The rules for how a brand moves: easing, timing, how objects enter and leave, and a kit of 3D assets your own team can reuse for years without calling me.

  • Easing and timing rules, written down
  • A 3D asset kit with lighting presets
  • Twenty-four templated assets
  • A handover session with your team

From$24,000

Recent Project · BrightvalePrism Index
By the day or the project

04

Art Direction

Look development and direction for agencies and in-house teams: materials, light and camera, set before production starts, so the people who animate it know exactly what good looks like.

  • Material and lighting look development
  • Style frames for pitches and sign-off
  • Shoot and render supervision
  • White label for agencies, if you prefer

Day rate$1,400

06Process

From brief to master in about five weeks.

No surprises at the end: every stage is signed off before the next one starts, and you see rough cuts twice a week.

07Questions

Before you write, the usual questions.

Something else? Write to me and you will have an answer within two working days.

About half of my work comes through agencies. I am happy to work under your name, join your calls and present with you, or stay behind the scenes and deliver style frames and renders to your team.

CAD files if you have them, a few photos if you do not, your launch date and where the film will run. A finished product is not needed: most launches I work on are filmed before the first unit ships.

Two rounds per stage: look dev, animation and the final grade. Because you see rough cuts twice a week, notes stay small and the third round is almost never needed.

You do: the licence is worldwide, in all media and without expiry. Scene files and the asset kit are handed over on request, so your team or another studio can pick the work up later.

Tomas Reiter designs the sound for every product film, and it is included in the price. For loops it is optional, since most of them play without sound.

Sometimes. There is a short waiting list for cancellations, and small loops can fit between larger projects. Send the brief anyway and I will tell you honestly what is possible.
