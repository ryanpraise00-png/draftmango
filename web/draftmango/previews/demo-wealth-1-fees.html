No surprises

What's included, and what isn't.

Always included

  • Your adviser's time, by phone, video or in person
  • A yearly review and an updated plan
  • Quarterly statements and an online account
  • Tax certificates at the end of each tax year
  • Dealing and rebalancing in your portfolio

Agreed separately

  • Trust and estate work with your solicitor
  • Specialist pension transfers
  • Business sale planning, quoted in advance
  • Underlying fund charges, set by each fund
