Building over a working river.

  1. 01

    The Challenge

    The Clyde stays open to shipping all year, the banks are soft alluvium over old dock walls, and the crossing had to open before the 2026 timetable change.

  2. 02

    Our Approach

    We floated the deck segments in from a yard downriver, founded each pylon on 64 bored piles, and ran design and construction in one model so site changes reached the drawings the same day.

  3. 03

    The Outcome

    Opened on programme in 38 months, carrying 42,000 crossings a day, with 1.9 million hours worked and no lost-time injury.
