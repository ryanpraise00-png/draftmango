Partner. Build. Hand on.

Three words we have used since 1995. This is what each of them means in practice, in every company we back.

Most of the value in a company is made by the people who run it. Our work is to choose them well, back them fully — and know when to step aside.

  1. I

    Partner

    We back management teams we'd happily work for.

    Before we invest, we spend months with a team — on site, at dinner, in their customers' offices. If we would not want to work for them, we do not buy.

    Founder reinvestment
    20–40%
    Equity held by management
    10–15%
    Board seats held by Draftmango
    2 of 5
  2. II

    Build

    Operating partners, not spreadsheets: hiring, pricing, M&A.

    Every company gets a plan written with its management in the first hundred days, and an operating partner who has done the work before.

    Plan agreed
    Within 100 days
    Senior hires made together
    140 in 2025
    Add-ons per company
    2.4 on average
  3. III

    Hand on

    We exit to owners who will keep building.

    We plan the sale from the first year, and choose buyers as carefully as we chose the company. Most want the team as much as the business.

    Average holding period
    6.2 years
    Exits since 1995
    38
    Gross realised multiple
    3.1x
