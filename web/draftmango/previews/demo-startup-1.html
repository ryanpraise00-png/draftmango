A Note From the Founders

We built the tool we kept wishing we had.

Lea spent six years as a PM keeping customer requests in a spreadsheet only she understood. Sam spent those years building features nobody could prove anyone wanted.

So in 2024 we started Draftmango in Austin, with one rule: every request gets counted, and every person who asked hears back. Fourteen of us now, still answering the support inbox ourselves.

Lea & Sam Co-founders, Draftmango Software · Austin, Texas
