Before You Brief

Good questions, short answers.

Most campaigns land between $80,000 and $400,000 in production, plus my fee. A stills-only art direction job can start at $35,000. Tell me the number early and I will say what it buys.

Six to ten weeks from brief to delivery: two for the idea, two to four for casting and pre-production, one for the shoot, the rest for edit, grade and retouch.

Often. About half my work comes through agencies, where I lead the campaign idea and the shoot while they run strategy, media and the rollout.
