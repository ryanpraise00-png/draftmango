04About Odile

A photographer from Marseille, mostly elsewhere

Fourteen years on assignment for magazines, hotels and travel brands, and a personal series every year in between.

I grew up above a bakery in Le Panier, the oldest quarter of Marseille, and learned to see in the hour before it opened: flour in the window light, the street still empty.

I bought my first camera with summer wages at sixteen and spent eight years assisting fashion and interiors photographers in Paris. I went freelance in 2013, with one magazine client and a rented darkroom.

Today I shoot around forty commissions a year: stories for magazines, hotels from nine rooms to ninety, and campaigns for the travel brands that send people to both. I work with one assistant, plan every day around the light and grade every frame myself.

Between commissions I make one personal series a year, usually on film, usually at night or at dawn. One of them, After the Rain, has become a book.
