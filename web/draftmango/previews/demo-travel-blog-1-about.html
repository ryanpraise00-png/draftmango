Since 2018 · Portland, Maine and the Road

Hello, we’re Linnea and Jasper.

A writer and a photographer who swapped a small flat for a smaller van, and have been writing down the routes ever since.

How It Started

One gap year that never ended.

In 2018 Linnea was editing a food magazine and Jasper was shooting weddings. We gave ourselves twelve months, one secondhand camper and a rule: write down every route properly, with the costs, so friends could copy it.

The friends became readers, the readers became 34,000 people on a Sunday newsletter, and the twelve months became eight years. We still pay for every trip ourselves, and we still argue about the playlist.

How We Work

Three promises we haven’t broken.

  • We pay our own way

    No free stays, no press trips. If a place is in a story, we paid the full price for it.

  • Real budgets

    Every itinerary ends with what we actually spent, in dollars, for two people.

  • Clear links

    Some links earn us a small commission. They’re marked, and they never change what we recommend.
