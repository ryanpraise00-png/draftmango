Not quite a standard case? Good.

A third of our clients fall outside a lender’s tick-box. These are the situations we sort every week.

  • Self-Employed

    One year of accounts, retained profits or day rates — matched to the lenders that accept them.

  • Family Help

    Gifted deposits, family guarantees and joint borrower, sole proprietor mortgages.

  • Shared Ownership

    Buy a share of a home, pay rent on the rest and staircase up when you’re ready.

  • New Build

    Offers that last long enough for the builder’s timetable, with incentives checked properly.

  • Moving Back to the UK

    Lenders who count income earned abroad and a short UK credit history.

  • Renovations

    Further borrowing for an extension, a loft or a new kitchen, set against your home’s new value.

Important information

Your home may be repossessed if you do not keep up repayments on your mortgage.

Think carefully before securing other debts against your home.

Draftmango Mortgages Ltd is authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FRN 000000). Most buy-to-let mortgages are not regulated by the FCA.

Representative example

A mortgage of £200,000 over 25 years, initially on a fixed rate of 3.89% for 5 years and then the lender’s variable rate of 7.24% for the remaining 20 years, would require 60 monthly payments of £1,044.31 followed by 240 monthly payments of £1,393.66. The total amount payable would be £399,746 made up of the loan amount plus interest of £198,747 and a product fee of £999. The overall cost for comparison is 6.1% APRC representative.
