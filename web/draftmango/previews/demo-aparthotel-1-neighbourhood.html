The Hosts’ Guide

How guests spend a week here.

Shop like you live here

  • The canal market, Tuesday and Friday mornings
  • Markthalle Neun for cheese, bread and Thursday street food
  • A late-opening supermarket two doors from the gate

Coffee and a laptop

  • Four cafés within five minutes that welcome laptops
  • Our own lounge, open 7 am to midnight
  • Day passes at a co-working space on the canal

Green, every morning

  • A 5 km loop along both banks of the canal
  • Görlitzer Park’s long lawn and its small farm
  • Tempelhofer Feld, twelve minutes by bike

Across the city

  • U1 and U8 from Kottbusser Tor, every five minutes
  • Bikes to borrow from the bike room, free
  • Night buses on Skalitzer Straße after the last train
