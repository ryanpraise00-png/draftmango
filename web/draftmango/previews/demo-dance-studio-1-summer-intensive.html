The Intensive

A summer that counts.

The rhythm of a company: class every morning, rehearsal every afternoon, and a new piece made with you in the third week. Dancers from other schools are welcome.

Taught by Elena Varga, Marcus Lee and Maya Lindgren, with two guest choreographers announced in the spring.

A Day

Nine to three.

Bring ballet and contemporary wear, pointe shoes if you're on pointe, lunch and a big bottle of water.

  1. 9:00

    Ballet Technique

    Barre and centre, by level.

  2. 10:45

    Pointe · Men's Class

    Strength, jumps and partnering basics.

  3. 11:30

    Contemporary

    Floor work, release, improvisation.

  4. 12:30

    Lunch

    In the lobby, or out on Fulton Market.

  5. 1:15

    Repertoire

    Classical variations and the week's new piece.

  6. 2:30

    Conditioning & Stretch

    Then pick-up at 3.

Three Weeks

Build, rehearse, perform.

  • 01

    Technique

    Clean up the basics with daily correction and a placement on day one.

  • 02

    Repertoire

    Classical variations and a contemporary piece, learned like a company.

  • 03

    Creation & Showing

    A new piece made with the guests, shown to families Friday, July 23 at 4 pm.
