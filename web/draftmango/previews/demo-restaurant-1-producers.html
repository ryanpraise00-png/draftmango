Pell Ridge Farm Sonoma, 48 miles north

The people behind the plates.

Farms, a mill, the day boats and a creamery, most within a hundred miles of Hayes Street. We pay them first, every Friday.

Every Week

Six we ring every Monday.

They tell us what is ready; the menu follows. Some have sold to us since the first autumn in 2016.

Producer of the Autumn Chapter

Ashland Orchard, Sebastopol.

Forty old quince trees on a hillside planted in the 1920s, picked by hand from the first week of October. They are hard as wood when they arrive; Clara poaches them for a day until they turn the colour of the claret.

How We Buy

Three promises we keep.

  1. I

    Whole harvests

    We take the misshapen and the oversized too, and write the menu around them.

  2. II

    Paid on Friday

    Every invoice, every week, before our own payroll.

  3. III

    Named on the menu

    Each course carries the name of the people who grew or caught it.
