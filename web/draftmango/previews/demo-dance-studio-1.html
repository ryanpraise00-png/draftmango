Six Disciplines

Find your style.

Every discipline is taught by level, from a first class to company-ready. Most dancers here study two.

  • 01 Ballet Technique, line and musicality. Ages 5+
  • 02 Contemporary Floor work, release and improvisation. Ages 9+
  • 03 Jazz Turns, leaps and sharp style. Ages 7+
  • 04 Hip Hop Grooves, freestyle and crew choreography. Ages 7+
  • 05 Tap Rhythm you can hear across the room. Ages 7+
  • 06 Musical Theatre Singing, acting and dance, together. Ages 9+
