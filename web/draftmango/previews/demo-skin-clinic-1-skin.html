After a Peel

Five days, day by day.

  1. 1

    Tight & Pink

    Like a mild sunburn. Moisturise, no actives.

  2. 2

    Darker

    Pigment rises to the surface. That is the peel working.

  3. 3

    Flaking

    Fine peeling around the mouth and nose. Never pick.

  4. 4

    Settling

    Most flaking is done. Make-up sits well again.

  5. 5

    Clearer

    Brighter, more even skin. SPF 50 every morning.
