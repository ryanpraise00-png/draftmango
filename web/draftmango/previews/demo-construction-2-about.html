How It Started

We still sign every contract at the kitchen table.

I started Draftmango with one truck, a framing crew of three and a borrowed nail gun. Our first job was a screened porch on Fair Street. The owners still call us when a gutter needs looking at.

What changed is the size of the jobs. What did not change is how we run them: our own people on site, one project manager who answers the phone, and an estimate that names every allowance.

We bought the old Columbia Avenue warehouse in 2009. The trucks, the lumber and the people who plan your job all work out of the same building.

What Has Not Changed

Three habits from the first truck.

  1. 01

    Our own crews

    Framing, finish carpentry, masonry, plumbing, electrical and paint are done by people on our payroll. We train them, we keep them.

  2. 02

    One name per job

    A project manager from the estimate to the walk-through, with a direct number. You never explain your house twice.

  3. 03

    Prices on paper

    Itemized estimates, named allowances and written change orders before any extra work starts.
