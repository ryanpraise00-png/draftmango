When Boards Call Us

The plan stopped explaining the numbers.

Usually after a new chief executive, a missed year, or a deal that changed the shape of the company. The questions are rarely new. The answer is overdue.

Fourteen Weeks

From diagnosis to a vote.

  1. Weeks 1–2

    Diagnose

    Interviews with the board and the top thirty leaders; the numbers nobody has joined up.

  2. Weeks 3–6

    Options

    Three to five real choices for the portfolio and the plan, each one priced.

  3. Weeks 7–10

    Decide

    A board paper and an off-site; one recommendation, argued in full.

  4. Weeks 11–14

    Commit

    A three-year plan with owners, a one-page scorecard and the first hundred days.
