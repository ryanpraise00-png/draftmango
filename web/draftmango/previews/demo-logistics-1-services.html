How a Shipment Moves

Six stops, one coordinator.

  1. Same day

    Booked

    Rate confirmed, space held on the next sailing or flight, documents checked.

  2. Day 1

    Picked Up

    Our driver collects, photographs the seal and scans every piece.

  3. Day 1–3

    Export Cleared

    Export filing done, cargo loaded and the departure confirmed to you.

  4. Live

    In Transit

    Live position, and any delay flagged to you within the hour.

  5. Before arrival

    Import Cleared

    Delivered

    Signed proof of delivery with a photo, in your inbox and the portal.
