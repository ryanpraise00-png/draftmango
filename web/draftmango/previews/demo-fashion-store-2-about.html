Draftmango Supply started at a Saturday market in South Austin in 2016. Today it’s four stores in Texas and 86 people who care, a little too much, about how a tee hangs and a jean sits.

How it started

We were tired of tees that twisted after three washes

Ellie and Dan met working the floor of an Austin denim shop, where the same question came up all day: why doesn’t anything just fit? In the spring of 2016 they had sixty heavyweight tees made in four colors, washed them twice so they wouldn’t shrink, and set up a folding table at a Saturday market in South Austin.

They sold out by noon. The next week they brought a hundred, and a notebook for people’s sizes. That notebook became the Lockhart jean a year later, and the habit stuck: we still ask everyone how things fit, and we still change the pattern when the answer is “almost.”

Ellie Nesbitt & Dan OrmsbyCo-founders, Austin

How a Draftmango Supply jean gets made

Four steps, and the last one happens in our stores, while you wait.

  1. 1Cut12 oz cotton denim in four cuts, waist 24 to 40, three lengths, so most people never need a tailor.
  2. 2WashEvery wash is washed and dried twice before it ships. The size on the label is the size after wash ten.
  3. 3FitEach size run is tried on 40 people in our Austin studio before we sign it off.
  4. 4HemFree in any store, chain-stitched like the original, so your jeans end where you do.

What we promise

Four things we won’t trade away, however big we get.

Fit first

Four cuts and three lengths for every jean, and a free swap if we still got it wrong.

Washed soft

Everything is washed before it reaches you. No break-in, no shrinking surprises.

Basics that come back

Core colors stay in stock all year, so the tee you love is there when it wears out.

Plain prices

The same price all year, two end-of-season sales, and never a made-up “was” price.
