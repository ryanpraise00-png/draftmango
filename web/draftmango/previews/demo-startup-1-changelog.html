Oct 9, 2026Feature

Dark mode

Every screen, every chart and the public portal too. Draftmango follows your system setting, or pick one in Preferences. Your portal can follow your visitors' setting or stay on your brand's light theme.

214214 voters notified

Oct 2, 2026Feature

Keyboard shortcuts

Triage the inbox without touching the mouse. Press ? anywhere to see them all.

  • J and K move through posts
  • M merges the post into another
  • S changes the status

117117 voters notified

Sep 30, 2026Improvement

Faster search across every board

Search now looks through post titles, comments and the quotes you captured from chat, and answers in under 200 ms on boards with 10,000 posts.

Sep 25, 2026Feature

Two-factor sign-in

Authenticator apps and passkeys, on every plan. Team admins can require it for everyone in the workspace.

231231 voters notified

Sep 22, 2026Fix

Small fixes

  • Merged posts no longer count a voter twice.
  • Portal links in release emails open the right board.
  • CSV import keeps line breaks inside quoted cells.

Sep 18, 2026Beta

Chat thread capture

React to any message with an emoji and the thread becomes a post, quote and author attached. In beta for Pro teams; tell us where it trips up.

Sep 11, 2026Improvement

Roadmap embeds

Drop your public roadmap into any page with one line of code. It inherits your fonts and follows the page's light or dark scheme.
