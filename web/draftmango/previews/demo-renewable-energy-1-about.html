Our Story

Six people, one wind mast, a rented room.

Draftmango Renewables started in 2008 above a hardware store on Washington Avenue, with a wind mast on a Nebraska ranch and a plan to own what we built instead of selling it on.

Kettle Creek came on line in 2011. Seventeen years later we run 38 plants in 11 states from a converted mill on the same river — and we still sign every lease at a kitchen table.

How We Work

Four things we don’t trade.

  • We stay

    We own what we build for its whole life. Nothing is built to flip.

  • Land first

    Drainage, fences and planting dates come before the turbine layout.

  • Plain numbers

    We publish output, payments and grants plant by plant, every spring.

  • Everyone home safe

    Any crew member can stop any job, and nobody asks why.
