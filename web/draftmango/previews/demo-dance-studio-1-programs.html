How Levels Work

Placed by how they dance, not just their age.

  1. Free Trial

    Any class, any discipline. Parents watch the last ten minutes.

  2. Placement

    Teachers set the level in the first two weeks of term.

  3. Reports

    A short written report in January and June, for every dancer.

  4. Move Up

    Most dancers go up a level each fall, and everyone performs in June.
