The Standard

What 450 hours buys you.

A weekend course teaches someone to lead a class. A comprehensive certification teaches them to see why your left hip lifts on the third rep — and what to change.

  • Mat and the small props60 hBall, ring, roller

  • Anatomy and special populations50 hBacks, pregnancy, older bodies
