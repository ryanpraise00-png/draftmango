About Milo

A designer who fell for the browser.

Creative developer in Rotterdam. Twelve years of front-end, six of them on my own.

The Short Version

I build the part of a site people remember.

I trained as a graphic designer in Eindhoven and wrote my first shader to animate a poster for a student party. It took a week. I was hooked.

Five years leading front-end at a Rotterdam studio taught me the other half: deadlines, budgets, old phones and clients who need to update the copy themselves.

Since 2021 I work on my own, with one project at a time and a Friday for the Lab.

The Long Version

From posters to pixels.

  1. 2021 – nowIndependent creative developerRotterdam. Launches for brands, the front-end half for design studios, a workshop every spring.
  2. 2016 – 2021Lead front-end developer, Studio HaverkampRotterdam. Grew the web team from two to seven and shipped the studio’s first WebGL work.
  3. 2015 – 2016Front-end developer, Oddly Good Co.Amsterdam. Campaign microsites, a lot of them, all on tight deadlines.
  4. 2011 – 2015BA Graphic DesignEindhoven. Graduated with an interactive poster wall that reacted to sound.

How I Work

Three promises on every project.

It works on the bus

Every scene is tested on a four-year-old phone on a slow connection before anyone calls it done.

Everyone can use it

Keyboard, screen readers and reduced motion are part of the first prototype, not a patch at the end.

You own all of it

The repository, the docs and a recorded walkthrough. Another developer can pick it up tomorrow.
