Our story

Two people, one desk and a bookshop

Web studio, New York, since 2012

Back in the spring of 2012, Nora Castellanos and Martin Keller shared one borrowed desk on Broadway. Nora drew the screens, Martin built them. Their first client was a bookshop in the West Village that needed a website before its tenth birthday party. It launched two days early, and the owner sent three friends their way the same week.

Fourteen years later, Draftmango is 38 people across two studios, and the habit from that first project still runs the place: the people who design a site sit next to the people who build it, from the first workshop to the day it goes live. No account layer, no hand-offs, no surprises in week nine.

In 2014 we packaged the system behind our own client sites and sold it as a template. It found more customers in a month than we had clients in a year. Today the studio and the template line feed each other: client work keeps the templates honest, and the templates give every client a head start.
