Inversions Without Fear.

Two and a half hours with Lucas on the shapes most of us avoid: headstand, forearm stand and handstand, all at the wall, all in small steps. Nobody is asked to go upside down before they want to.

  • How to come down kindly, and how to fall
  • Partner spotting, one pair at a time
