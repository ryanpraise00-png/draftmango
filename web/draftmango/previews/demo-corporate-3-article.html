What a five-day close actually takes.

It isn't heroics on day four. It's a calendar that starts on day one, a reviewer who isn't the preparer, and three decisions most companies never write down.

Hannah Pryor, CPAOctober 8, 2026 · Head of Finance Services

Most finance teams we take over close their books in two to three weeks. Within a quarter, most of them close in five business days. Nobody works harder; the work simply moves to the days it belongs to.

Here is what changes, in the order we change it.

1. The close starts on day one of the month

A slow close is usually a month of work done in a week. Bank and card feeds reconciled daily, bills coded the day they arrive, and invoices issued on schedule mean that by the last day of the month, 80% of the close is already done.

  • Reconcile bank and card accounts every day, not every month.
  • Code and approve bills within 48 hours of receipt.
  • Book payroll journals the day payroll runs.

2. The reviewer is never the preparer

A senior accountant prepares; a CPA reviews. The review takes a day, and it is the day that makes the numbers boring — the variances are explained before anyone outside finance sees them.

“The board doesn't need the numbers on day three. It needs numbers on day five that nobody will question on day six.”

3. Three decisions, written down

Every slow close we have inherited was waiting on a decision someone made in their head. We write three of them into the plan in the first week:

  1. The cut-off. Which invoices and bills belong to this month, and who decides.
  2. The materiality line. Below what amount an accrual is estimated, not chased.
  3. The pack. The three numbers the board reads first, on page one.
Behind on your books? Start with a catch-up before you shorten the close. Three months behind usually takes three weeks.

What it looks like after a quarter

At Fieldstone Labs, a 140-person biotech, the close went from 18 days to five, and the board pack now arrives before the CFO asks for it. The team didn't grow. The calendar did the work.
