How a Stay Works

No front desk queue. Hosts when you need them.

You let yourself in with a code, and Marlene and Theo answer messages from 7 am to 11 pm, in English or German. At night, the on-call number reaches a person in Berlin.

  1. Any time before

    Book online, no deposit

    Pick the layout and the dates. Stays of 28 nights or more pay month by month, not up front.

  2. 48 hours before

    Your door code arrives

    A personal code for the gate and the apartment, valid from 3 pm on arrival day. No queue at a desk.

  3. Every week

    Cleaned, fresh linen

    Housekeeping comes on the weekday you choose. Towels and bed linen are changed every visit.

  4. When plans change

    Extend with a message

    Staying on? Message the hosts and the dates move, at the rate of your new total length.

Questions

Before you book, the usual questions.

One night is fine. There is no upper limit: our longest guest stayed fourteen months. From 28 nights you pay month by month instead of up front.

Heating, electricity, water, 500 Mbit Wi-Fi, the weekly clean with fresh linen and towels, the laundry room and the lounge. The only extra is the city tax for stays under two months.

Yes, for stays of three months or more we give you the landlord confirmation (Wohnungsgeberbestätigung) the Bürgeramt asks for, and Theo can help you book the appointment.

Your door code arrives 48 hours before arrival and works from 3 pm. Check-out is by 11 am: just pull the door shut behind you. Need it later? Ask, it is often free.

Dogs and cats are welcome in the courtyard-side studios and one-bedrooms for $15 a night, capped at $150 a month. Please tell us when you book.

Stays under 28 nights can be cancelled free up to 7 days before arrival. On a monthly stay, give 14 days’ notice and you pay only to the new end date, at the rate that length earns.
