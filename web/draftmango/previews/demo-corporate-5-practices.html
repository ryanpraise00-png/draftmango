How a Mandate Runs

Four stages, and a vote at the end.

  1. Weeks 1–2

    Diagnose

    Interviews with the board and the top thirty leaders; the numbers nobody has joined up.

  2. Weeks 3–6

    Options

    Three to five real choices, each one priced and tested.

  3. Weeks 7–10

    Decide

    A board paper and an off-site; one recommendation.

  4. Weeks 11–14

    Commit

    The plan, its owners and the first hundred days.
