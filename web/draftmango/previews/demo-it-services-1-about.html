#1 · March 2009 A jammed printer

Still the people who pick up.

Our founder, Ryan Mercer, spent his twenties on hold to IT companies that never called back. So he started one that did. The rule from day one: a real engineer answers, and the first fix comes on the first call.

We've grown since then, but the rule hasn't changed. We only take clients within an hour's drive of an office, so someone can always be there the same day.

We don't sell hardware we wouldn't use, and we don't lock anyone into long contracts. Clients stay an average of nine years because it works.
