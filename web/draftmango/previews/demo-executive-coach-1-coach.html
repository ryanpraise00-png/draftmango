About Amelia

Amelia ran operations before she coached anyone. She knows what a Monday looks like when the plan meets the warehouse, and she coaches for that Monday.

Eleven years in consumer goods, the last four as Chief Operating Officer of a European homeware group: 2,400 people in six countries. She began coaching in 2008, co-founded Draftmango Leadership in 2012 and leads our faculty of sixty-four coaches.

Her clients are usually in their first eighteen months in a bigger role. She is warm, quick and very direct, and she will ask you what you would stop doing long before she asks what you will start.
