Other Ways

Three ways to know your size

  1. Check the settings

    Open Settings, then About: most devices list the screen size in inches right there.

  2. Measure once

    Height of a phone, the case of a watch, the diagonal of a tablet screen. The finder does the rest.

  3. Ask the fit desk

    Send a photo of the back of your device and we reply with the size, usually within the hour.
