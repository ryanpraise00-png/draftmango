About

320 architects, urbanists and engineers in New York, London and Singapore — one practice, one set of drawings.

Our Story

One drawing board, and a city to answer to.

People320

Draftmango Architects began in 1988 in a loft on Hudson Street: three architects, one drawing board and a commission for a branch library nobody else wanted. The library is still open, and so are we.

Thirty-six years later we work from three studios and on one set of drawings. A tower in Singapore is drawn by night in London and checked by morning in New York — and every building is visited again one and three years after the keys are handed over.
