Our Story

Built by the people who clean up

For ten years our founders flew in after breaches. The pattern never changed: the warning was there, nobody had time to read it.

So they built the opposite. Software that reads every signal and says why it matters, and a team awake at every hour to act on it. We still measure ourselves by one number: how long an attacker stays inside.
