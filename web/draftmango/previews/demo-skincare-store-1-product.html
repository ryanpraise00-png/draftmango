C15 Bright Serum

30 ml · one-time

$42

Brighter in four weeks, steady every morning.

Vitamin C is the most studied antioxidant in skin care, and the pure form, L-ascorbic acid, is the one that reaches the skin. It fades the faint patches the sun leaves, evens the tone and helps your SPF do its job. Vitamin E and ferulic acid keep it stable, the amber glass keeps the light out.

  • Fades dull, uneven patches
  • Defends against daylight and city air
  • Sits well under cream and SPF, no pilling

Full list

Water, ascorbic acid, propanediol, glycerin, ethoxydiglycol, tocopherol, ferulic acid, sodium hyaluronate, panthenol, xanthan gum, sodium citrate, citric acid, phenoxyethanol.

No fragrance, essential oils, alcohol denat. or silicones.

  1. 01Cleanse, then pat dryVitamin C works best on dry skin; damp skin can make it sting.
  2. 02Three to four dropsPress into face and neck in the morning. A light tingle in the first week is normal.
  3. 03Moisturise, then SPFWait a minute, follow with cream and your daily SPF 30 or higher.
  4. 04Keep it cool, use in 3 monthsThe cap closed and away from the window; a slight honey tint over time is normal.
