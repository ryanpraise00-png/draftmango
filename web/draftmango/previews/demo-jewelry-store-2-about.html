Our Story

It began with a ring that needed resetting

Recycled gold, lab-grown stones, since 2014

Simon Fairleigh brought his grandmother’s ring to a goldsmith in Charleston in 2012, to have the stone reset. The goldsmith was Helena Linwood, back from eight years at a bench in Florence. She talked him out of a new setting and into keeping the old claws, and he talked her into opening her own workshop.

Two years later she did: one bench in a rented room above a frame shop on Queen Street, a second-hand rolling mill and a single design, the oval signet we still make as the Cachet. Simon, who had spent ten years grading and buying stones in New York, joined in 2016, the year the workshop moved to King Street with a small boutique at the front.

Today twenty-six of us work at King Street: twelve goldsmiths at seven benches, a stone desk, a small studio that answers clients and packs every sand box by hand. We sell our own pieces beside two independent houses we admire, Maison Lune for pearls and hoops and Thale & Co. for fine chains and bands, and we look after all of them the same way.

Milestones

Twelve years at the bench

From one rented room to King Street: the years we changed how we work, not the years we grew. Keep scrolling, the rail moves with you.

One bench on Queen Street

Chapter 02 2016

What We Use

Materials we can stand behind

Fine jewelry is mostly metal and stone, so that is where our care starts. Here is what goes into every piece, and what never does.

01 / 03

Recycled gold, alloyed here

Owen Kinsley refines old jewelry and bench scrap into fine gold, then alloys it to 14k or 18k in our casting room. Solid all the way through: never plated, never hollow, never newly mined.

02 / 03

Lab-grown stones, chosen loose

Diamonds and emeralds grown, not mined, graded independently, the report in the box. Simon picks each one loose, under daylight; pearls, sapphires and morganites come from cutters we know by name.

03 / 03

Made to last, then looked after

Claws checked twice, four polishing passes, a hallmark inside. Then Lifetime Care: free cleaning, claw checks, re-plating and resizing for as long as you wear it.
