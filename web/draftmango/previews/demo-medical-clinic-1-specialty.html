Cardiology · 7 cardiologists

Heart care, close to home.

Blood pressure, palpitations, cholesterol or a check after a scare: our cardiologists see you within the week, and most tests happen in the same building.

Next available: Tue · Downtown

What We Treat

Everyday heart care, done properly.

Most of what we see is common and very treatable. We explain what the numbers mean and agree the plan with you.

  • High blood pressure
  • Chest pain
  • Palpitations and arrhythmia
  • High cholesterol
  • Heart failure
  • Care after a heart attack
  • Shortness of breath
  • Heart murmurs

No referral needed on most plans. Some HMO plans ask for one: your primary doctor can send it the same day.

Tests & Treatments

Most tests, one floor down.

The Downtown cardiology suite runs every test below; Worthington handles ECGs and monitors.

  • 10 min

    ECG

    A quick tracing of your heart’s rhythm, done during your visit.

  • 45 min

    Echocardiogram

    An ultrasound that shows how your heart pumps and how the valves move.

  • 60–90 min

    Stress Test

    A walk on the treadmill while we watch your heart at work.

  • 24–48 h

    Holter Monitor

    A small recorder you wear at home to catch rhythms a visit can miss.

  • 15 min

    Coronary Calcium Score

    A low-dose scan that helps decide whether you need a statin.

  • Monthly

    Blood Pressure Clinic

    Short check-ins with a nurse, in person or on video, until the numbers settle.

Your First Visit

About an hour, start to plan.

Bring your medicines (or a photo of the labels) and any home blood pressure readings.

  1. 01

    Forms in the Portal

    Fill in your history at home; it takes about ten minutes.

  2. 02

    Check-In

    Arrive fifteen minutes early with your photo ID and insurance card.

  3. 03

    Visit and ECG

    45–60 minutes with your cardiologist, with an ECG in the same room.

  4. 04

    Your Plan

    A written plan in the portal within a day, copied to your primary doctor.
