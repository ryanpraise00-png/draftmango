Twelve weeks from audit to an agent in production.

Five phases, two milestones you can hold us to, and a results report every month after launch.

The twelve-week plan

W1 W2 W3 W4 W5 W6 W7 W8 W9 W10 W11 W12

  1. Weeks 1–2

    Discover

    Workflow map, baseline metrics and a ranked shortlist.

  2. Weeks 2–3

    Design

    The agent's blueprint, its guardrails and who approves what.

  3. Weeks 4–7

    Pilot

    30 Days

    One agent on real data, measured every week.

  4. Weeks 8–12

    Deploy

    5 Weeks

Phase by Phase

What happens, and what you keep.

Discover

2weeks

Weeks 1–2 of 12

We sit with the people who do the work, map each workflow step by step and time it. You leave with the numbers that decide what to automate first.

Deliverables

  • Workflow map
  • Baseline metrics
  • Ranked use-case shortlist
  • ROI model for the top three

Design

2weeks

Weeks 2–3 of 12

We draw the agent before we build it: what it reads, what it may do, and where it must stop and ask. Your risk team signs off the guardrails on paper.

Deliverables

  • Agent blueprint
  • Guardrails and approval rules
  • Evaluation set of real cases
  • The pilot's success metric

4weeks

Weeks 4–7 of 12

One agent runs on real data from week four, with a person approving anything that matters. Every Friday you see the same three numbers: time saved, accuracy and exceptions.

Deliverables

  • A working agent on real data
  • Weekly scorecard
  • Evaluation report
  • Go or no-go recommendation

Who Is Involved

Deploy

5weeks

Weeks 8–12 of 12

The agent goes into production in week eight and reaches the whole team by week twelve. We train the people who work beside it and hand over everything we built.

Deliverables

  • Production rollout
  • Team training
  • Runbook and documentation
  • Monitoring dashboard

Who Is Involved

Draftmango

Optimise

Monthly

Each month we tune the agent against its numbers and add the next workflow from the shortlist. You get a results report, and you can stop any month.

Deliverables

  • Monthly results report
  • Tuning and model updates
  • The next workflow, live
  • Quarterly roadmap review
