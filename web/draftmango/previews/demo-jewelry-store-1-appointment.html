What to Expect

An unhurried hour, or half of one

  1. 01

    We prepare a tray

    From your note, a jeweler sets out pieces and stones to try, in your sizes and metals.

  2. 02

    You try, we listen

    Compare stones in daylight, try widths side by side, ask anything. Nothing is sold on the day unless you want it to be.

  3. 03

    A written summary

    The pieces you liked, the sizes and a quote arrive by email that evening, held for 14 days.
