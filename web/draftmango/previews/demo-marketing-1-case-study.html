The brief

Fizzwick had great flavours and a shelf full of bigger brands. They wanted Gen Z to pick the can up — and post about it — before summer ran out.

The idea

Make the most unboring can in the fridge.

One rule for every creator: no slow pours, no pack shots — just summer, with a can in it.
