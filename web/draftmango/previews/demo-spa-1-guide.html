Your first visit, gently explained.

What to bring, when to arrive, how the circuit works and what happens in the treatment room. Everything we would tell you at the desk.

Your Visit

From the gate to the garden.

  1. The day before

    A reminder

    We text the time and a few questions about your health.

  2. −30 min

    Arrive

    Park by the garden gate; tea is ready at the desk.

  3. −20 min

    Change

    A robe, slippers and a locker for your phone.

  4. Your hour

    Circuit & treatment

    Your therapist meets you in the lounge.

  5. After

    Leave slowly

    Stay in the garden as long as you like, until 9 pm.

What to bring

Bring

Just a swimsuit.

  • A swimsuit for the circuit
  • Your gift card or booking name
  • Contact lenses out, or their case
  • An empty afternoon

We Provide

Everything else.

  • Robe, slippers and towels
  • A locker with a key on a wristband
  • Shampoo, conditioner, body oil and hair dryers
  • Tea, water and fruit all day

Booking

Two to three weeks for a weekend, a few days for a weekday. Members book up to 30 days ahead.
Up to 24 hours before, at no cost. Later than that we charge half the booking, because the room and the therapist were kept for you.
Yes, even on a day pass: we cap it at 24 guests so it stays calm. Treatments of 60 minutes or more include it.

Treatments

For a first visit, a 60-minute Swedish Massage. Deep Tissue is for knots you already know about; Hot Stone for cold winter shoulders.
Of course. Tell us when you book and we will hold the right person for you.
What you are comfortable in. You are covered by a warm sheet the whole time, and only the area being worked on is uncovered.

Thermal Circuit

Yes, on the whole circuit. Robes, slippers, towels and lockers are ours.
Not at all. A cool shower beside it does most of the work.
Twelve to fifteen minutes at most, less on your first round. Leave the moment you want to.

Health & Pregnancy

After 14 weeks, yes, for a Prenatal Massage, the warm pool and the lounge. Please skip the sauna, the steam room and the plunge.
Tell us when you book. High blood pressure, heart conditions and recent surgery change what we suggest; some need a note from your doctor.
Guests 16 and over. Under 18s come with an adult and stay off the circuit after 6 pm.

House Policies

Three things, kindly put.

  • Changes & cancellations

    Free up to 24 hours before. Later, or not at all, and we charge half the booking.

  • Running late

    Your treatment still ends on time, so the next guest's does too. Call us and we will do what we can.

  • Gratuity

    Not included. If you would like to leave one, 18–20% at the desk goes straight to your therapist.
