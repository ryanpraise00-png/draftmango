The Small Print

Policies, plainly.

A single class is valid for 30 days, the 5-pack for three months and the 10-pack for six, from the day you buy it. The intro offer — 3 classes for $99 — runs 30 days from your first visit.
Memberships are month to month. Email the desk 30 days before your next billing date and that's it — no exit fee, no phone call.
Up to eight weeks a year, for $15 a month — free with a doctor's note. Travel, injuries and new babies all count.
Not on the 8-a-month membership — it resets on your billing date. Packs keep their classes until they expire.
Cancel up to 12 hours before class at no charge. Later than that it's $20, and a no-show is $25. Privates need 24 hours.
Many plans cover pilates with a letter of medical necessity. Check with your provider, then ask the desk for an itemized receipt.

Gift a first class.

Gift cards in any amount, by email or at the desk. The $25 first class makes a good one.
