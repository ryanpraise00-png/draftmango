Lending disclosures. Loans are made by a partner bank, Member FDIC, or by Draftmango Funding Lending LLC under state licenses; your loan agreement names the lender. All loans are subject to credit approval and are not available in all states. Rates, terms and amounts depend on your business’s revenue, time in business and credit; the lowest rates go to the most qualified borrowers. Checking your rate uses a soft credit check that does not affect your credit score; a hard inquiry happens only if you accept an offer.

APR example. A $50,000 term loan repaid in 12 monthly payments of $4,629.33 (19.9% interest plus a 2.5% origination fee of $1,250 deducted from the loan proceeds) has total payments of $55,551.96 and an annual percentage rate of 24.83%. Payment and APR examples are for illustration only.

SBA 7(a) loans are made under the U.S. Small Business Administration’s loan program; Draftmango Funding is not affiliated with or endorsed by the SBA. Time-to-funds figures are averages for approved applications and are not guaranteed.
