Consumer & Retail

Putting the margin back into 140 stores.

How Halden Retail added 310 basis points of EBITDA margin in 14 months — and kept it after we left.

Client
Halden Retail UK grocery and home, £2.1bn sales
Industry
Consumer & Retail 140 stores, 11,000 colleagues
Length
14 months March 2024 – May 2025
Team
2 partners, 9 consultants and 12 from Halden
Capabilities
Pricing & Commercial Operations & Supply Chain

  1. 01 The Challenge

    A growing chain with a shrinking margin.

    Halden had grown to 140 stores and £2.1bn of sales, but its EBITDA margin had slipped 260 basis points since 2021. Forty thousand lines were priced by rule of thumb, and stores held six weeks of stock on lines that sold in two.

  2. 02 The Approach

    Three weeks to find it. Four waves to fix it.

    A price waterfall for every category showed where the margin went. We repriced in four waves, category by category, testing each in twelve stores first — and reset store ordering with the managers who run it.

  3. 03 The Result

    Stores that run it without us.

    Fourteen months on, margin was up 310 basis points and stock cover down 11% — and every store was pricing and ordering on the new model, run by Halden's own team.
