Landowners

Farms keep farming.

A wind or solar lease takes a few acres out of a section and pays for decades. Here is what signing one with us means.

  1. 01A payment every yearFixed lease payments for the life of the plant, rising each year with inflation.
  2. 02Crops and cattle stayYou plant right up to the turbine pads; sheep graze our solar rows.
  3. 03Roads and drainage put rightField roads, tile lines and fences we cross are repaired at our cost.
  4. 04The land stays yoursWe lease, we never buy, and the site is restored when the lease ends.

Communities

$2.4M

a year in community funds, decided locally.

Every plant puts money back into its county, and a board of neighbors decides where it goes. Most of it ends up in schools, fire halls and roads.

  • $180,000 · Story County, Iowa

    Sixty scholarships a year

    For high-school seniors heading to college or a two-year trade program.

  • $650,000 · Socorro County, New Mexico

    A new volunteer fire hall

    Two engine bays and a training room, opened next to Red Mesa in 2025.

  • $1.1M · Carbon County, Wyoming

    Twelve miles of county road

    Rebuilt to carry turbine loads — and left better than we found it.
