Your jean size without a fitting room: three measurements that do it

Waist, hip and inseam, taken on a pair you already love. It’s how we match jeans at the counter, and it works on your kitchen table too.

Sophie NylandFit Lead, Austin South
  1. 01Measure the waistband, then double it
  2. 02Measure the seat, under the fly
  3. 03Measure the inseam to the hem
01 · Waist 02 · Hip 03 · Inseam Before you order

Most of the jeans that come back to us don’t come back because they’re badly made. They come back because the size on the label meant something different in the last store.

So we stopped asking people for their size and started asking for three numbers. Take them on the pair of jeans that fits you best, button done up, laid flat on a table. You need a soft tape and ten minutes.

Step 01Measure the waistband, then double it

Lay the jeans face up and smooth the waistband flat. Measure straight across the top edge, from one side seam to the other, and double the number. That’s your jean size: a 15.5-inch band is a waist 31.

From the counterHalf the people we measure wear a size bigger than they need, because last year’s jeans stretched out. Ours are washed twice and won’t.

Step 02Measure the seat, under the fly

Go down to the bottom of the fly and measure straight across, side seam to side seam, then double it. This is the number that decides the cut. If your hip is more than ten inches bigger than your waist, a Relaxed will feel better than a Straight in the same size.

  • Slim fits a hip up to your waist plus 9 inches.
  • Straight fits up to waist plus 10, Wide up to plus 11.
  • Relaxed gives you the most room, up to waist plus 11.5.

Let the Fit Finder do the maths. Slide your three numbers into the Denim Fit Guide and it names your cut, size and length, and tells you if you’ll need a hem.

Step 03Measure the inseam to the hem

Fold one leg back and measure the inside seam, from the crotch to the bottom of the hem. Under 30.5 inches, take a Short; up to 32.5, a Regular; above that, a Long. A little long is fine: we hem for free in all four stores.

Free, while you waitAustin South and Houston Heights chain-stitch hems in about twenty minutes. East Side and the Pearl have them ready the next day.

One last thing: if you like your jeans to sit higher or lower than the pair you measured, tell us. The rise changes everything, and we’d rather swap a pair than have you put up with it.
