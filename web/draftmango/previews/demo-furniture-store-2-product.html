The Shape

One curve, back to arms

Karin drew the Sola in 2019 for a flat in Copenhagen with one good window. The back sweeps forward into the arms in a single line, so it looks right from every side of a room, not only from the front.

Made of

Wool, springs, solid oak

  • Wool 80%, polyamide 20%, dense and matte
  • Pocket springs under high-resilience foam
  • Kiln-dried beech and oak frame, glued and screwed
