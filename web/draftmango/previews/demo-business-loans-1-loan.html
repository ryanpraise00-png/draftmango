Line of Credit · Up to $250K

Draw it when you need it. Pay for what you use.

A revolving line for the gaps every business has: stock before the season, payroll before the big invoice clears, the repair that can’t wait.

How a Line Works

Borrow, repay, borrow again.

  1. 01

    Get a limit

    Apply once. Most owners know their limit, from $10K to $250K, within a couple of hours.

  2. 02

    Draw what you need

    Move money to your checking account in the app. Draws before 3pm ET land the same day.

  3. 03

    Repay and reuse

    Each draw is repaid over 6 or 12 months. As you repay, that part of the limit opens up again.
