Date by Date

What each deadline asks of you.

Calendar-year filers. If your business uses a fiscal year, your dates shift with it; ask us for your own list.

  1. FriJan 15

    Fourth-quarter estimated tax

    Form 1040-ES, payment 4 of 4 for 2026

    The last estimate for income earned September through December. Paying now keeps the underpayment penalty off your return.

    Self-employedInvestors

  2. MonFeb 1

    W-2s and 1099-NEC

    To workers, contractors and the government

    January 31 falls on a Sunday, so the date moves to Monday. Collect contractor W-9s now and this one takes an afternoon.

    EmployersAnyone paying contractors

  3. MonMar 15

    Partnership and S-corp returns

    Form 1065 and Form 1120-S, or Form 7004

    The owners’ K-1s come from these returns, so they are due a month before personal returns. An extension moves filing to September 15.

    PartnershipsS-corps

  4. ThuApr 15

    Tax Day

    Form 1040, first 2027 estimate, Form 4868, C-corp Form 1120

    Personal returns, or an extension to October 15. An extension is more time to file, not to pay: what you owe is still due today.

    IndividualsC-corps

  5. TueJun 15

    Second-quarter estimated tax

    Form 1040-ES, payment 2 of 4

    Only two months after the first one. Our Year-Round clients get their mid-year projection the week before.

    Self-employedInvestors

  6. WedSep 15

    Third quarter and extended business returns

    Form 1040-ES payment 3 of 4, extended 1065 and 1120-S

    The busiest date of the fall: estimates for individuals, and the final date for extended partnership and S-corp returns.

    Self-employedBusinesses

  7. FriOct 15

    Extended personal returns

    Form 1040 under an April extension

    The final date for 2026 personal returns that were extended. There is no further federal extension after this one.

    Individuals

Missed a Date?

File late, pay late, or both.

Each costs something different. Filing on time, even without the money, is almost always the cheaper mistake.

Talk to a CPA Today
Filing late 5% a month Of the tax still unpaid, for each month or part of a month the return is late, up to 25%.
Paying late 0.5% a month Of the unpaid tax, up to 25%, plus interest. A payment plan lowers the rate while it runs.
Short on estimates Interest per quarter Worked out like interest on each quarter’s shortfall, from its due date until it is paid.
