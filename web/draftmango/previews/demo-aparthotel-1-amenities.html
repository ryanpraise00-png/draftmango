The Service

A rhythm you set, a team you know.

  • Hosts on message 7 am – 11 pm, a person on call at night
  • Parcels signed for and put in your locker
  • Bins and recycling emptied from the landing
  • A full clean on the weekday you pick, about 90 minutes
  • Fresh linen and towels, extra sets in the wardrobe
  • Basics topped up: soap, coffee, tea, dish tabs
  • A deep clean: oven, windows, behind the sofa
  • A check-up of the boiler, lights and smoke alarm
  • One invoice for the month, in euros or dollars

Arrival to Departure

No keys to collect, no desk to queue at.

  1. Any time before

    Book online, no deposit

    Pick the layout and the dates. Stays of 28 nights or more pay month by month, not up front.

  2. 48 hours before

    Your door code arrives

    A personal code for the gate and the apartment, valid from 3 pm on arrival day. No queue at a desk.

  3. Every week

    Cleaned, fresh linen

    Housekeeping comes on the weekday you choose. Towels and bed linen are changed every visit.

  4. When plans change

    Extend with a message

    Staying on? Message the hosts and the dates move, at the rate of your new total length.
