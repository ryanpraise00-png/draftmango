Injectables · Fine Lines

Neuromodulator, dosed by the unit.

A few precise units soften forehead, frown and eye lines while your face still moves. Twenty minutes, no downtime, a review at two weeks.

What It Treats

Lines that come from movement.

The neuromodulator relaxes the small muscles that fold the skin when you frown, squint or raise your brows. We dose by the unit, so you pay for what your face needs and nothing more.

  • Forehead lines10–20 units
  • Frown lines15–25 units
  • Crow's feet10–24 units
  • Jaw clenching40–60 units

What to Expect

From the chair to the review.

  1. Day 0

    The Treatment

    A few small injections in twenty minutes. Little marks fade within the hour.

  2. Days 3–5

    It Begins

    The lines start to soften as the muscles relax, gradually and evenly.

  3. Week 2

    Your Review

    Full effect. We take photos in the same light and add a free top-up if needed.

  4. Months 3–4

    Next Visit

    Movement returns slowly. Most patients book again at three to four months.

Aftercare

  • Stay upright for four hours
  • No exercise or facials for 24 hours
  • No pressure on the area the same day
  • Make-up is fine after a few hours

Not For You If

  • You are pregnant or breastfeeding
  • You have a neuromuscular condition
  • There is an infection in the area
  • You have an event in the next 10 days
