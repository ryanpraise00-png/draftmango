The Theatre

Lakeside Theatre.

A 600-seat house by the lake, twenty minutes from the studios. Dancers arrive ninety minutes before curtain at the stage door; the front of house opens an hour before.

  • Red Line to Grand, then a 12-minute walk east
  • Garage parking next door, $14 for the evening
  • No filming from the seats; the full film comes to every family
