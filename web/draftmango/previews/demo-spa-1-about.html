Nobody rushes here.

A day spa and thermal house among the vines of Sonoma, open since 2011: nine treatment rooms, four temperatures and a garden under old olive trees.

Since 2011

It started with three rooms and a borrowed sauna.

Draftmango Spa opened in an old stone press house on Vineyard Lane with two therapists and one rule: nobody rushes. No back-to-back bookings, no music you have to listen to, no sales at the end of a facial.

I joined as a therapist in 2015 and have run the house since 2019. We have grown one room at a time, added the circuit and the garden, and kept the same quiet.

— Hannah Lowell, Spa Director

What We Keep

Three small habits.

  1. 01

    Fifteen minutes between guests

    Every therapist has time to reset the room, warm the bed and wash their hands slowly.

  2. 02

    Rooms at 24 °C

    Warm enough to forget you are undressed, with a heated bed under the linen.

  3. 03

    Phones in lockers

    Past the lounge door the house is quiet. Even ours stay at the desk.
