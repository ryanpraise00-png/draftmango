A lab-grown diamond is carbon crystal, the same as a mined one, grown over a few months instead of a few billion years. Ours is cut as an elongated oval (1.4 : 1), graded independently, and the report travels in the box.

What the report tells you

Solid 18k gold, 100% recycled and alloyed at our bench: never plated, never hollow. Soak it in warm water with a drop of soap, brush behind the claws with a soft brush, dry it on cotton. Bring it in once a year and we check the claws for free.

The care guide

Free insured delivery in the US, signed for. Size 6 in yellow gold ships in two days; every other size and gold is made to order in four weeks. Overnight $35; Canada $45 insured. Returns within 30 days, insured both ways, with a free label.

Shipping and returns

Unsure of the size? Order the free sizer, it posts in two days, or choose your best guess: resizing is free for life, up to two sizes. Engraving is cut by hand inside the band, up to 18 characters, in our serif or in script.

Four Weeks at the Bench

How your Aube is made

Each ring is cast from gold we recycle ourselves, set by Iris Selwyn under the loupe, then polished in four passes. You get a photo from the bench before it ships.

  1. Week 01

    Cast in recycled gold

    Your gold is alloyed to 18k and cast into the tapered band.

  2. Week 02

    Set by hand

    The oval is seated low and the four claws are shaped around it.

  3. Week 03

    Finished slowly

    Four polishing passes, then the claws are checked twice.

  4. Week 04

    Engraved and boxed

    Hallmarked, engraved if you asked, and sent insured.
