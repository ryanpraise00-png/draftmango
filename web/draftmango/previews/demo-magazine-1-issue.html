No. 42, The Slow Issue

A season of long lunches, small gardens and journeys taken at the speed of a train. Twelve features, nine departments, one very patient potter.

From the Editor

On not hurrying

We planned this issue for two years and then, fittingly, pushed it back a season. The stories needed time: you cannot report on a year by visiting for a weekend.

What you are holding is the slowest issue we have made, and I think the best. Read it out of order. Leave it on the table. Come back to it in June.

Mira CastellaneEditor in Chief
