On the Board

Three classes. One program.

Every class follows the same six-week block, so the days you train fit together.

Small-Group Strength A main lift, accessories and a short finisher, coached for twelve or fewer. 50 min · Cap 12 · 30 a week Conditioning Intervals on bikes, rowers, sleds and ropes. Hard work, planned to the minute. 45 min · Cap 16 · 26 a week Barbell Club Squat, bench, deadlift and the Olympic lifts, with a coach at your bar. 75 min · Coached · 12 a week

How Booking Works

Book. Show up. Or cancel early.

  1. 01

    Seven Days Ahead

    Classes open for booking in the member app a week out, at the minute they start.

  2. 02

    The Waitlist Moves

    Full class? Join the waitlist. You move up automatically until two hours before, and we text you.

  3. 03

    12 Hours to Cancel

    Cancel 12 hours out and nothing happens. Later, or a no-show, is a $20 fee, so the spot goes to someone else.
