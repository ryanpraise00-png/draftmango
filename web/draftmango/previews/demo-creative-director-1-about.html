About Vera

Close to the camera

Creative director for campaigns, brand films and art direction. Copenhagen-based, represented in London, on set wherever the brief takes it.

The Story

Designer, art director, then the whole picture.

I grew up in Aarhus, studied visual communication in Copenhagen and joined Fjordhus as a designer in 2011. Within three years I was art directing their fashion clients, and by 2017 I ran the creative side of their biggest accounts.

In 2019 I moved to London as executive creative director of Hale & Morrow, a department of forty people across campaigns, film and content. We won a lot, and I spent most of it in meetings.

In 2024 I came back to Copenhagen and went independent. Now I take on six to eight briefs a year and stay with each from the pitch to the final grade, with a small, senior crew chosen for the work.

How I Work

Three rules I don’t break.

01

One idea per brief

If it takes two sentences to explain, it isn’t ready. Every route I pitch fits on a single line.

02

Real people, when it fits

Clubs, workshops and streets before agency books. A face nobody has seen sells better than a famous one.

03

Stay until the grade

The idea that won the pitch is the one that ships. I sit in the edit, the grade and the retouch.
