The first ten minutes matter most.

While the phone rings, these steps keep the evidence intact and the damage small.

DoNow

  • Unplug the network cable or turn off Wi-Fi on machines that look affected.
  • Leave them powered on: memory holds evidence we'll need.
  • Talk by phone or a personal device, not company email or chat.
  • Write down what you saw and when, even if it seems small.

Don'tYet

  • Don't pay or reply to a ransom note before we've spoken.
  • Don't wipe, reimage or restore from backup.
  • Don't reset every password at once; it can tip off the attacker.
  • Don't post about it, internally or outside.
