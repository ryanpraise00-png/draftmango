A Day at the Lodge

Nothing to plan. Everything on time.

  1. 7:00

    Morning practice

    Ninety minutes on the deck, or in the hall if it rains.

  2. 8:30

    Breakfast

    Oats, eggs, fruit and very good coffee.

  3. 10:00

    Walk or rest

    A forest trail, a book by the stove, or a sauna.

  4. 12:30

    Lunch

    The long table, everyone together.

  5. 4:30

    Yin or Nidra

    Slow and quiet before dinner.

  6. 6:30

    Dinner

    Three courses from the lodge kitchen.

  7. 8:30

    Quiet hours

    Phones off in the common rooms, lights low.

Rates
