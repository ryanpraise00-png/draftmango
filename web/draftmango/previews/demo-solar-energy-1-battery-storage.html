How Backup Works

An outage, hour by hour.

A summer storm takes the street down at 7:40 pm. This is what your house does, without you touching a thing.

  1. 7:40 pm

    The grid drops

    The battery’s gateway sees it first and cuts your home off from the street, as the code requires.

  2. Under 1 s

    Stored power takes over

    Lights stay on, clocks don’t blink and the Wi-Fi keeps streaming.

  3. Overnight

    The essentials run

    Fridge, lights, fans, outlets and the pool pump timer, on one battery through the night.

  4. 6:15 am

    The sun refills it

    Panels power the house and recharge the battery, even if the grid is still out.
