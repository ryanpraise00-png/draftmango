The House · Opened 2025

Ten floors above the Avenida, built for slow mornings.

We kept the concrete frame of a 1960s office block and built a hotel around it: pale fins against the sun, smoked oak and linen inside, and glass from floor to ceiling wherever the city deserves it.

Now it holds 84 rooms and suites, Lume and its open fire on the ground floor, a gym and steam room below, and an eighteen-metre pool on the roof. Big enough for everything, small enough that the concierge knows your name.
