My story

Three chapters, and the one I'm writing now.

  1. One2007–2016

    The HR Years

    I joined a Bristol engineering firm at 24 and stayed ten years, the last three as Head of People. I ran the restructures, sat in on the exit interviews, and kept meeting brilliant people who knew exactly what was wrong with their working lives and couldn't see a way out.

    I was one of them. I just didn't know it yet.

  2. Two2016

    The Wall

    In the spring of 2016 I stopped sleeping, then stopped being able to open an email without my chest tightening. My GP signed me off for six months.

    Somewhere in month three, on the sofa with a library book I couldn't finish, I wrote down one question: what would I do if I wasn't afraid of wasting the last ten years?

  3. Three2017 to now

    Coaching

    I retrained, took my first clients from a borrowed desk in a Bristol co-working space, and found that everything from the HR years was useful after all. I became an ICF Professional Certified Coach in 2021 and wrote The Second Chapter in 2025.

    Today I work from a small studio by the harbour and on video with people all over the world. More than 700 of them so far.

What I believe.

  1. 01

    Small steps beat big leaps.

    Nobody needs to quit on Monday. We find the smallest move that tells you something, then the next one.

  2. 02

    You know more than you think.

    My job isn't to hand you answers. It's to ask the questions that bring yours to the surface.

  3. 03

    Rest is part of the plan.

    A plan that only works if you're exhausted isn't a plan. We build the pauses in from the start.
