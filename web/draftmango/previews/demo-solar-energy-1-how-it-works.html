The Five Steps

What happens, and when.

Olivia from Customer Care is your one contact from the first call to the day the app shows power.

  1. 1Within 48 hours

    Free Assessment

    Send your address and last bill. We size the roof from satellite imagery, count the sun it gets and email a first layout with a price for each way to pay.

  2. 2Week 1

    Custom Design

    A NABCEP-certified designer visits: roof, attic, rafters and the main electrical panel. You sign off a final layout and one fixed price.

  3. 3Weeks 2–6

    Permits & Utility

    We file the city permit, the HOA form where there is one and the utility’s interconnection request, and follow each one up until it is approved.

  4. 4Usually 1 day

    Install Day

    A crew of three or four, led by Ben Tolliver’s team, mounts the panels, inverters and any battery. A battery adds half a day.

  5. 5Weeks 6–10

    Power On

    The city inspects, the utility gives permission to operate, and we switch on with you and set up the app. Your first lower bill follows the next cycle.

Install Day

Done before the heat.

Crews start at sunrise in summer. You can stay home or go to work; we only need the garage and the main panel.

  1. 7:00

    The crew arrives

    A walk-round with you, ladders up, drive and yard covered.

  2. 7:30

    Mounts and rails

    Each mount flashed into a rafter, tiles lifted and reset.

  3. 10:00

    Panels on

    Microinverters clipped under, panels set row by row.

  4. 1:00

    Wired in

    Conduit down the wall, the disconnect and the panel tie-in.

  5. 3:00

    Walkthrough

    Photos of every mount sent to you, the yard swept, gone.
