Our Story

Built one signature at a time.

  1. 1998Three partners, one floor

    Draftmango Partners opens on Congress Street with 14 people and 40 audit clients.

  2. 2004PCAOB registration

    We register with the PCAOB and sign our first listed-company audit. The firm moves to 100 Federal Street.

  3. 2011Tax and Advisory practices

    Clients ask us to do more than audit. Tax, valuations and transaction advice join under one engagement letter.

  4. 2018Coast to coast, and Toronto

    Offices open in Denver, San Francisco, Seattle and Toronto for clients that grew across the border.

  5. 20261,200 people, 2,400 clients

    Nine offices, 140 partners and the same rule as in 1998: a partner leads every engagement.

Three rules we don't bend.

Written on the first page of every engagement letter since 1998.

  1. 01

    Sign only what we would defend

    If a number would not survive a regulator's question, it does not leave the building with our name on it.

  2. 02

    The same team next year

    Staff turnover on client teams is 9%, so the people who learned your business are there to use it.

  3. 03

    Say it early

    Bad news travels at interim, in writing, with a fix attached. Nobody hears about an adjustment in the board meeting first.
