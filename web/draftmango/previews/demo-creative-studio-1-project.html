Aurora Labs: a science centre that works after dark.Case Study

An identity and a wayfinding system for a science centre that does its best work after dark.

Client
Ghent science centre
Services
Identity, Wayfinding, Motion
Year
2026
Team
6 people, 14 weeks

The Challenge

Aurora Labs moved its best programme to the evening: talks under the dome, light works by the water, late labs for adults. The old signs were made for school groups at ten in the morning. After dark they simply disappeared, and visitors lost their way between the halls.

Our Approach

We started at night, walking the building with the people who run it. The identity grew from one idea, light that finds you, and the wayfinding takes it literally: lit columns, one typeface and colours that still read in the dark. Motion carries the same marks onto the screens and the dome.
