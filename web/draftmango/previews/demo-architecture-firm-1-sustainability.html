How we measure.

The same four steps on a garden pavilion and on a 48-storey tower.

  1. 01BaselineBefore a line is drawn: what a typical building of this type would emit.
  2. 02ModelEvery option carries its carbon count, beside its cost and its floor area.
  3. 03BuildMaterial passports on site; every substitution is counted again.
  4. 04PublishThe result goes in The Carbon Ledger, whether it beat the baseline or not.

Materials

Keep, then timber, then the rest.

  • Reuse FirstThe greenest building is often the one already standing. We audit it before we design.
  • Timber FramesMass timber up to twelve storeys, sourced from certified forests within 800 km.
  • Leaner ConcreteWhere concrete is needed, less of it, with half the cement replaced.
  • Daylight Before PlantShade, cross-ventilation and daylight first; machines only for what is left.
