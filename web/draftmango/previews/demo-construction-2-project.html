Hillsboro Ridge House

A cedar and dark-brick family home on a steep wooded lot in Brentwood, handed over six days ahead of schedule.

The Brief

A family of five, a forty-foot drop and one season of good weather.

The Delaneys bought a wooded lot off Hillsboro Road that falls forty feet from the street to the creek. Their architect set the house into the slope: an entry level at the road, a double-height great room below, and a screened porch over the trees.

We stepped the foundation down the hill, framed the house over the winter and had it dried in by March, before the spring rains. Brick, cedar and limestone came from within a hundred miles.

Emily Harlan ran the job and sent twenty-six Friday reports. After framing, there was not one change order.
