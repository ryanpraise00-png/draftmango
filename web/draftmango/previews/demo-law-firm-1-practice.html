§Practice · Transactions

Deals that reshape a company.

Public and private mergers, acquisitions, joint ventures and governance, for boards, founders and the investors behind them.

§ 01Overview

The deal is the easy part.

Price is agreed in a week. What takes the months is everything around it: the regulators, the financing, the people, the tax and the company that has to keep running while it is sold.

Our M&A partners run those threads as one plan. Specialists from tax, employment, antitrust and finance sit on the deal team from the first call, not when a problem surfaces.

Most of our clients come back for the next deal. Some have done twelve with us.

§ 02What We Do

Six kinds of mandate.

Often more than one on the same deal.

Mergers, tender offers and schemes for listed buyers and targets, with the proxy, the fairness process and the regulators in one plan.
Auctions and negotiated sales for founders, families and sponsors, from the letter of intent to the closing funds flow.
Structures that hold up when the partners disagree: governance, deadlock and exit terms written for the bad year.
Separating a business from its parent: transition services, shared contracts, people and IP, on a timetable that holds.
Board processes, special committees and conflicts, and the minutes that show the decision was made well.
Preparation before a campaign, and a calm response during one, with the board and its bankers.
