Manufacturing · Packaged Food · 3 Plants

Changeovers 38% faster. $4.2M in eleven months.

How three food plants stopped losing an hour on every switch, and kept the gain after we left.

Client
Harlow Foods, $240M revenue
Practice
Operations & Process Excellence
Length
3-week Diagnostic, 14-week Program, Retainer
Partner
Claire Ashdown

The Challenge

Seventy minutes per changeover.

Harlow runs nine packaging lines across three plants in Illinois and Wisconsin. Product counts had doubled in five years, and each line now switched products four times a shift. Every switch took about seventy minutes, and the year's budget had been missed by $3M.

Two equipment projects had already been approved. Megan Doyle, the COO, wanted to know whether they were needed before she spent the money.

Our Approach

One line first. Then the other eight.

The Diagnostic timed 160 changeovers. Sixty percent of the lost time came from waiting: for parts, for a mechanic, for a quality sign-off. None of it needed new equipment.

  • Standard work for each changeover, written with the operators
  • Parts and tools staged before the line stops
  • A daily tier meeting on every shift, ten minutes long
  • A weekly plan that groups products to cut the number of switches
