What to Bring

Props are here. Bring yourself.

  • Water

    A bottle, filled. There is a tap by the cubbies.

  • Layers

    Something warm for the end of class, light for the Garden Room.

  • A towel for the heat

    For the Garden Room. Ours are $2 if you forget.
