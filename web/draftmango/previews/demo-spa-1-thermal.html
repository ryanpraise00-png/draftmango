The four stops

85°C · 185 °F

Sauna

12–15 minutes

Dry heat on cedar benches. The top bench is the hottest; sit lower on your first round and climb when you feel ready.

Tip: sit on your towel, and leave the moment you want to.

45°C · 113 °F

Steam Room

10–15 minutes

Full humidity and a breath of eucalyptus. Lower heat than the sauna, and it feels warmer: the air is wet.

Tip: breathe through the nose, slowly.

34°C · 93 °F

Warm Pool

As long as you like

Indoors under tall windows onto the vines, heated and open all day. Where most guests start, and where many finish.

Tip: float on your back for one minute, ears under.

12°C · 54 °F

Cold Plunge

30 seconds to 2 minutes

Outdoors under the olives, in to the shoulders. The first ten seconds are the hard part; then your breath slows down.

Tip: walk in by the ladder, never jump.

How It Works

Heat, cold, rest. Three times.

  1. 01

    Heat

    10–15 min

    The sauna or the steam room, until you feel properly warm through.

  2. 02

    Cold

    30 s – 2 min

    The plunge, or the cool shower beside it if the plunge is too much today.

  3. 03

    Rest

    10 min

    A daybed in the lounge, a glass of water, nothing to do.

About an hour for three rounds. The warm pool fits anywhere in between.

On the Circuit

A few quiet rules.

  • Swimsuits on

    Robes, slippers and towels are ours to give.

  • Quiet voices

    The lounge is silent; the garden is for talking.

  • Phones in lockers

    Past the lounge door, nobody needs to reach you.

  • Drink water

    Jugs in every room; a glass after each round.

  • Guests 16+

    Up to 24 guests on the circuit at a time.

  • Ask first

    Pregnant, or a heart condition? Talk to us before the heat.
