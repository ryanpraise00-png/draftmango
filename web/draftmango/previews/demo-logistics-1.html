How a Shipment Moves

Six stops, and you see every one.

Every milestone lands in the portal and your inbox as it happens. When something slips, your coordinator calls before the system does.

  1. Same day

    Booked

    Rate confirmed, space held on the next sailing or flight, documents checked.

  3. Day 1–3

    Export Cleared

  4. Live

    In Transit

    Live position, and any delay flagged to you within the hour.
