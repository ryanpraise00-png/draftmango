Step by Step

Thinnest to thickest, two minutes in all.

Water-light formulas first, so each one reaches the skin; the richest last, to hold the rest in. That is the whole rule.

  1. 0:30

    Cleanse

    One pump on damp skin, thirty seconds of small circles, rinse with lukewarm water and pat dry.

  2. 0:45

    Treat

    Three to four drops of vitamin C pressed into face and neck. Give it a minute to settle before the next step.

  3. 0:45

    Protect

    Two finger-lengths of SPF 50 for face and neck, every morning, cloudy days too. It doubles as your moisturiser.

  1. 0:45

    Cleanse

    A scoop of balm on dry skin melts SPF and the day away; add water, it turns milky, then rinse.

  2. 0:30

    Repair

    Two drops of retinal on dry skin, two nights a week to start, then every other night after a month.

  3. 0:45

    Seal

    A pea of night cream over everything; on retinal-free nights it is all your skin needs.
