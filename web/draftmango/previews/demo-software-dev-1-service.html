Inside a Sprint

Two weeks, working software, every time.

The rhythm every product team runs, from the first sprint to the fiftieth.

  1. Day 1

    Plan

    1 hour

    You and the lead agree the sprint goal and what “done” means.

  2. Day 2–8

    Build

    Daily builds

    Small pull requests, reviewed within hours, on staging every day.

  3. Day 9

    Demo

    30 minutes

    We show working software, not slides — you click through it yourself.

  4. Day 10

    Release

    Staged rollout

    Behind a flag, to 5% of users first, with rollback one click away.
