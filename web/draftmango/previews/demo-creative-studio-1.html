How a project moves

Twelve weeks from the first workshop to launch day, and you're in the room at every turn.

  1. 1–2 Weeks

    Discover

    Workshops, interviews and a one-page brief we both sign.

  2. 3–4 Weeks

    Define

    Strategy, naming and two creative routes; you choose one.

  3. 5–10 Weeks

    Design

    The system built out in the open, with a review every Thursday.

  4. 11–12 Weeks

    Deliver

    Files, guidelines and launch support, then a check-in after 60 days.
