Our Story

Made slowly, in Los Angeles.

Draftmango Studio is a small team of pattern cutters, sewers and stylists. We design, cut and wash every piece in our own studio, ten minutes from the Melrose flagship.

One shirt, then a wardrobe.

Clara Evans and Tom Hollis met cutting patterns for other people’s labels. In 2019 they rented a corner of a garment workshop downtown and made one shirt for themselves: a men’s pattern, widened through the body, in linen washed three times until it fell soft. Friends asked for one, then their friends did. That shirt is still the Varenna.

Today the studio has its own skylit floor, twelve people and a flagship on Melrose. We still release small drops instead of big seasons, re-cut our essentials a little each year instead of reinventing them, and make sure every piece can be washed at home and worn for a long time.

Materials

Five fabrics, chosen to last.

We work with a short list of mills and tanneries we visit every year, and only with fibres that age well and wash at home.

  • European linen

    Rain-fed flax, woven in France and Belgium, garment-washed in LA.

  • Organic cotton

    Certified organic, for our tees and summer knits.

  • Grade-A cashmere

    Long fibres that pill less and soften with every wear.

  • Full-grain leather

    Vegetable-tanned, so it darkens and shapes to you.

  • Natural straw

    Raffia and palm leaf, woven by hand for our hats and totes.

How It’s Made

Three steps, one roof.

  1. Cut

    Patterns drafted and graded in the studio, cut by hand in small runs so nothing sits in a warehouse.

  2. Washed

    Garment-washed three times with a gentle enzyme, so the piece is soft and settled before you wear it.

  3. Finished

    Pressed, checked and packed by the person who sewed it, in plastic-free paper.
