Rooms · Skyline King

A wall of glass, the river beyond.

Twenty-four rooms on floors five to eight, each with floor-to-ceiling glass over the rooftops to the Tagus and an oak bench built into the window.

Twenty-six square metres, and the whole city in the window.

  • 26 m²
  • King bed, or two singles
  • Sleeps 2
  • Floors five to eight

The Skyline Kings face south-west, down the line of the Avenida to the river, so the light comes in low in the afternoon and turns the room gold before it sets behind the bridge. The glass is triple-glazed and the linen blackout curtains close tight for a late morning.

Inside: a low king bed made in Portuguese linen, an oak window bench with cushions, a paper lamp, a desk in black steel and a bathroom in warm grey stone with a walk-in rain shower. The wine on the table is from a small producer in Alentejo; the chocolate is from a maker in Príncipe Real.
