Climate Correspondent

Celia Renner

Celia covers the harbor, the weather and the people who plan for both. Since joining Draftmango Daily in 2019 she has reported from three continents on how cities adapt to rising water, and from every one of the city's 14 flood zones.

Before that she spent six years at a regional public radio station. She grew up two blocks from the old ferry terminal and still takes the boat to work.
