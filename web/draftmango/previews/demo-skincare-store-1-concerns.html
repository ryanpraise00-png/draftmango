Six concerns, one routine each.

Dryness

Tight by noon, flaky by evening.

The barrier is short of the lipids that hold water in, so skin feels tight after washing, flakes around the nose and drinks moisturiser by lunch. Rebuild the lipids first; everything else works better after.

  • 3%CeramidesRefill the barrier
  • 5%GlycerinHolds water in
  • PureSqualaneSoftens, no shine

Dullness

Flat, uneven, tired-looking tone.

Old cells linger on the surface and scatter the light, and the sun leaves faint patches behind. A daily antioxidant and a gentle acid bring the evenness back in about four weeks; SPF keeps it.

Breakouts

Clogged pores, shine by lunchtime.

Oil and dead cells settle in the pore and the odd spot follows, usually along the jaw. Keep the pore clear with a low-strength acid, calm the redness that stays behind, and never strip: stripped skin makes more oil.

  • 0.5%Salicylic acidClears the pore
  • 10%NiacinamideCalms, balances oil
  • 0.1%RetinalKeeps pores clear

Fine Lines

Softer lines, smoother texture.

Collagen slows down from our late twenties and the first lines settle where we smile and squint. Vitamin A is the most studied answer; start low, two nights a week, and keep the barrier comfortable around it.

  • 0.1%RetinalFaster vitamin A
  • 1%BakuchiolA gentle alternative
  • 3%CeramidesKeeps it comfortable
