Jargon, decoded.

The six terms you’ll hear in your first call.

LTV
Loan to value: the mortgage as a share of the price. Lower LTV, lower rates.
APRC
The overall yearly cost, fees and the later variable rate included.
DIP
A Decision in Principle: a lender’s early yes, based on a soft search.
SVR
The standard variable rate you move to when a deal ends. Usually the one to avoid.
ERC
An early repayment charge for leaving a deal before it ends.
Porting
Taking your current deal with you to a new home.

Representative example

A mortgage of £200,000 over 25 years, initially on a fixed rate of 3.89% for 5 years and then the lender’s variable rate of 7.24% for the remaining 20 years, would require 60 monthly payments of £1,044.31 followed by 240 monthly payments of £1,393.66. The total amount payable would be £399,746 made up of the loan amount plus interest of £198,747 and a product fee of £999. The overall cost for comparison is 6.1% APRC representative.
