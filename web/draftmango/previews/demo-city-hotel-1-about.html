About the House

A new house, on an old frame.

Marta and Henrique Calado took a shuttered 1960s office block above the Avenida and spent three years turning it into the hotel they kept looking for.

From Marta & Henrique

We wanted the hotel we kept looking for.

Calm, beautifully made and in the middle of things, with a restaurant the city actually comes to and someone at the desk who knows where to eat tonight. We never found quite that, so we built it.

We are still here most days: Marta with the team in the morning, Henrique somewhere between the plant room and the roof. If something in your room is not right, tell either of us. If something in the city is wonderful, tell us too.

Marta & Henrique CaladoFounders, Draftmango House

The Story

Sixty years of concrete, three years of work.

  1. 1964

    The frame is poured

    Ten floors of offices for an insurance company, a block off the Avenida.

  2. 2019

    The last tenant leaves

    Suspended ceilings, grey carpet and a view nobody looked at.

  3. 2021

    The Calados buy it

    Estúdio Vale Morais draws a hotel around the old frame.

  4. 2022

    Back to the columns

    Stripped to the concrete, new fins, 212 windows to the floor.

  5. 2025

    Draftmango House opens

    Eighty-four rooms, Lume and the rooftop pool, in May.
