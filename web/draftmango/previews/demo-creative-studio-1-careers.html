Why Draftmango

Six things fourteen people agreed are worth keeping, whatever the studio grows into.

    One hour a week when the whole studio looks at the work. No decks about the decks.

  • Four-day week in August

    Monday to Thursday all through August. The Fridays are yours.

    Courses, books, a conference or a type workshop abroad. You choose, we pay.
