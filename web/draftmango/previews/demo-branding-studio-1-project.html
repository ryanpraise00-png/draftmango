01

The brief

Fieldnote made good notebooks that looked like everyone else's. Two founders, one range and a wholesale list that had stopped growing. They needed a name for the range, a face for the shelf and rules a small team could keep.

02

The idea

Bring the outdoors in: a leaf pressed into every cover, colours taken from a forest floor, type quiet enough to let the paper lead. One mark, everywhere — from a 16-pixel favicon to the sign over the shop door.
