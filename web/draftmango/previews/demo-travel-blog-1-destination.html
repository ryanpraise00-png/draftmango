Iceland, slowly and on a budget.

Three trips, two winters and one very small rental car. Everything we know about the island, from the ring road to the hot pots nobody signposts.

Why Go

Weather you can’t plan, light you’ll never forget.

Iceland is small enough to drive around in a week and big enough to fill a month. The south coast holds most of the famous sights within a day of Reykjavík; the east fjords and the north hold the quiet.

It is expensive, but not as expensive as people say. Cook breakfast, fill a thermos, swim in the town pools instead of the lagoons, and a couple can travel well on about $310 a day, car included.
