Meridian Tower

Singapore · 20251.3521° N, 103.8198° E

A tower that gives the street back: a colonnade, a public plaza and a garden every tenth floor.

The Brief

Height, without turning its back.

Harbourline Land asked for 104,000 square metres of offices and homes on a plot the size of a city block. We gave a third of the ground floor back to the street.

The Idea

Split the tower into five villages of ten floors. Each village shares a triple-height garden on its top floor, open on two sides to the breeze, so no office or home is more than five floors from a tree.

02

The Street

At the foot, 26 tapering fins carry the tower down to a shaded colonnade. Behind them, a plaza of water steps and young rain trees, open day and night, links the canal path to the station.

Measured a year after opening.

Post-occupancy review, June 2026

  • 4,200 m² Public garden, in the sky and at the street
  • −31% Embodied carbon against a 2020 benchmark tower
  • 70% Of the garden levels cooled by the breeze alone
