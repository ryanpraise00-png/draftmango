The Method

One formula, nothing hidden.

Three steps, each worked with the calculator's starting values.

  1. 01Baseline

    People × Hours a Week × 46 Weeks

    What the task takes from your team today, over a working year.

    12 × 10 × 46 =equals 5,520 Hours a Year Today

  2. 02Share

    × Share Automated

    The part an agent can take. Never all of it: people keep the judgement calls.

    5,520 × 60% =equals 3,312 Hours Returned a Year

  3. 03Value

    × Hourly Cost

    Returned hours at what an hour really costs you, salary plus overhead.

    3,312 × $42 =equals $139,104 Value a Year

Weeks to Payback = pilot cost ÷ value a week. Here: $18,000 ÷ $3,024 = 6 weeks.
