Lab-Grown Diamonds

The same diamond, grown in months

A lab-grown diamond is carbon crystal, identical to a mined one in hardness, fire and composition. Growing it takes weeks rather than ages, which is why a larger, cleaner stone costs less. Simon chooses each one himself, loose, under daylight.

Cut

How the facets return light. We never compromise here.

Colour

Colourless to near-colourless, invisible once set.

Clarity
VVS1 to VS2

No inclusion you can see without a loupe.

Carat

Weight, not size: shape changes how big it looks.
