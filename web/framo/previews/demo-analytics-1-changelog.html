14 Oct 2026Framo Analyst

Framo Analyst now explains every chart

Open any chart and press Explain. The analyst writes two lines on what moved, which segment drove it and what changed at the time, with the events it checked listed underneath.

Explain is on for every Growth and Enterprise workspace today. Free workspaces can use it within their 20 monthly questions.

2 Oct 2026Analytics

Session replay for React Native

Replays now work in React Native apps, on the same timeline as web sessions. Inputs are masked by default, and replays link from every funnel step.

23 Sept 2026Data

Warehouse sync every 15 minutes

Two-way warehouse sync now runs every quarter of an hour on Growth, down from hourly. Enterprise workspaces can query in place with no sync at all.

11 Sept 2026Analytics

Conversion windows per funnel step

Each step can now have its own window, so “trial started” to “invited team” can be a day while “invited team” to “paid” gets a fortnight.

  • Median time between steps, shown under each bar
  • Exclusion steps for people who saw a different flow
  • Saved windows travel with the funnel when shared

28 Aug 2026Fixes

Small fixes, August

  • Cohort tables keep their sort order after a refresh
  • Dashboard emails respect the workspace time zone
  • CSV exports keep leading zeros in IDs
