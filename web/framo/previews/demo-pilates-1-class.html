Full Body Reformer.

Legs, core, arms and back in one steady flow — our signature class, twelve times a week in Studio One. Fine for your first class, still hard in your hundredth.

Book a Reformer

At a Glance

Everything, nothing rushed.

Six blocks that work the whole body in order — feet first, release last. Your instructor calls the spring change before every block and gives a lighter or heavier option, so a first-timer and a ten-year member can share a row.

The Fifty Minutes

Six blocks, feet first.

The order stays the same every class; the exercises inside each block change with the month's focus.

  1. 0–8Footwork & BreathHeavy springs
  2. 8–16Legs in StrapsMedium
  3. 16–24Long Stretch & PlanksLight to medium
  4. 24–32Kneeling ArmsLight
  5. 32–42AbdominalsLight
  6. 42–50Mermaid & ReleaseMedium

November Focus Glutes & Obliques

This month, the sides.

Each month one muscle group gets a little more of every class. In November it's the muscles that keep your pelvis level when you walk and turn. December moves to the upper back and shoulders.

  • Side-lying leg series In the straps, top leg slow2 × 10Light spring
  • Skating Standing on the carriage, one leg presses3 × 8Medium spring
  • Kneeling side bend One hand on the footbar, reach long2 × 6Light spring
