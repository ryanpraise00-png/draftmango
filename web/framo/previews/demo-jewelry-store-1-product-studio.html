Ring size guide

  1. Wrap a strip of paper

    Around the base of the finger you will wear the ring on, snug but able to slide over the knuckle.

  2. Mark and measure

    Mark where the paper meets, lay it flat and measure in millimeters. Find that circumference in the chart.

  3. Between two sizes?

    Take the larger one for wide bands over 4 mm. Every ring is resized free once in its first year.

Rather try it on? We mail a free set of ring sizers with every video appointment.
