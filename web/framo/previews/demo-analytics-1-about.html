Our Story

Built by people tired of waiting.

Our founders ran product at two London start-ups and spent more time queuing for numbers than using them.

So they built the tool they wanted: one event model, answers in seconds, and no fee for asking another colleague in. The analyst came later, once the data underneath was something people trusted.
