The words we use, defined.

Six numbers come up on every call. This is exactly what we mean by each one.

ROASReturn on Ad Spend

Revenue a platform credits to its own ads, divided by what they cost.

Revenue ÷ ad spend
MERMarketing Efficiency Ratio

All revenue divided by all marketing spend. The number we steer by.

Total revenue ÷ total spend
CACCustomer Acquisition Cost

What it costs to win one new customer, every channel included.

Spend ÷ new customers
LTVLifetime Value

The gross margin a customer brings over their whole time with you.

Margin × orders kept
AEOAnswer Engine Optimisation

Getting your brand named and cited in AI answers, not only ranked.

Citations ÷ prompts tracked
iROASIncremental ROAS

Revenue that would not exist without the ads, proven with holdout tests.

Lift ÷ ad spend
