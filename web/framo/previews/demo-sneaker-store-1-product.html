Size guide

Measure your longer foot at the end of the day, heel against a wall, and match the length below. Between two sizes, take the larger.

  1. Stand on a sheet of paper, heel to the wall.
  2. Mark the tip of your longest toe.
  3. Measure heel to mark in centimetres.
