Five services, one drawing set

Take one service or all five. Every project is led by a partner, and most begin with a site visit and a conversation about how you want to live or work.

01 Houses, civic buildings, additions

Architecture

New buildings from the ground up, drawn around the site, the light and the way you will live or work in them. A partner leads from the first visit to handover.

  • Site and zoning study
  • Concept models and drawings
  • Permit and construction set
  • Construction administration

Typical LengthAbout six months from brief to permit for a house

02 Rooms, joinery, materials

Interiors

Interiors drawn with the building, not after it: built-in joinery, lighting and a short list of honest materials. We also take on interiors in buildings we did not design.

  • Material and finish palette
  • Joinery and lighting drawings
  • Furniture and fixture schedule
  • Installation on site

Typical LengthFour to nine months, concept to installation

03 Barns, mills, warehouses

Adaptive Reuse

Old structures kept and given a new use. We survey what is there, keep what is worth keeping and add only what is missing, with historic tax credits where they apply.

  • Measured survey of the building
  • Structure and envelope review
  • Historic tax credit support
  • Phased construction plan

Typical LengthStarts with a two to three week measured survey

04 Campuses, sites, phasing

Masterplanning

A plan for a whole site over ten or twenty years: where buildings go, how people move between them and what gets built first. For schools, farms and small campuses.

  • Site analysis and capacity
  • Phasing plan
  • Design guidelines
  • Community workshops

Typical LengthThree to six months for a plan

05 Before you buy or build

Feasibility Studies

Three to four weeks to learn what a site or a building can hold, what the code allows and roughly what it will cost. Many of our projects start here.

  • Zoning and code summary
  • Two massing options
  • Order-of-magnitude cost
  • A written recommendation

Typical LengthThree to four weeks

06 / Process

Five phases, one drawing set

A typical house runs about six months from brief to permit. Construction follows at the builder's pace.

  1. 01 · Weeks 1–3

    Brief & Site

    • Site visit and survey
    • Zoning and code review
    • Written brief and budget
  2. 02 · Weeks 4–9

    Concept

    • Massing models
    • Two or three options
    • First cost check
  3. 03 · Weeks 10–16

    Developed Design

    • Plans, sections, elevations
    • Materials and samples
    • Engineers coordinated
  4. 04 · Weeks 17–26

    Permits & Documents

    • Permit set and filing
    • Construction drawings
    • Bid package for builders
  5. 05 · To Handover

    Construction Administration

    • Weekly site visits
    • Shop drawing review
    • Punch list and handover
