Built by people who've run a business.

We spent years doing our own books at the kitchen table. Framo Business is the account we wished we'd had.

In 2019 our founders were helping a friend's café through a cash-flow scare. The bank app showed a balance, and nothing else.

So we built an account that shows where money is going, what tax is owed and who still has to pay. Today 640,000 businesses run on it.
