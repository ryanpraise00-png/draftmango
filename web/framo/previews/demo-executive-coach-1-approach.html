How coaching works here.

Five steps, no surprises: from a free first conversation to a review measured against the goals you set. What you say stays with your coach.

What we believe

We don’t give leaders answers. We give them better questions, and the time to answer them.

Amelia Hart and Tom AshworthCo-founders, Framo Leadership

Five steps, in depth.

You always know where you are and what comes next. So does your sponsor, within limits you agree.

  1. Meet two, choose one.

    You meet two coaches we put forward for your role and goals, one after the other. You choose; nobody follows up to sell.

    When
    Within a week of your call
    Where
    London, Edinburgh or online

  2. Hear what others see.

    Your coach interviews eight to ten people you work with. You read the write-up first, then set two or three goals with your sponsor.

    Interviews
    Eight to ten, 30 minutes each
    Goals
    Two or three, agreed in writing

  3. One thing, by Friday.

    Seventy-five minutes every two weeks, at Hanover Square, Charlotte Square, online or walking. Each session ends with one thing you will do differently.

    Sessions
    75 minutes, fortnightly
    Between
    Your coach on message

  4. Progress, not content.

    At the start, the midpoint and the end, you, your coach and your sponsor meet for thirty minutes on progress against the goals. Never on what was said.

    Length
    30 minutes, three times
    Shared
    A one-page progress note

  5. Measured, honestly.

    The 360° is re-run with the same people. You see the difference in their words and in the scores, and decide with your sponsor what comes next.

    Re-run
    The same people, the same questions
    Then
    End, extend or hand over

What we measure.

Four things, for every leader, every year. The numbers are audited and published.

Goal progress
Rated by you and two stakeholders at each check-in.
The 360°, re-run
The same people and questions, at the start and the end.
Your sponsor’s view
A short written assessment at the final review.
Eighteen months on
Promotion and retention, tracked since 2019.

Confidential, by default.

  • ICF Code of Ethics
  • Every coach in supervision
  • EMCC Quality Award

What Stays Private

  • What you say in a session
  • Your coach’s notes
  • The 360° write-up, unless you share it

What Your Sponsor Sees

  • Attendance
  • Progress against the goals
  • The review summary you approve

The One Exception

A serious risk of harm to you or to someone else, as the ICF Code sets out. We would tell you first.
