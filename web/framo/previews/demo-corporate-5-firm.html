The Firm · Since 2004

A boutique, on purpose.

Thirty-eight partners in New York, Chicago and London. We take few mandates, we lead every one ourselves, and we have nothing to sell you at the end.

Our Story

Started by one partner, kept small by choice.

Henry Caldwell opened the firm in New York in 2004 with one client and a rule he still keeps: the partner who wins the mandate leads it. Victoria Ashford joined two years later. London followed in 2011, Chicago in 2016.

We have grown by staying narrow: strategy and leadership questions for chief executives and boards, nothing else.
