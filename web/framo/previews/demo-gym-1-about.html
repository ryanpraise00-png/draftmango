Our Story

Four platforms in a borrowed bay.

Framo started in 2017 with one coach, four platforms and a 6 am class in a shared bay off East 5th. People came for the barbell and stayed because someone wrote down what they lifted.

Two years later we moved into the warehouse on East 6th St: 14,000 square feet of brick, steel and rubber floor. We built it out one platform at a time, then added the turf lane and the recovery zone.

We are still independent. No franchise, no investors, and the coaches own a share of the club they coach in.
