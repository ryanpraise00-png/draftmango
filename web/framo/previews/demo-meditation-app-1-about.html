We make rest a little easier.

Framo Rest began with two bad sleepers and a cheap microphone. Today it helps 2.8 million people switch off at night.

It started with one story.

In 2019 Clara Wynn was sleeping four hours a night. Her friend Daniel Moss, a sound engineer, recorded her a slow story about a ferry crossing a bay. She never heard the end of it.

They shared it with twenty friends, then two thousand strangers. Framo Rest grew from there: a small team in Edinburgh writing, recording and testing every session with clinical psychologists before it reaches you.
