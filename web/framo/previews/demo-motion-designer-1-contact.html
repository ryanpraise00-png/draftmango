What Happens Next

From your brief to a fixed quote in about a week.

  1. A reply in two days

    A first idea, the questions that matter and an honest view on your timeline. If I am not the right person, I will say so and suggest someone who is.

  2. A thirty-minute call

    We go through the product, where the film runs and how we will know it worked. NDA signed before the call if you need one.

  3. A fixed quote

    A one-page brief, a schedule by week and a price that does not move. Half to start, half on delivery.
