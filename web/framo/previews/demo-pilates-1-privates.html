Who It's For

When a class of twelve isn't the right start.

Most people take a handful of privates, then join the group classes with a plan their instructor has already shared with the team.

  • Coming Back From Injury

    Backs, knees, shoulders — we work alongside your physical therapist's notes.

  • Pregnancy & After

    With Olivia, at any stage, including a postnatal core check from six weeks.

  • A Head Start

    Learn the machine one to one, then walk into Full Body knowing every spring.

  • A Specific Goal

    Runners, climbers, golfers: the hips and the trunk your sport leans on.

How It Goes

Listen, then move.

  1. A Ten-Minute CallYour instructor calls before the first session: history, goals, anything a doctor has said.
  2. The First SessionFifteen minutes of how you move — standing, walking, rolling down — then forty on the reformer or tower.
  3. A Plan You KeepA short written plan by email, with the springs and the exercises to skip in group classes.
