Aftercare

The first 48 hours.

The set or lift takes two days to cure. Look after it now and it lasts weeks longer.

  1. 01

    Keep Them Dry

    No water, steam or sauna for 48 hours. Wash your face around the eyes.

  2. 02

    Skip the Mascara

    For two days after a lift; never on a set. It is the fastest way to lose them.

  3. 03

    Brush Daily

    A clean spoolie each morning keeps the line neat. We give you one.

  4. 04

    Oil-Free Only

    Oil dissolves the bond. Use an oil-free cleanser and remover around the eyes.
