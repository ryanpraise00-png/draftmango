The Atelier

Made by hand, a few steps from our counter

Twelve goldsmiths work behind the boutique on King Street. They cast, set and finish every Framo Atelier piece, and they look after it for as long as you wear it.

Our Story

100%

12

Pieces cared for each year
3,800
  1. 01

    Recycled Gold

    Every piece starts as recycled gold, refined and alloyed to 14k or 18k here.

  2. 02

    Set by Hand

    Stones are seated under the loupe, claw by claw, then checked twice.

  3. 03

    Finished Slowly

    Filed, sanded and polished in four passes until the gold reads soft.

  4. 04

    Engraved and Hallmarked

    Stamped with our mark and the gold’s purity, and engraved free if you like.
