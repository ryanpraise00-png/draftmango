The Hearth Almond wood, lit at five

The people at the pass.

A kitchen of fourteen and a floor of twelve, led by three who have cooked and poured together for years.

Chef and Owner

Julian Ward

Grew up in Sonoma, cooked in London, Copenhagen and Napa, and opened the Dining Room in 2016. He still drives to Pell Ridge most Mondays to walk the rows before writing the week's menu.

“A good leek, cooked in the coals until it gives, needs nothing from me but salt and time.”

Pastry and Cellar

The last course and every glass.

Pastry chef, since 2019

Clara Bennett

Bakes the rye at four in the afternoon and the madeleines to order. Her quince comes from Ashland Orchard and the bay for the ice cream from a roof a mile away.

Wine director, since 2021

Owen Price

Keeps four hundred bottles from small growers in the cellar and writes the non-alcoholic pairing with the kitchen. Ask him for something you have never tried.
