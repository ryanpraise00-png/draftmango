Before you choose

Long shapes spread their weight: an oval, a pear or a marquise of 1.5 carats faces up larger than a round of the same weight. A round returns the most light; an emerald cut shows long, quiet flashes instead of sparkle.

Yes. Book an appointment and Simon brings three stones of your shape to the table, loose, with their reports. By video, we film each one under daylight and send you the clips.

Most clients choose a 6 and resize after the proposal: resizing is free for life, up to two sizes, and takes about a week. Our ring sizer page has a few discreet ways to find out.

It is carbon crystal, the same as a mined diamond in hardness, fire and composition. It is grown in months rather than mined, which is why a larger, cleaner stone costs less.
