About the Firm · Since 2009

Too big to wing it. Too small for a global bench.

We started Framo Advisory for the companies in between: $20M to $500M, owner-led or private-equity backed, with a few decisions that matter more than the rest.

Our Story

One client, one rule. Seventeen years on.

Daniel Reeves left a Midwest food group in 2009 to help one of its suppliers, Harlow Foods, fix a plant that kept missing its budget. The rule he set then still holds: the partner who scopes the work leads it, and the fee is fixed before anyone starts.

Denver followed in 2015 and Toronto in 2019. Today 64 consultants work from three offices, most of them people who have run plants, finance teams and integrations themselves.
