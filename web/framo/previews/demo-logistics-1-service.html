Why Ship Ocean With Us

Contract space, not a waitlist.

Every year we sign allocations with nine carriers on the lanes our shippers use most. When a peak hits and spot space disappears, your box is already on the vessel plan.

Your coordinator books, files the export documents, watches the vessel and hands over to our broker at destination, who files the entry while the ship is still at sea.

  • Allocations with nine carriers, so rollovers are the exception
  • Weekly LCL consolidations from 19 origin ports in Asia, Europe and Latin America
  • Free time negotiated up front: 14 days at most US ports, no surprise demurrage
  • Live vessel position and exceptions flagged within the hour
