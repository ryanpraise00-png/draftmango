Our Story

Made slowly, on Greene Street

Two bench jewelers, one long table under an arched window, and a simple idea: fine jewelry you never take off, made close enough to visit.

Since 2016

We wanted jewelry you forget you're wearing.

Nora trained as a setter in Copenhagen; Hanna spent eight years at a bench on 47th Street. They met fixing other people's pieces and kept noticing the same thing: the rings people loved most were the plain, well-made ones they wore every day.

In 2016 they rented a narrow room on Greene Street and made thirty pieces. Today six jewelers work at the same long table, still casting, setting and polishing every piece by hand — and you are welcome to come and watch.

What We Use

Three materials, chosen with care

  • Recycled gold 14k and 18k from two US refiners, alloyed without new mining. Our vermeil is a thick 2.5 micron layer over recycled silver.
  • Lab-grown diamonds The same carbon crystal as a mined stone, graded by an independent lab over 0.3 ct, and chosen by eye in daylight before we set it.
  • Freshwater pearls Cultured in clean freshwater farms, sorted by hand for lustre and a soft, even round, never dyed.

Ten Years at the Bench

From thirty pieces to a long table

We have grown slowly on purpose: one bench at a time, on the same street since the first day.

  1. 2016

    A narrow room on Greene Street

    Two benches, one window and the first thirty pieces, sold to neighbours who walked in to watch.

  2. 2018

    Recycled gold, only

    Every ounce of 14k and 18k now comes from two US refiners, alloyed without new mining.

  3. 2019

    The first bridal rings

    Lab-grown solitaires, each stone chosen in daylight with the couple and made to order in four weeks.

  4. 2021

    Number 142 and the arched window

    The atelier and the showroom move into one bright room, the long table set under the window.

  5. 2024

    A jeweler, anywhere in the US

    Video appointments begin, with a free try-on kit of sizers and samples sent ahead of the call.

The Bench

Six jewelers, one long table

Every piece passes through four pairs of hands before it leaves Greene Street. Ask for any of them when you visit.

  • Theo Lauritsen

    Caster

    Pours every band and signet from recycled gold, then checks each one against the light.

  • Ida Lund

    Stone Setter

    Sets the claws and the pavé under the microscope, one diamond at a time.

  • Liv Jakobsen

    Hand Engraver

    Cuts initials, dates and drawn crests with a graver, never a laser.

  • Elias Krogh

    Polisher & Finisher

    Gives every piece its last mirror shine, and the free cleanings that follow for life.

Care & Policies

Looked after, for as long as you wear it

The small print, written plainly. Questions? Call the atelier on (212) 555-0148.

Solid gold and diamonds can be worn every day, in the shower too. Clean them with warm water, a drop of mild soap and a soft brush. Take vermeil and pearls off for swimming, workouts and perfume, and keep them in their pouch.
Every ring includes one free resize in its first year, insured shipping both ways. After that, resizing starts at $45. Eternity bands can't be resized, so we remake them at cost in the new size.
We repair any fault in how a piece was made, for as long as you own it: loose stones, worn claws, broken clasps. Cleaning, polishing and re-plating are free too, in the atelier or by insured mail.
Free insured shipping across the US, signed for at the door, in 2–4 business days; express is $25. Return anything not engraved within 30 days for a full refund, with a prepaid label in every box.
