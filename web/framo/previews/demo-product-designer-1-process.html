How I Work

Small steps, tested weekly, measured after launch.

The same four steps for a two-week sprint or a six-month build. You always know what happens this week and what lands in your files on Friday.

Week 1

Find the real drop-off

Analytics, support tickets and five interviews with people who quit. We agree on one metric and the number that would count as a win.

You get a one-page brief and the success metric.

Weeks 2–3

Map the shortest path

Flows on a wall before pixels on a screen. Every step has to earn its place; most products lose a third of theirs here.

You get the flow map and the screens it needs.

Weeks 3–8

Test it every Thursday

A clickable prototype in front of six people each week. Your team watches live, and Friday's changes come straight from Thursday's sessions.

You get the prototype and a two-minute clip per session.

Until release

Ship it with the engineers

Specs inside the components, reviews of every build and a launch checklist. Three months later we read the metric together.

You get production files and the after-launch read-out.

A Design Sprint

Ten working days, one tested answer.

Your team gives two half-days: the kickoff and the Thursday sessions. The rest is mine.

Week 1 · Understand

  1. MonKickoffGoals, the metric and the team's best guesses
  2. TueInterviewsFive people who quit, on video
  3. WedThe mapToday's flow on one wall, the leaks marked
  4. ThuSketchesThree directions, each a different bet
  5. FriDecisionWe pick one together, in an hour

Week 2 · Prove

  1. MonPrototypeThe chosen flow, clickable in Figma
  2. TuePolishReal copy, real data, real edge cases
  3. WedPilotA dry run with someone on your team
  4. ThuSix sessionsReal users, your team watching live
  5. FriRead-outFindings, the build plan, the files
