The Challenge

Weeks 0–2 · Audit

Ferncastle was spending more every month just to stand still.

Thirty-one ad sets bid against each other for the same buyers. The creative hadn’t changed since launch, and the email list only heard from the brand during sales. New customers were expensive, and few came back.

The Approach

Months 1–3 · Rebuild

Fewer campaigns, more ideas, and a reason to come back.

  1. Account Rebuild Thirty-one ad sets became three offers, each with its own budget and margin floor. Weeks 1–3
  2. Weekly Creative Sprints Six new concepts every Monday, judged on cost per order rather than clicks. From Week 4
  3. Second-Order Flows Welcome, care-guide and replenishment emails timed to how long sheets last. Month 3
03

The Results

Months 4–6 · Scale

By June, every channel was paying its own way.

With margin floors in place, the budget grew 40% and returns kept climbing. Flows now bring in almost a quarter of revenue, and the brand plans its launches around the sprint calendar.
