A goal in, the work done. Every step on the record.

An agent is a workflow step that plans its own route — inside the limits you set.

  1. Goal

    “Resolve this ticket” or “Research this lead.”

  2. Plan

    It picks the steps and the tools it needs.

  3. Act

    It reads, looks up and drafts in your apps.

  4. Ask

    It stops for a person where you said it must.

  5. Report

    It finishes the job and logs every call it made.
