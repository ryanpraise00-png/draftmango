Our Story

A planning firm, not a sales floor.

Maya Abbott spent her first six years in finance at a firm that paid planners by the product. In 2011 she rented one room in South End, Charlotte, and set a different rule: the only money we take comes from the families we plan for.

That rule shaped everything after it. Flat fees instead of commissions, plans written in plain English, and a planner who stays with you for years instead of handing you on once the paperwork is done.

Milestones

Fifteen years, one rule.

We grew by referral, one family at a time. Most new clients still come from someone we already plan for.

  1. 2011One room in South End

    Maya Abbott opens Framo Planning as a fee-only practice with eleven founding clients.

  2. 2014Flat fees for everyone

    Hourly billing goes; three flat annual plans take its place. Daniel Beckett joins as the second CFP® professional.

  3. 2017Investment management, optional

    Clients ask us to run their portfolios too. We say yes, as an add-on that is never required.

  4. 2020Every meeting by video

    The whole firm moves online in two weeks; by the end of the year we plan for families in 20 states.

  5. 2023Equity compensation practice

    Owen Devlin starts our RSU and stock-option desk in Austin, and Theo Faulkner, EA, leads tax planning.

  6. Today640 households, 34 states

    Twelve CFP® professionals and an Enrolled Agent, still under one rule.
