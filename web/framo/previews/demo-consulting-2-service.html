Is This Your Lever?

Five signs pricing is costing you.

If three of these sound familiar, pricing will likely pay back faster than any other lever.

  • Reps discount 15% or more on most deals, and nobody can say why.
  • Your list price hasn't changed in two years, while costs have.
  • One package serves every customer, from the smallest to the largest.
  • Customers ask for a quote before they will talk to sales.
  • Gross margin falls a little every year as the deals get bigger.
