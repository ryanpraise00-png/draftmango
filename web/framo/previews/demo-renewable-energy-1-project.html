Prairie Ridge Wind, Story County, Iowa.

420 MW over 28,000 acres of corn and soybeans, leased from 64 farm families. Operating since December 2021.

Operating

Our largest Iowa wind farm.

Six years from the first wind mast to first power. Today Prairie Ridge makes about as much electricity in a year as 140,000 Iowa homes use, and sells all of it to a Midwestern electric cooperative.

How It Came Together

From one mast to 120 towers.

  1. 2015Two wind masts go upOn the Hendricks and Lund farms, logging through two winters.
  2. 2016The first leasesKitchen-table meetings across two counties; 64 families sign by the end of 2017.
  3. 2019County permitAfter four public meetings and a layout redrawn around two farmsteads.
  4. 2020Construction310 workers at the peak, most from within an hour's drive.
  5. 2021First powerCommercial operation in December; the Story City operations building opens with 18 technicians.
