Our Story

Elena Varga opened one studio on a second floor in 2012, with forty students and a rule she still keeps: every dancer performs, every year.

  1. 2012

    One studio

    Forty students, ballet and jazz, on Fulton Market.

  2. 2016

    The crew

    Andre Collins starts hip hop; his teens dance every June since.

  3. 2017

    Five studios

    The school takes the whole building.

  4. 2019

    Pre-Professional

    The track opens, by audition, ten hours a week.

  5. 2026

    620 dancers

    24 teachers and a summer intensive since 2021.

What We Believe

Technique first, with joy.

  • Every dancer performs

    From Creative Movement to the company, everyone has a June.

  • Honest feedback

    Reports in January and June, written by the teacher.

  • Small classes

    Up to 10 for the youngest, 12 for children.

  • Safe in every way

    Sprung floors; every teacher background-checked and CPR-trained.
