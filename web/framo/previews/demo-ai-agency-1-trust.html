Security Questions

01Data

Your data stays in your perimeter. None of it trains a public model.

An agent reads from your systems with the least access it needs, works in a tenant that is yours alone and writes its results back to your cloud.

02Human Approval

High-impact actions wait for a person. Someone you name decides.

The agent prepares the work. You choose which actions need a signature and who gives it, and until then nothing is sent, paid or deleted.

03Models

The best model for each task. No lock-in to any of them.

We pick a model per step on your own test cases, for accuracy, speed and cost. All of them sit behind one interface, so one can be replaced without rebuilding the agent.

04Evaluation

Tested before launch, and again before every change.

Each agent has test sets built from your past cases, the awkward ones included. A build ships only when every set clears the threshold we agreed with you.

05Audit Trail

Every action is on the record. Who, what, when and on which data.

The log is written as the agent works, not rebuilt afterwards. Search it, export it and keep it for as long as your policy says.

06Compliance

Built to the frameworks your auditors already use.

Controls are designed in from the first workshop, so the evidence exists on the day your auditors ask for it.
