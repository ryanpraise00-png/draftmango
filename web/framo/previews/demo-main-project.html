Castellan: a members' club site that books itself

Twelve workspace studios, one question every morning: is my favourite desk free? We rebuilt the site around the answer.

The brief
Client
Castellan Studios
Industry

Services
UX research, web design, development
Platform
Web & installable app
Timeline
14 weeks
Launched

Visit castellan.work

Castellan runs twelve workspace studios for about four thousand members. The old site was a brochure with a booking form bolted on, and members phoned the front desk to ask what the website could not tell them. We built a site that opens on live availability, books a desk in two taps and greets you at the door.

The challenge

Bookings were slow on a phone, the club's warmth was nowhere on screen, and the front desk spent mornings answering the same three questions.

Our approach

Research first, then one rule for every screen: availability before anything else. Everything else became one tap away.

The solution

From a brochure to the club's front door

Before

Pretty, and in the way

  • A full-screen gallery stood between members and the booking form.
  • Seven steps and a password reset to reserve a single desk.
  • Availability lived in an email that arrived the next day.
  • Five-second loads on a phone, which is where most visits start.
After

Calm, quick and personal

  • The homepage opens on live desk availability, not a banner.
  • Two taps, wallet payment and a calendar invite.
  • Door code, Wi-Fi and the barista's name on the day.
  • Static pages from the edge, loading in under a second.
How we got there

Four stages, fourteen weeks

01 — Discover

Forty members, one desk diary each

We spent a working day with members and asked them to keep a desk diary for a week. Nobody mentioned the gallery. Everyone mentioned the morning scramble for a seat by a window.

InterviewsDesk diariesAnalytics audit
02 — Map

From "I need a desk" to "see you tomorrow"

Every step of a member's day went on one wall. The sitemap fell out of it: availability first, booking second, everything else one tap away.

Journey mapSitemapContent model
03 — Design

Oat, charcoal and one blue accent

A quiet interface that feels like the studios do. Eighty-four components, one motion spec and a type scale tuned for a phone held in one hand on a tram.

Design system84 componentsPrototype
04 — Build & Launch

Live availability, zero waiting

Static pages served from the edge, a real-time layer that updates desks as they are booked, and a CMS the club's team edits without us.
