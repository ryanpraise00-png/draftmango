What we look for.

We add a business rarely — five since 1952. When we do, it meets all four tests.

  1. 01

    Ownable for decades

    A market that will still need it in thirty years.

  2. 02

    A leader in its niche

    First or second where it competes, on quality rather than price.

  3. 03

    A part in the transition

    Lower-carbon already, or a credible plan to get there.

  4. 04

    A team that stays

    Founders and managers who want to keep building it with us.
