About Framo · Since 2007

Started by examiners. Run like an exam.

Three former Federal Reserve examiners opened Framo Governance on K Street in 2007. Today 280 specialists help regulated companies prove what they do.

Our Story

We used to read the binders. Now we help write them.

Caroline Prescott spent twelve years examining banks for the Federal Reserve. Too often she found good companies with weak evidence: controls that worked, with nothing to show it. In 2007 she and two colleagues set out to fix that from the other side of the table.

We still work the way an examiner thinks. Every finding has an owner, every fix is tested, and every test leaves a trail someone else can follow. That is why 94% of our clients have no repeat findings the year after remediation.

How We Work

Four habits we never skip.

  • Evidence over opinion

    If it is not tested and filed, it did not happen. We hold our own work to that standard first.

  • A partner in the room

    Every engagement is led by a partner who attends your meetings, not a slide deck sent from afar.

  • Plain English

    Boards, examiners and engineers read the same report. We write it so all three understand it.

  • Fixed, honest fees

    A fixed fee per phase, agreed before work starts. No hourly meter running in the background.
