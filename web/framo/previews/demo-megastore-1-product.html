From the Appliance Desk

A big drum that stays quiet

Our Riverside install crew fits more of these than any other washer, and the reason is simple: 4.5 cubic feet takes a king-size comforter in one go, the direct-drive motor runs at 48 dB, and the steam cycle lifts the smell out of gym kit without a hot wash.

Twelve cycles cover everything from a 15-minute quick wash to a sanitize run at 158°F. Load sensing tops up only the water each load needs, and the smoked glass door opens a full 180° so the drum is easy to unload.

  • 48 dB direct driveQuiet enough for an open-plan kitchen.
  • Steam RefreshFreshens a dry load in 20 minutes.
  • Load sensingUp to 30% less water on light loads.
  • 1,400 rpm spinClothes come out drier, the dryer runs shorter.
