01 · Live Tracking

Every mile, mapped as you run it.

GPS that holds a clean line under trees and between tall buildings, with pace, splits and heart rate on one screen.

  • Auto-pause at crossings and lights
  • Offline maps for trails with no signal
  • Splits per kilometer or mile, your choice

02 · Adaptive Plans

A plan that bends around your week.

Tell it the race and the days you can train. After every session it reads how it went and reshapes what comes next.

  • Plans from 5K to ultra, plus strength and mobility
  • Missed a day? Key sessions move, the load eases
  • Taper and race week built in

03 · Audio Coaching

A real coach, right in your ear.

Cues recorded by our coaches play when your pace drifts, your heart rate climbs or the hill begins. Not on a timer.

  • Six coaches for running, strength, cycling and mobility
  • Plays over your music or podcast, then fades back
  • Cues in English, Spanish, German and French

Find your crew, then show up.

  • Club challenges with a shared weekly goal

05 · Safety Beacon

Someone always knows where you are.

Share your live location with the people you choose for the length of a run, and let them know if you stop moving.

  • Contacts don't need the app, just a link
  • A no-movement alert after the time you set
  • Starts by itself on runs after dark, if you want

And all the small things.

The details you only notice when another app gets them wrong.

  • Heart Rate Zones

    Five zones set from your own runs, not a formula for your age.

  • Route Builder

    Draw a loop of the distance you want, and it snaps to paths runners actually use.

  • Watch Sync

    Pair once with your watch or chest strap. Every run lands in the app within seconds.

    Sleep, load and resting heart rate in one number each morning.
