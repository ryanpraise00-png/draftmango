Help

Shipping, returns and sizing

The questions we answer most. If yours is not here, ask us above.

Express shipping is free on every US order and takes one to two business days. Orders placed by 2 pm ET leave the same day; a signature is asked for orders over $1,000. Canada and the UK ship for $25 flat in three to five business days, with duties shown at checkout.
You have 30 days from delivery to return a piece, unworn with its tags. Start the return in your account: the label and the pickup are free. Refunds land within five business days of the piece arriving; exchanges ship the day your return is scanned. Pieces marked Final Sale cannot be returned.
Every piece carries the buying team’s fit note (“runs large, take a size down”) and a Size Guide with US, UK, IT and FR conversions and the garment’s measurements. Between sizes? A stylist can send both ahead of a video call, or try them with you in store.
Nothing. Sessions are 60 minutes in store, 45 on video with a box of pieces sent ahead, or at home within twenty miles of either store, with no minimum spend. Book a session
Every order leaves in our ivory box with a handwritten card, free. Gift cards run from $100 to $2,500, sent by email or posted in the box, and never expire.
