What to have ready

  • Your policy number (it's on your ID card)
  • The date, the time and the address
  • Photos of the damage, before you clean up
  • A police report number, after a theft or a crash
  • Names and numbers of anyone involved
  • Estimates from contractors, if you have them

If anyone is hurt, call 911 first. Then make the place safe: water off, power off, car off the road.
