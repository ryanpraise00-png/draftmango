Framo is now in public beta.

Eighteen months, 12,400 waiting teams and one very long spreadsheet later, anyone can sign up. Here is what is in it, what is not yet, and how the beta works.

This morning we took the waitlist down. If you have been holding a place in line since last spring: thank you, and sorry it took this long. Your invite is in your inbox. If you only heard of us today, you can sign up in about a minute.

Framo started as an argument between the two of us. Lea had six years of customer requests in a spreadsheet only she could read. Sam had six years of features nobody could prove anyone wanted. We both thought the other was the problem. We were both right.

What is in the beta

Everything we have built so far, on every plan, for free. That means the whole loop:

  • Collect requests from chat, email, your support desk and a public portal, all in one inbox.
  • Prioritize them by votes, revenue and reach, and merge the duplicates.
  • Plan on a board that publishes your roadmap as you drag things around.
  • Announce what shipped, with an email to every person who asked.

The best feedback tool is the one that makes you write back.

What is not in it yet

A mobile app, duplicate detection that understands new wording, and a roadmap you can export as a PDF for your board meeting. All three are on our own public roadmap. You can vote on them, and you will get an email when they ship. That is rather the point.

How the beta works

It is free until launch, with no card. We will give you 30 days' notice before prices start, beta teams keep 30% off their first year, and anyone who does nothing simply stays on the Free plan with all their data.

One thing we ask in return: tell us what is missing. Post it on our board, vote on what is already there, and reply to the release emails. We read every one, usually before lunch.

— Lea & Sam, Austin
