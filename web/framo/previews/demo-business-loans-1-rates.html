What Sets Your Rate

Four things decide the number. None of them is a guess.

  • Time in business

    Twelve months gets you in. Three years or more usually lowers the rate.

    Helps: a longer track record

  • Cash flow

    We look at deposits and balances, not just annual revenue.

    Helps: steady deposits, few overdrafts

  • Credit history

    A personal score of 625 qualifies. Higher scores reach the lowest rates.

    Helps: on-time payments, low utilization

  • Product and collateral

    Secured products like equipment financing price lower than unsecured ones.

    Helps: matching the product to the need
