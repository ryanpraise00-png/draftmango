The Method

Coached. Programmed. Measured.

Three things every class at Framo has, whether it is your first week or your fifth year.

  1. 01

    Coached

    A coach on the floor for every class, cueing, loading and scaling each lift to you. Groups cap at 12.

  2. 02

    Programmed

    Six-week blocks that build on each other, so Monday's squat connects to next month's.

  3. 03

    Measured

    Every lift logged, and a body-composition scan each month on All-In. Progress is a number, not a feeling.
