Anatomy of a homepage

Every part of the page has one job

This is the homepage of our IT Services template, designed with the same process as our client work. Tap a number to see what each part is there to do.

  1. 1 NavigationSeven items at most, named the way your customers talk, with the main action always in reach.
  2. 2 HeadlineWhat you do and who it is for, in under ten words. The details can wait for the next line.
  3. 3 Primary ActionOne clear next step, repeated down the page. A quieter second button for people not ready yet.
  4. 4 ProofRatings and client names right below the fold, where visitors decide whether to trust you.
  5. 5 ServicesA photo, a name and one line each, so people find their own problem in seconds.

Process

The design phase, week by week

Discover and Design are the first two of our four steps. Build and Launch follow, with the same team.

  1. Week 1

    Discover

    Kick-off workshop, analytics review, competitor teardown, and the sitemap signed off.

  2. Weeks 2–3

    Wireframes

    Key pages in grey with real copy. Two review sessions, comments in one shared file.

  3. Weeks 3–4

    Interface Design

    Visual direction on the homepage first, then every template and the design system.

  4. End of week 4

    Prototype & Test

    A clickable prototype tested with real users, final tweaks, then handoff to Build.
