Still Water — light like cold water.

01The Brief

Sell the feeling of cold water before the bottle existed.

Verdane's new serum had a launch date in June and a glass supplier running late: the retail stills were due before the first bottle was. We had the CAD file, one frosted sample and a line from the formula notes: like cold water on skin.

So the light became the subject. Water caustics move over travertine and frosted glass, and the sage sphere stands in for the green clay in the formula. I built two sets, a smooth sage ground and a bed of moss, rendered twelve stills at 6K and one ten-second loop in which only the water moves.

  • ChallengeTwelve retail stills of a bottle nobody had photographed yet.
  • AnswerWater light as the subject, the bottle rendered from CAD.
  • ResultNot one retailer asked for a photo; the stills are still on shelves.
