Shipping & Returns

The small print, kept small

Free shipping over $60, free gift wrap on every order and 60 days to swap or return.

Order by 2 PM and it leaves Minneapolis the next working day. Standard delivery takes 3–5 working days and is free over $60 ($6 below); express arrives the next working day for $14. Pick-up at the shop is always free.
Yes, on every order. Each piece is wrapped in recycled kraft with a cotton ribbon, and we write your note on the tag by hand. Prices stay off the parcel when it goes straight to them.
Within 60 days, free, even with the tags off as long as it has not been worn outside. Gift returns go back as store credit to whoever received the gift, so the giver never knows.
Send back any outgrown Framo piece with the free label from your account. We check it, mend it if needed and credit up to 30% of its price within about five days. How Trade-In Works
To Canada, for a flat $18 and free over $150. Duties are included in the price at checkout.
