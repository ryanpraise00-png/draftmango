The Hour

Sixty minutes, minute by minute.

The 90 minutes add the feet and a longer, slower back.

  1. 0–5 minA short talkThe pressure you like, and anywhere to leave alone.
  2. 5–20 minBack and shouldersWarm oil, long strokes from the low back to the neck.
  3. 20–35 minLegs and feetCalves, the backs of the knees, a slow press on the soles.
  4. 35–50 minArms, hands, neckYou turn over under a warm sheet; the hands take their time.
  5. 50–60 minScalp and a slow closeFingertips through the hair, then a few quiet minutes alone.

Before & After

A little care on either side.

Before you come

  • Arrive 30 minutes early and start in the warm pool
  • A warm shower loosens the shoulders
  • No heavy meal in the hour before
  • Tell us about a pregnancy, an injury or a medical condition

After your hour

  • Water and tea in the lounge
  • The warm pool, not the sauna straight away
  • Keep the oil on; it soaks in within the hour
  • Stay in the garden as long as you like, until 9 pm
