How We Renovate

Old houses hide things. We plan for them.

Every renovation starts with opening a small piece of wall or floor where we expect trouble. What we find goes into the estimate, so the surprises happen before you sign, not after.

Then one crew does the work in order: demolition, rough-ins, inspections, walls, finishes. Your project manager sends photos every Friday and tells you which rooms are off-limits next week.

Every renovation includes

  • A probe opening before the estimate
  • Permits, inspections and the inspector on site
  • Dust walls, floor protection and a daily clean-up
  • A Friday report with photos
  • Two years on finishes, ten on structure

What We Remodel

Four kinds of renovation, priced in the open.

Kitchens that work the way you cook.

Walls moved, islands added, plumbing and gas relocated, and the cabinets set by the carpenters who framed the openings.

  • Layout and lighting plan with you
  • Cabinets, counters and tile installed
  • A temporary kitchen while we work

Primary baths, down to the subfloor.

We waterproof every wet wall, slope every shower floor and set the tile ourselves, then come back after a month to check the grout.

  • Curbless showers and tub decks
  • Heated floors and new ventilation
  • One bathroom kept working

Basements you will actually use.

Moisture checked and fixed first, then egress windows, insulation, a bathroom if you want one and built-ins around the media wall.
