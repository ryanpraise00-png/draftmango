Six Disciplines

Find your style.

Every discipline is taught by level, from a first class to company-ready. Most dancers here study two.

Ballet

Ages 5+

Technique, line and musicality: the base of everything else. 13 classes a week.

Contemporary

Ages 9+

Floor work, release and improvisation, for teens and adults. 5 classes a week.

Jazz

Ages 7+

Turns, leaps and sharp style, from Children C1 to the adult open class. 4 classes a week.

Hip Hop

Ages 7+

Grooves, freestyle and crew choreography, with Andre and his crew. 6 classes a week.

Tap

Ages 7+

Rhythm you can hear across the room. Kids on Wednesday and Saturday, adults Tuesday. 3 classes a week.

Musical Theatre

Ages 9+

Singing, acting and dance, together; the showcase's big numbers start here. 3 classes a week.

Good to Know

Before the first plié.

  • Ten minutes early

    Time to change, tie shoes and warm up; the studio doors open five minutes before class.

  • Make-up classes

    Missed one? Take any class at the same level within the term; the front desk books it.

  • Parent Watch Week

    November 16–21: every studio door open, every class on show.
