§ 01Biography

Fifteen years on the sell side, and the buy side.

Catherine joined the firm from law school in 2011 and became a partner in 2018. Her practice covers public and private acquisitions, carve-outs and special committee work, most often for founder-led and family-owned companies selling for the first time.

She led the firm’s work on the $2.4 billion sale of Halden Logistics in 2026 and has advised Northlake Foods on each of its acquisitions since 2019.

The partners elected her Chair in 2024. She still keeps a full deal practice, and sits on the board of a New York legal-aid charity.
