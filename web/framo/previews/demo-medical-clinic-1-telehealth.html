Is Video Right?

Most everyday problems work well on video.

Good for a video visit

  • Colds, flu and COVID
  • Rashes and skin worries
  • Urinary infections
  • Allergies
  • Refills and follow-ups
  • Going over lab results
  • Therapy check-ins
  • Blood pressure reviews

Better in the clinic

  • Injuries needing an X-ray
  • Physicals and vaccines
  • Ear pain in young children
  • Stitches and wound care
  • Lab work and ECGs
  • Anything needing an exam

Chest pain, trouble breathing or signs of a stroke? Call 911, not a video visit.
