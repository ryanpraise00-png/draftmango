How We Think

Four rules we don't bend.

They're written on the wall in both studios, and in every statement of work.

01

Engineers lead

No account layers. Your first call is with the person who'll review the code.

02

Show, don't report

Working software on staging every week beats any status document.

03

You own everything

Your repositories, your cloud accounts, your keys — from the very first commit.

04

Leave it better

Docs, tests and a trained team when we go. Many clients never need us again — that's fine.
