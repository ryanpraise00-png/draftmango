Is The Reset right for you?

It's for you if

  • You're running on empty, but still getting through the week
  • You want structure, and people going through the same thing
  • You can give ninety minutes on a Monday evening
  • You'd like to start small before committing to 1:1 coaching

It's not for you if

  • You're in crisis or signed off right now: please see your GP first, and I can suggest support
  • You're looking for therapy
  • You want one-to-one depth: Next Chapter is the better fit
