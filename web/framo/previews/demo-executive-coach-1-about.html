Coaching leaders since 2012.

Two coaches who had run businesses first started Framo Leadership in two rooms on Hanover Square. Today we are sixty-four coaches, and we still meet every leader before we suggest a single one.

Built by people who ran things.

Amelia Hart had been a chief operating officer; Tom Ashworth had run an engineering firm. Both had been coached, and both had sat through leadership programmes that changed nothing on a Monday morning. In 2012 they started the firm they would have wanted to hire.

Every coach who has joined since has led at the level they coach. We measure every programme against the goals the leader sets, and publish the numbers once an auditor has checked them.

  1. 2012

    Founded in London, two coaching rooms on Hanover Square.

  2. 2015

    Edinburgh opens on Charlotte Square, with the Library.

  3. 2019

    We start tracking every coached leader for eighteen months.

  4. 2021

    The EMCC Quality Award, renewed every three years.

  5. 2026

    Sixty-four coaches in eighteen countries; 4,200 leaders coached.

What we hold to.

Four promises we make to every leader and every sponsor, written into each contract.

  • 01

    Confidential by Default

    What you say stays with your coach. Your sponsor sees progress, never the content.

  • 02

    Exact, Not Generic

    No frameworks for their own sake. Every session ends with one thing you will do differently.

  • 03

    The Leader Sets the Goals

    We measure against your goals, agreed with your sponsor in the first month. Not against ours.

  • 04

    Measured, Honestly

    The same 360°, re-run with the same people. Our numbers are audited every year.
