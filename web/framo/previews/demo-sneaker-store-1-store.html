In the store

The parts of a sneaker shop a website can’t do.

  • The wall

    Six hundred pairs, one of each, sorted by sport. Point at any of them and we bring your size out.

  • Try-on bar

    Every half size of every style, a treadmill for a quick jog and a gait check if you want one. Free.

  • Cleaning bar from $25

    Hand-cleaned uppers and midsoles while you wait, or back the next day for suede and deep stains.

  • Swaps & returns

    Bring any online order back within 30 days, worn indoors only, and swap the size on the spot or get your money back. Raffle pairs are final sale.

  • Gift cards

    Any amount from $25, by email on the day or in a printed sleeve from the counter. Good online and in store.

  • Two-hour pickup

    Order online, choose Milwaukee Ave and collect from the try-on bar in two hours, until 8 PM.

Book a fitting

Twenty minutes with one of our fitters: we measure both feet, watch you walk and jog, and bring out three pairs that suit how you move. Free, and no need to buy.
