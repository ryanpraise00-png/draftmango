About Us Brooklyn, New York · since 2016

A magazine that takes its time

Framo Magazine is an independent quarterly about culture, design, food, travel and ideas, written for people who would rather read one good story than twenty quick ones.

We started Framo in 2016 with one rule: no story goes to print until it is ready, even if the issue has to wait.

The first issue was 96 pages, printed in a run of 2,000 and sold from a folding table at a Brooklyn book fair. Issue 42 runs to 184 pages and reaches readers in 31 countries.

We are still eleven people, and we still read every pitch. Most of what we publish begins as an email from someone who noticed something small and could not stop thinking about it.

How an Issue Is Made

Nine months, four seasons ahead

  1. 01

    Pitch

    Months 1–2

    We read around 600 pitches a season and commission about twenty.

  2. 02

    Report

    Months 2–6

    Writers stay as long as the story needs. The cover story took a year.

  3. 03

    Photograph

    Months 4–7

    Shot on film where we can, in natural light, never staged.

  4. 04

    Print

    Month 9

    On FSC uncoated paper at a family press in Vermont, 38,000 copies.
