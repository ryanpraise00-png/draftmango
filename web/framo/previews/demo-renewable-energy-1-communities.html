How the Boards Work

Five neighbors, twice a year.

Applications close March 31 and September 30. Decisions follow within six weeks.

  1. 01

    Apply in a page

    Any school, fire department, church or club in a host county can ask for up to $15,000.

  2. 02

    The board decides

    Five residents, named by the county; Framo staff sit in but never vote.

  3. 03

    Paid in full

    One check, and every grant listed in our yearly report.
