What It Pays For

Covered, and not covered.

The usual terms for this line. Your agent walks you through your own policy, line by line, before you sign.

Covered

  • The house itself, rebuilt at today's building prices
  • Your belongings, at what it costs to buy them new
  • Fire, smoke, wind, hail and lightning
  • Sudden burst pipes and water from appliances
  • A hotel and meals while the house is repaired
  • Liability if someone is hurt on your property

Not covered

  • Floods from outside, which need a separate flood policy
  • Earthquakes, unless you add the endorsement
  • Slow leaks and wear that build up over time
  • Pests, mould and maintenance
