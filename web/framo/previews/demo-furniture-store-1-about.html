Our Story

Made slowly, in Portland.

Since 2014 we've built sofas, chairs and beds the old way — hardwood frames, hand-tied springs, fabric cut for one order at a time — and sold them without the markup of a middleman.

The Workshop

It started with one sofa in a Slabtown garage.

We couldn't find a sofa that was deep, well made and honest about its price, so we built one. Friends asked for another. Thirteen years later there are 26 of us in a brick warehouse a few streets from that garage.

Every upholstered piece is still made here, to order, by someone who signs the frame before it leaves.

Since 2014

From a garage to three showrooms.

We have grown one workbench at a time, and every upholstered piece is still made a few streets from where the first sofa was.

  1. 2014

    One sofa in a garage

    Nina builds a deep, honest three-seater on NW Raleigh St. Friends order four more.

  2. 2016

    The brick warehouse

    Frame shop, sewing room and fabric library move under one roof in Slabtown.

  3. 2018

    A showroom next door

    The Portland showroom opens on Raleigh St, with every fabric to touch and sit on.

  4. 2021

    Second Home begins

    We start taking pieces back to re-upholster, and pack every delivery in paper and blankets.

  5. 2024

    Seattle and Denver

    Two more showrooms, in Georgetown and RiNo, each with a designer on the floor.

How We Build

Three things we never skip.

They're the parts you can't see in a photo — and the reason we can put ten years on every frame.

  • Kiln-dried hardwood

    Responsibly sourced oak and ash, glued, doweled and corner-blocked. No particleboard, ever.

  • Springs tied by hand

    Eight-way hand-tied coils under every seat, so a sofa keeps its shape for decades.

  • Fabrics that last

    Every fabric rated for 50,000 rubs and free of PFAS stain treatments.

Second Home

When you're done, we take it back.

Moving, or ready for a change? We collect any Framo piece, re-upholster it in the workshop and sell it again at a lower price — and you get 20% of what it sells for as store credit.

  • Packed in paper and blanketsNo foam, no plastic wrap on deliveries since 2021.
  • Recut, not thrown outFabric offcuts become cushion fill and swatch books.
