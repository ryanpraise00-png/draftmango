General Contracting

03Guaranteed Maximum Price

Construction Management at-Risk

We join during design, price it as it develops and commit to a guaranteed maximum price. We carry the risk of keeping it; savings under it go back to the owner.

  • Open-book costs and shared contingency
  • Early packages for steel and site work
  • Phasing plans for buildings that stay open

04One Contract

Design-Build

One contract for design and construction, for owners who want one number to call. Our architect partner and our estimators work at one table, so the drawings are priced as they are drawn.

  • A fixed opening date written into the contract
  • Budgets set by a bond or a board, held
  • Design reviews with the people who will build it

05Our Own Crews

Self-Perform Concrete & Carpentry

  • Rough carpentry, doors, frames and hardware
  • Crews that move between our jobs, not between contractors
