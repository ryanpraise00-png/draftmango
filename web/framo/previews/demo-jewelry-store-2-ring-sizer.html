Ring Sizer & Care Guide

Find your size, then keep it shining

Three ways to measure at home, a chart for US, UK and EU sizes, and how we look after solid gold. Not sure? Resizing is free for life.

Three Ways to Measure

Choose the one that suits you

  1. 01

    Order the free sizer

    A set of brass-coloured gauges arrives in two days, with a return envelope. The most accurate way, after a visit to King Street.

    Free in the US. No account needed. Your sizer is on its way. It posts tomorrow.

  2. 02

    Measure a ring you own

    Lay a ring that fits the same finger on a ruler and read the inside diameter, edge to edge, in millimetres. Put the number in the calculator below.

  3. 03

    Wrap a paper strip

    Wrap a thin strip of paper round the base of the finger, mark where it meets, and measure the length flat. That is the circumference.

Care Guide

Solid gold, looked after gently

Solid gold does not tarnish or wear through, it only softens. Ten minutes a month keeps it bright; once a year, we do the rest for free.

  1. 01

    Soak

    Ten minutes in warm water with a drop of mild washing-up liquid.

  2. 02

    Brush

    A soft toothbrush behind the claws and along the links, where lotion gathers.

  3. 03

    Dry on cotton

    Pat dry, then polish lightly with the cloth in your box. No paper towels.

  4. 04

    Store apart

    Each piece in its own pocket, so diamonds never scratch softer gold.

Lifetime Care

Once a year, bring it home to the bench

We clean it ultrasonically, re-polish it, check every claw under the loupe, re-plate white gold and resize it if you need, for free. Post it to us insured, or drop it at King Street.
