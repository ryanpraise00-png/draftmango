Studio 4, Raven Lane · Galway

One room, one table, a lot of yellow.

I’m Tilda Brix, an illustrator from the west of Ireland. I draw for publishers, brands and public spaces from a bright room above a bike shop.

My Story

I still draw everything in pencil first.

I grew up in a small town on the Clare coast, the kid who drew on the back of every receipt in her parents’ hardware shop. I studied illustration in Dublin, then spent four years in London drawing picture books and learning how a cover has to work from across a bookshop.

In 2021 I came home to the west and took a room on Raven Lane in Galway. The pictures have stayed flat, bright and a little grainy: shapes cut clean, colours mixed by hand, texture from an old risograph I rescued from a school clear-out.

Most days start with a walk through the market and end with paint under my nails. I take on about twenty commissions a year, so each one gets the time it needs.

Tilda
