Brakes

Brakes, measured before they are quoted.

Pads, discs, calipers and fluid for every make. We measure, photograph and text you the numbers first.

Brakes wear quietly until they do not. Every brake job starts with a measurement: pad thickness in millimeters, disc thickness against the maker's minimum, fluid moisture in percent. You get the numbers and the photos before we quote.

Most front-axle jobs take about two hours, so you can wait in the lounge or take the shuttle and be back before lunch.

What is included

  • Pad and disc measurement, photographed
  • New pads with fresh hardware and clips
  • Caliper slides cleaned and lubricated
  • Brake fluid moisture test
  • Bedding-in and a road test
  • 24-month / 24,000-mile warranty

Signs it is time

A squeal or a grind

The wear tab is touching, or the pad is down to the metal.

A shudder when braking

Usually a disc worn unevenly, often after mountain driving.

A soft, long pedal

Moisture in the fluid or air in the lines. Do not wait on this one.

A warning light

Pad sensors, low fluid or an ABS fault. We read it for free.
