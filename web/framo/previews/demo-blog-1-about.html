About · The Writer

Hi, I’m Elin. Pull up a chair.

I write the Sunday letter from a top-floor flat in Stockbridge, Edinburgh: what I cooked, read and walked to that week, and the small rituals that make an ordinary week feel like enough.

The Story

It started as a letter to eleven friends.

In 2019 I left a job in Gothenburg that I was good at and tired of, moved into 48 square meters under a Stockbridge roof, and promised myself one slow hour a day. I wrote to a few friends each Sunday about how it was going. They forwarded it. Their friends forwarded it.

Seven years on, the letter reaches 18,400 people and still sounds like it did then: a kitchen, a window, a walk, and whatever is on the stove.

    The barley soup letter is forwarded 3,000 times in one week. The recipes get their own page.

    Small Trips begins

    A train to Northumberland in October, and a new rule: one weekend away each season, no car.

  4. 2024

    Supporters

    Readers ask how to help. The answer: $6 a month, a printable recipe card, and no ads ever.

  5. 2026

    18,400 readers

    Still one letter, still Sunday at eight, still mostly written before the second cup of tea.
