03Process

From brief to master in about five weeks.

No surprises at the end: every stage is signed off before the next one starts, and you see rough cuts twice a week.

  1. BriefHalo Drift

    • GoalLaunch film
    • Length0:40 + 3 cuts
    • LaunchMarch 2027

    Week 1

    Brief

    A call, then a one-page brief: what the film must do, where it runs and how we will know it worked.

  2. Clay Pass · v12

    Weeks 1–2

    Look Dev

    Form, materials and light first. Style frames are approved before a single frame moves.

  3. Rough Cut · 48f

    Weeks 2–4

    Animation

    Simulation and keyframes, shared as rough cuts twice a week so notes stay small.

  4. 16:9 1:1 9:16

    Week 5

    Delivery

    Graded masters and cut-downs for every format, plus stills for print and the press kit.

05About

Hi, I'm Kaja. I make objects that don't exist feel like you could hold them.

I studied product design in Weimar, spent six years in two Berlin animation studios and have worked on my own since 2021. Every project starts from the object: what it is made of, how light crosses it and how it would move if it were real.
