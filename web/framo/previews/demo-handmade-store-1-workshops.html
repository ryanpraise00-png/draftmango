What to Expect

An apron, a stool and the maker’s own tools

  1. Arrive at ten to

    Coffee, an apron and a seat at the long oak table. Six seats, never more.

  2. Learn by doing

    A short demo, then hands in. The maker comes round to every seat, more than once.

  3. We finish it

    Clay is glazed and fired, candles cure, prints dry. Collect in three weeks or we ship for $7.

  4. Bring a friend

    Seats make good gifts. Private classes for six to twelve from $70 a person.
