Our Story

Founded by operators, for operators.

Hannah Cole started Framo in London in 2008 with two former operating executives. All three had sat on the client side of too many slide decks. They wanted a firm that stayed until the plan showed in the numbers — and that had nothing else to sell.

  • Owned by its partners, with no outside investors
  • No software, no outsourcing, no conflicts to manage
  • 600+ engagements in 30 countries since 2008
