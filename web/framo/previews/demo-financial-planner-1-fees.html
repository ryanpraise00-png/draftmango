Investment Management

Optional, and priced in two steps.

About half of our families keep their accounts where they are and we advise on them. If you'd rather we manage them, the fee is a share of the assets we manage, charged quarterly.

On the first $1M
0.75%
On everything above $1M
0.50%

Example: with $1.5M managed, the yearly fee is $7,500 on the first million plus $2,500 on the rest, $10,000 in all, on top of the planning fee.

What we never charge

  • Commissions on any product
  • Fund revenue sharing or 12b-1 fees
  • Account opening or transfer fees
  • Hourly add-ons for a quick question
  • A fee to leave

Fund expense ratios are paid to the fund companies, not to us; we choose funds that average under 0.10%.

Why a Flat Fee

One flat fee. Nothing to sell.

Many advisors charge a share of what you invest, so the bill grows with your savings even when the work doesn't. We charge one flat annual fee for the plan, whatever your balance.
