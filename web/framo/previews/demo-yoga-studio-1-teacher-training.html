What You Will Study

Two hundred hours, six threads.

  • 60 h

    Asana & alignment

    The standing, seated and resting poses, how they are built and how to offer them two ways.

  • 40 h

    Teaching & sequencing

    Cues, voice, pacing, holding a room and writing a class that ends on time.

  • 30 h

    Anatomy & physiology

    The spine, hips and shoulders; breath and the nervous system; injuries you will meet.

  • 30 h

    Philosophy & ethics

    The Yoga Sutras in plain words, and the ethics of touch, consent and care.

  • 25 h

    Practicum

    Practice teaching in small groups every weekend, then a community class you lead.

  • 15 h

    Breath & meditation

    Simple pranayama and sitting practice you can keep at home.
