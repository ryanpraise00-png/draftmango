Low, wide and slow to get up from

The Arden is one moulded shell of high-resilience foam wrapped in a down-blend layer and upholstered in a single piece of fabric, so there are no seams where you sit. It rests on two steam-bent oak runners joined by a hidden steel plate; felt pads underneath keep it quiet.
