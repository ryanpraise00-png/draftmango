Since 2016 · Nolita, New York

It started with one question: what’s your shade?

Framo Cosmetics is a colour counter for every skin: base in 40 shades, 30 brands tested on real faces, and artists who match you by the window, in daylight.

Who We Are

A makeup store built around the shade, not the shelf.

Most counters sell you what they stock. We start with your skin: its depth, its undertone, how it behaves by four in the afternoon. Then we look across 30 brands for the base, the blush and the lip that work on it, and we tell you honestly when the right shade is not ours.

That is still the whole idea, ten years and two floors later.

Our Story

A makeup kit, a salon chair and Saturday mornings.

In 2016 Clara Jansen, a working makeup artist, started “Shade Match Saturdays” from her kit at a friend’s salon on Elizabeth Street. She matched foundation by eye, by the window, then wrote down which brand made each client’s shade, whoever it was.

Leah Kessler, then a beauty buyer, came for a match and stayed for the waiting list. Two years later they opened a counter of their own at 118 Prince Street, with nine brands and one long shade wall.

“If a shade isn’t right in daylight, it isn’t right. Everything we do comes back to that window.”

Clara Jansen · Co-founder, Creative Director

Ten Years, Seven Chapters

From Saturday matches to 30 brands.

  1. 2016

    Shade Match Saturdays

    Clara matches foundation from her kit at a salon on Elizabeth Street. The waiting list runs six weeks.

  2. 2018

    The counter on Prince Street

    Clara and Leah open at number 118 with nine brands, a shade wall and one chair by the window.

  3. 2019

    Our own line

    Satin Lip Colour launches in eight shades, tested on every artist in the studio. It is 24 shades today.

  4. 2021

    Shade Match goes online

    Three questions, two minutes. Hannah Klein turns the counter’s method into the quiz you can take at home.

  5. 2023

    Second Skin, 40 shades

    Our foundation launches Fair to Deep, in cool, neutral and warm, on the same day for every shade.

  6. 2025

    The second floor

    Four makeover chairs and the lesson table move upstairs. Learn Your Look books out every Saturday.

  7. Today

    30 brands, 34 people

    Fourteen artists, a hand-packing room in Long Island City and 12,480 verified reviews, with notes on every reviewer’s shade.

How We Choose a Brand

Four tests before the shade wall.

Leah’s buying team tests every line we are offered. Most do not make it, and we say why.

4 in 5brands we test never reach the counter

  1. 1

    Worn on 12 skin depths

    Two weeks on our artists and regulars, Fair to Deep, through heat, office air and a long day.

  2. 2

    A real shade range

    Fewer than 30 shades of base and the line does not come in. Nudes have to read as nudes on every depth.

  3. 3

    Vegan, cruelty-free

    We read the formula sheets ourselves: no animal-derived ingredients, no animal testing anywhere.

  4. 4

    A fair price

    The price has to be fair for what is in the tube. A beautiful case does not count as an ingredient.
