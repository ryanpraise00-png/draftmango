How We Teach

Technique first, always with joy.

  1. Every dancer seen

    Up to 10 in the youngest classes and 12 for children, so corrections are personal.

  2. Honest feedback

    A short written report in January and June: where they are, and what comes next.

  3. A stage every year

    Every class works towards the June showcase; performing is part of learning.
