Case Study · Healthcare

A patient app that 1.2 million people use.

Harbor Health runs 46 clinics across New England. We built the app their patients now use to book, check in and see their results — on iOS and Android, in seven months.

The Challenge

Forty-six clinics, four booking systems, one phone line.

Patients waited an average of 11 minutes on hold to book. Each clinic ran its own scheduling software, and none of them spoke to the patient record. Harbor wanted one app — without replacing the systems their staff relied on.

Our Approach

An API in front of everything, the app on top.

A two-week Discovery Sprint mapped every booking flow. We put one scheduling API in front of the four legacy systems, then built native apps on it — testing with real patients in two clinics from week six.

  • Prototype in patients' hands by week three
  • Clinic by clinic rollout, no big launch day
  • HIPAA review signed off before the first release

Under the Hood

One API between the app and four old systems.

Each request travels the same path, from a patient's phone to the clinic's own scheduler.

  1. Patient App Swift · Kotlin
  2. Identity OAuth 2
  3. Scheduling API v3.1
  4. Adapters 4 systems
  5. Patient Record FHIR
