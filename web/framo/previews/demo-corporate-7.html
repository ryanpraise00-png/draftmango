How We Work

Four steps from finding to proof.

The same method whether you face a consent order or a new rule. You see the plan, the team and the fee before we start.

  1. 01 Assess

    2–4 weeks

    Assess

    We read your program the way an examiner will: policies, controls, testing and the evidence behind them.

    • Gap assessment against the rule and your exam history
    • Findings rated by severity and regulatory exposure
    • A board-ready summary in plain English

  2. 02 Remediate

    8–20 weeks

    Remediate

    We fix what matters first, beside your people: controls redesigned, owners named, procedures rewritten.

    • Control design and procedure rewrites
    • Named owners and a weekly remediation tracker
    • Training for the teams who run the controls

  3. 03 Evidence

    4–6 weeks

    Evidence

    We test every fix independently and file the proof, so closing a finding takes one meeting, not three.

    • Independent validation testing
    • An evidence binder indexed to each finding
    • Support in the close-out meeting with the regulator

  4. 04 Sustain

    Ongoing

    Sustain

    A light retainer keeps a partner on call, testing on schedule and your next exam free of surprises.

    • Quarterly control testing
    • Regulatory change alerts mapped to your controls
    • The 24-hour desk included
