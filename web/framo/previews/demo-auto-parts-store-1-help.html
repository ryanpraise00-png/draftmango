Answers

The questions the desk hears most

Fitment guarantee

If a part we marked as fitting the vehicle in your garage does not fit, we take it back, pay the return label and send the right part first, even if the box is open.

Every part number is matched to the year, model and engine, and where it matters to the VIN, against the maker’s data and our own returns. Doubtful fits are checked by a person on the parts desk before the order ships.

Call the parts desk on (303) 555-0172 with the VIN or the number on the old part. We cross-reference 40 brands and usually find it while you wait.

Orders, shipping & pickup

Standard shipping takes 2–4 business days from our Denver warehouse and is free over $49 ($7.99 under). Express reaches most of Colorado the next business day for $14.99 when you order by 2 PM.

Yes. Order by 2 PM and collect after 4 PM at the parts counter of Framo Auto Service, 1840 Larimer Street. Bring the order number; we text you when it is ready.

Until it leaves the warehouse, yes: call the desk or reply to your confirmation email.

Returns & cores

Unopened parts come back free within 90 days. Electrical parts can be returned if they have not been installed; fluids once opened cannot.

Batteries, alternators, calipers and starters carry a refundable core charge. Return the old part within 30 days, with the prepaid label in the box or at the counter, and the charge comes back to your card.

Three to five business days after the part reaches us; core refunds the same week.

Warranty

Every part carries its maker’s warranty: lifetime on Stoppa rotors and Forgehand hand tools, three years free replacement on Ampwell batteries, two years on pads, filters and bulbs.

Parts bought here and fitted at Framo Auto Service have the parts warranty doubled, and the labor is covered for 24 months or 24,000 miles.

Installation

Yes, at Framo Auto Service on Larimer Street. Pick a bay when you check out or later from My Account; most brake, battery and filter jobs are done the same day.

Fixed labor prices for parts bought here: a front brake kit is $149, a battery $39, an oil and filter change $49. The service centre’s pricing page lists the rest.

DIY Guides

Do it yourself, with the right part

Moderate · 30 min

Replace a car battery in your driveway

  1. Note the radio code, then switch everything off and take the key out.
  2. Undo the negative (black) terminal first, then the positive, then the hold-down clamp.
  3. Lift the old battery out with your legs, clean the tray and the terminal clamps with a brush.
  4. Seat the new battery, clamp it down, connect positive first, then negative.

You’ll need

  • Ampwell AGM Battery, Group H6
  • Ampwell Smart Battery Charger

Easy · 5 min

What the dipstick is telling you

  1. Park on level ground and wait five minutes after the engine stops.
  2. Pull the dipstick, wipe it, push it fully home, pull it again.
  3. Between the two marks is fine; at the low mark add half a quart of your grade.
  4. Dark and gritty, or milky? Book a change, or call the desk before you drive far.

You’ll need

  • Torqline Full Synthetic 5W-30
  • Sifta Premium Oil Filter

Easy · 10 min

Streaks on the glass? Fit new wiper blades

  1. Lift the arm off the glass and lay a towel on the windshield in case it snaps back.
  2. Press the tab on the J-hook and slide the old blade down and off.
  3. Clip the new blade on until it clicks; the adapter for your arm is in the box.
  4. Lower the arm gently and run the washers to check the sweep.

You’ll need

  • Clearsweep Beam Wiper Blade, 24-Inch
