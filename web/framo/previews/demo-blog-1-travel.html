Small Trips · 33 Weekends

Weekends a train ride away.

Two nights, one bag, no car. Places a train reaches from Edinburgh in a morning, written up the week after.

How I travel

  • By train, almost always

    All but two trips here start at Waverley. Under five hours, with a window seat and a flask.

  • Two nights is plenty

    One full day in the middle, nothing booked, and a long walk whatever the weather.

  • One bag, packed the night before

    Boots, a wool layer, a paperback, and room for something from a bakery on the way back.
