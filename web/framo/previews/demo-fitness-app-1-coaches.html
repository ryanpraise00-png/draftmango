How Coaching Works

Cued by your run, not a timer.

Every cue is recorded by a coach in a studio, then played at the moment it matters.

  • Your coach writes the sessionWarm-up, the main set and the cool-down, with the cues for each part.
  • The app listens as you goPace, heart rate, distance and the hill ahead decide which cue plays.
  • You hear it once, when it countsYour music dips, the coach speaks, the music comes back.
