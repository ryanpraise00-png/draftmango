01 · Texture

A cushion, not a coat

The bullet glides on in one pass with a soft satin sheen. Colour lands even on the first swipe and builds without caking, so a sheer daytime wash and a full evening lip come from the same tube.

02 · Shade Range

24 shades, tested on 12 skin depths

Every shade is worn by our artists on fair to deep skin before it joins the wall, so a nude reads as a nude on you, not a concealer stripe. Peach Silk and Bare Nude are the two most-matched shades at Shade Match.

03 · Wear

Eight hours, then a soft fade

It holds through coffee and lunch, then fades evenly instead of leaving a ring at the edges. Blot once and press a liner underneath for a long night.

  • 8 hwear, tested on 40 people
  • 3finishes per shade
  • 0fragrance added
  1. 1

    Prep

    Smooth a little Tinted Lip Balm on, then blot. The colour sits better on a soft, even lip.

  2. 2

    Line, if you like

    Trace the edge with a Precision Lip Liner one step deeper than the shade, then blur it in.

  3. 3

    Swipe from the centre

    Start at the middle of the lower lip and work out, then press the lips together once.

  4. 4

    Build or blot

    A second pass for full colour, or tap with a fingertip for a stained, day-off look.
