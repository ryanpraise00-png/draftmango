How Booking Works

Twelve reformers, fair to everyone.

A full room is the reason the policies exist: a reformer held and not used is one someone on the waitlist could have had.

  1. 14 days

    Book Ahead

    Classes open two weeks out; Unlimited members see three.

  2. 2 hours

    Waitlist

    It moves up on its own until two hours before class, and we email you when it does.

  3. 12 hours

    Late Cancel

    Free before that. After, $20; a no-show is $25.

  4. 5 min

    The Door Closes

    Footwork sets your springs for the class; joining halfway through isn't safe.

Monthly Focus

One muscle group, a month at a time.

Every reformer class leans into the month's focus for one block, so a regular week builds on itself.

  • November · Now

    Glutes & Obliques

    Side-lying series, skating, kneeling side bends — the muscles that keep the pelvis level.

  • December

    Upper Back & Shoulders

    Rowing in the straps and long-box pulls for the posture a desk takes away.

  • January

    Deep Core & Breath

    The hundred, coordination and slow roll-downs — a reset to start the year.
