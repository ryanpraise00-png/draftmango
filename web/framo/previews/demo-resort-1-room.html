Five Rooms of One Villa

The Villa

Four rooms around a pool, behind a garden wall.

Eight of these stand along the west beach, each in its own walled garden of sea grape and buttonwood. The bedroom, the bathroom and an open living pavilion face a plunge pool and a deck where breakfast arrives at the hour you name.

A sand path runs forty yards through the garden gate to the beach. Your villa host looks after the rest, from the snorkel boat to a table at the Coral Room.
