Our Story

Small batches, actives on the front, since 2019.

Framo Ritual began as one water cream made in a kitchen in Ojai. Today it is a studio, a lab and a shelf of 24 brands, all chosen the same way.

Why We Exist

Skin care you can read before you buy it.

We are a skincare store run by a chemist and a buyer. Every formula on our shelf, ours or a partner’s, prints its actives and their strength on the front, has no added fragrance in a leave-on active and passes our own lab before it gets a place. Most do not. The ones that do make short routines that work, and we would rather sell you three of them than ten of anything else.

The Founders

A chemist and a buyer who kept asking why.

Maren Aldridge spent eight years formulating for other people’s brands and watching the actives get cut to fit a price. Clara Dunmore spent ten years buying for beauty counters and could not get a straight answer on what was inside. In 2019 they made one cream in Maren’s kitchen, wrote everything in it on the jar, and sold 300 at the Sunday market.

“If a formula needs fragrance to feel nice, it is not finished yet.”
Maren AldridgeCo-founder, Formulation
“We buy the way we would shop: fewer brands, every one of them tested.”
Clara DunmoreCo-founder, Buying

The Ritual Standard

How a formula earns its place on the shelf.

Read the Ingredients A–Z
  1. Week 1

    The brief

    One concern, one active, the strength that the research supports. If a brand cannot tell us the percentage, the talk ends there.

  2. Weeks 2–6

    On the bench

    Ines and Maren test texture, pH and how it layers under SPF; 30 of our team wear it for four weeks.

  3. Weeks 7–12

    Three months on the shelf

    Stability at room temperature, in heat and in daylight, in the pack it will ship in.

  4. Every batch

    Before it ships

    pH and appearance checked on every batch, made within 90 days of reaching you.

Strength on the front

Every active and its percentage printed where you can see it, before you open the box.

No added fragrance

None in any leave-on active; a light scent only in a few rinse-offs and body care.

Tested in our lab

pH and stability on every batch, ours or a partner’s; 1,300 batches since 2020.

Made within 90 days

Small batches, so the vitamin C on your shelf is as fresh as the day it was made.

Glass comes back

Send empty glass packs back free; we refill or recycle every one.

Never on animals

No formula, ingredient or partner brand tested on animals, at any stage.
