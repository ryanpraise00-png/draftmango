About Framo Move

Built by runners in Boulder.

We started Framo Move after one too many plans that didn’t care about our week. Seven years on, it coaches 6.2 million people.

Join the Team Press

2019 Founded after a Saturday club run

Our Story

It started with a missed run.

Claire had a marathon plan printed on her fridge. One sick week and the whole thing fell apart: the paper didn’t know, and didn’t care. Daniel wrote a script that night to move the missed runs around her calendar.

That script became the first adaptive plan. Today our coaches, engineers and designers still run together every Saturday, and every feature ships after the team has trained with it for a month.
