Our story

It started with a worn-out headphone cushion.

In 2016 Julia Brandt and Chris Dalton rented a narrow shop on East 6th Street and fixed what Austin brought in: cracked cushions, tired batteries, buds that only played on one side.

Customers kept asking the same thing at the counter: what should I buy instead? So they started selling only the gear that had already survived their repair bench. That rule never changed.

Today the bench is a lab, the back room is a warehouse that ships next day across the US, and the counter is still open as the Service Bar.

How we test

Four weeks on the bench before it reaches the shelf

About one product in four is turned down. When a brand fixes the problem, we test it again from step one.

  1. 1TeardownWe open one unit of every new product: build, battery cells, how it would be repaired.
  2. 2500 charge cyclesBatteries are cycled and logged. The capacity on the box has to hold.
  3. 3Drops, rain and sweatWaist-height drops on concrete; every IP rating checked in our rain booth.
  4. 4Two weeks of real lifeThe team wears and carries it daily, then writes the specs you read on the page.
