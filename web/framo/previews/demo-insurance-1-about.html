How It Started

Two desks, three carriers and one promise.

Helen Fairweather and Mark Greaves spent the nineties pricing risk for a large carrier. They kept seeing the same thing: people bought whatever policy the first caller sold them, and found the gaps on the worst day of their year.

In the spring of 1998 they rented a storefront on NW 23rd Avenue, signed three carriers and made one promise to every client: we work for you, and we'll tell you when switching saves money.

Helen's daughter Lena joined as an agent in 2011 and runs the agency today with Owen Tremaine, who built the claims desk. Helen and Mark still read every client letter we get.

What Independent Means

Four promises we've kept since the first desk.

An independent agency isn't owned by an insurer. It's the difference between a salesperson and someone on your side.

  • We work for you

    Not for one insurer. We compare 23 and bring back the three that fit you best.

  • We re-shop every renewal

    Sixty days before, every year, and we tell you if switching saves money.

  • One agent, quote to claim

    The person who set up your policy is the person who picks up when it matters.

  • No fees, ever

    The carrier pays our commission, and we'll tell you what it is if you ask.
