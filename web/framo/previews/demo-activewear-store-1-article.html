Your first 5K with Run Club: an eight-week plan

Three runs a week, one of them with us on Saturday, from walk-run intervals to a steady 5K on the Harbor Loop.

Lucas AchesonRun Club captain · Oct 12, 2027 Jump to the Plan
How It Works Weeks 1–4 Weeks 5–8 What to Wear

Most people who tell me they are not runners have simply started too fast. They run one hard mile, feel terrible and stop. This plan starts slower than you think you need, and that is the point.

You run three times a week: two short sessions on your own, and Saturday with the club, where the 3K group walks some of it and nobody finishes alone. By week eight you run the 5K Harbor Loop without stopping.

The RulesHow it works

  • Easy means easy. You should be able to talk in full sentences. If you cannot, walk until you can.
  • Never two days in a row. Rest days are when your legs adapt. A walk or the mobility routine is fine.
  • Repeat a week if you need to. Eight weeks is a guide, not a deadline. Saturday is still Saturday.

From the clubOf the members who started on the 3K Easy Loop last year, 7 in 10 ran the Harbor Loop by their tenth Saturday.

Weeks 1–4From walk-run to running blocks

Each session starts and ends with five minutes of brisk walking. The numbers are run / walk, repeated.

Weeks one to four
Week Tuesday Thursday Saturday with the club
1 1 / 2 min × 8 1 / 2 min × 8 3K Easy Loop, walk-run
2 2 / 2 min × 6 2 / 2 min × 6 3K Easy Loop, walk-run
3 3 / 1.5 min × 5 3 / 1.5 min × 5 3K Easy Loop, run the flat half
4 5 / 1.5 min × 4 5 / 1.5 min × 4 3K Easy Loop, walk only at the turn

Weeks 5–8To the Harbor Loop

Week five is the jump most people worry about. Slow down more than feels right and it will be fine.

Weeks five to eight
Week Tuesday Thursday Saturday with the club
5 8 / 2 min × 3 10 / 2 min × 2 3K Easy Loop, all running
6 12 / 2 min × 2 15 min easy 5K Harbor Loop, walk the bench stop
7 20 min easy 22 min easy 5K Harbor Loop, back pace group
8 25 min easy 15 min easy 5K Harbor Loop, no stops

Week eight is a party. Tell the host at check-in it is your first full 5K. The club waits at the end, and the coffee is on us.

What to WearKit that stays out of the way

You do not need much. Shoes that feel good in the store, something that does not chafe, and a layer for the 8 AM breeze off the bay.

  • Pace Run Shorts or Stride Run Short: lined, with a pocket that holds a phone without bouncing.
  • Lift Bra: medium support is plenty for easy miles; size by your underbust in the Size Guide.
  • Glide Long Sleeve: thumbholes for cold starts, off and tied round your waist by mile one.
