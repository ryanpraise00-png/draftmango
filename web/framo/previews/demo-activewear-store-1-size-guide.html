How to Measure

A soft tape, a mirror, two minutes

Measure over light underwear, the tape flat and level, snug but not tight.

  1. 01

    Waist

    Around the narrowest part, usually just above the belly button. Breathe out first.

  2. 02

    Hips

    Around the fullest part of your hips and seat, feet together.

  3. 03

    Underbust

    Right under the bust, where a bra band sits. This picks your bra size.

  4. 04

    Inseam

    From the crotch to the ankle bone, on a pair of leggings that fit you well.
