Eleven people, one rulebook.

We started in a print shop and still work like one: nothing leaves the studio before it’s proofed.

Freja Holm and Mads Lund founded Framo in 2016, in a former print shop in Kødbyen, Copenhagen’s old meatpacking district. The presses are gone; the long tables and the habit of pinning everything to the wall stayed.

We are independent and eleven people strong — strategists, designers, writers and motion people in one room. Since then we’ve drawn more than 140 identities for clients in 22 countries.
