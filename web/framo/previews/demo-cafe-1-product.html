Brew Guide

How we make it at the bar.

Weigh the coffee and the water; it’s the one habit that changes the cup. Water just off the boil.

  1. Pour-Over

    22 g : 350 g

    Medium-fine · 3:30 total. Bloom with 50 g for 40 seconds, then pour in slow circles.

  2. Drip

    60 g : 1 L

    Medium · about two tablespoons per cup, a fresh filter every time.

  3. French Press

    30 g : 500 g

    Coarse · 4 minutes. Break the crust, skim, press slowly.

  4. Espresso

    18 g : 36 g

    Fine · 26–30 seconds. Our dial at the bar; great under milk.
