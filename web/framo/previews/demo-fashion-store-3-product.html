Double-Face Wool Coat

4.964 reviews Runs generous

Price: $1,290

Free express shipping and returns · or 4 payments of $322.50

Two layers of wool, woven as one. Double-face cloth needs no lining: the edges are folded in and stitched by hand, so the coat is soft, light for its warmth and hangs straight to the calf. Notch lapels, three horn buttons, deep patch pockets.

Colour: Chocolate
Size: Choose one

The buying team’s fit note Cut generously through the body and shoulder. Take your usual size for room over knitwear, or one down for a closer line. Juliette Kroon, Buying Director

  • Free express shipping At your door Wednesday if you order by 2 pm
  • 30-day returns The label and the pickup are on us
  • Try it in store On the rail at Greenwich Street and East Oak Street
  • Relaxed fit, dropped shoulder, falls to mid-calf (46″ in size S)
  • Notch lapels, three dark horn buttons, two deep patch pockets
  • Unlined: the double-face edges are folded and hand-stitched
  • Centre back seam with a long vent for an easy stride

90% virgin wool, 10% cashmere, woven double-face in Biella and made up in Maison Ardelle’s Paris workshop. Dry clean only. Brush after wearing and hang on a broad wooden hanger; give it a day between wears.

Free express shipping on every US order, one to two business days; a signature is asked for orders over $1,000. Returns within 30 days, unworn with tags: we send the label and pick it up. Shipping and returns
