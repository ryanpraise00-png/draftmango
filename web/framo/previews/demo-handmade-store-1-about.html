Since 2017 · Portland, Oregon

Six makers in a garage, forty on Division Street

Framo Made began as a Saturday market in Tobias Dunsmore’s woodshop. Today it is a shop, a studio and a website for forty independent makers in Oregon and Washington.

Meet the Makers Visit the Studio

What We Are

A shop that works for the people who make things

We sell what forty independent makers make by hand — stoneware, candles, linen, baskets, wood, prints and jewelry — wrap every piece at our studio, and send 80% of each sale straight back to the person who made it.

No factories, no resellers, no “designed in” anywhere. Every maker on the shelf makes every piece themselves, in a studio we have stood in.

Our Story

From a garage market to a long oak table

Maren taught ceramics at a community studio. Tobias built furniture. Most of their friends made things by hand and had nowhere good to sell them.

  1. 2017

    The Bench Market

    Six makers, folding tables and coffee in Tobias’s garage workshop off SE Division, every Saturday from May to December.

  2. 2019

    A shop of our own

    Twelve makers move into 1418 SE Division St, a 1924 brick bicycle shop. The 80/20 split goes into the very first maker agreement.

  3. 2020

    The shop goes online

    Every parcel is packed and wrapped at the studio, with a note in the maker’s own hand. It still is.

  4. 2022

    The back room

    The storeroom becomes a workshop room. Ida Lund teaches the first wheel class to six people and a lot of clay.

  5. 2024

    Thirty studios

    Makers from Astoria to Spokane, and the first Bench Letter goes out on a Sunday morning.

  6. 2027

    Forty makers, one table

    1,280 pieces, six workshops a month and a team of six — with makers taking turns behind the counter on weekends.

How a Piece Gets Here

From a maker’s bench to your door

We say no more often than yes. A piece earns its place on the shelf before anyone sees it online.

  1. 01A maker applies

    Maren reads every application with two makers from the shop. We ask for photos of the bench, not just the work.

  2. 02We visit the studio

    We stand in every studio before a piece is listed — a garage in Sellwood, a loom in Astoria, a woodshop in Bend.

  3. 03Shot and listed

    Fiona photographs every piece in daylight on Division Street and writes down who made it, from what and where.

  4. 04Wrapped and sent

    Daniel wraps it in kraft and linen with the maker’s card, packs it in paper only and sends it within two days.

What We Promise

Four things we will not change

80% to the maker

Paid every two weeks, on every piece, since the first agreement in 2019. No listing fees, ever.

Made by them, by hand

No factories, no outsourcing, no resellers. If a maker takes on help, we meet the helpers too.

Paper, never plastic

Kraft paper, paper tape and crinkled paper padding. The linen ribbons are offcuts from Linden Loom.

Close to home

Every studio is in Oregon or Washington, a day’s drive from Division Street, so we can visit.

The Small Print

Shipping, returns and care

Shipping

Every order is packed and wrapped at the studio and leaves within one to two business days.

  • Standard, 3–6 days: $7, free over $90
  • Express, 1–2 days: $16
  • Pick up at the studio: free, next day

Returns

Free within 30 days in the US — we email a prepaid label. Custom and engraved pieces are final sale.

  • Broken in transit? A photo within 7 days
  • We replace it or refund it, your choice
  • The maker is paid either way

Care

Handmade things last decades with a little care. Each piece comes with its maker’s card.

  • Stoneware: dishwasher and microwave safe
  • Linen: cool wash, line dry — the full guide
  • Wood: hand-wash, oil monthly
