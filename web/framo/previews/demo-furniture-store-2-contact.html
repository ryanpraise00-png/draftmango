Delivery, the trial, returns and the warranty

The small print, in plain words. Anything missing? The orders desk is a call away.

Free over $250, otherwise $39, to the 48 contiguous states. In-stock pieces leave the Denver warehouse in 3–5 business days, made-to-order pieces in 4–6 weeks. We text you 48 hours ahead to pick the day and a two-hour window, and our crew carries every box to the room you choose. Assembly is $79 per order, any size.

Live with any sofa for 100 nights from the day it arrives. If it isn’t the one, call the orders desk: we collect it for free, no need for the box, and refund you in full within five working days of collection.

Yes, within 30 days, in their boxes. Drop them at the Denver studio for free, or we collect for $49. Sale pieces follow the same rules; made-to-order finishes can be returned for a 15% restocking fee.
