How a project runs

Eight weeks, four phases. You always know where we are.

  1. Week 1

    Listen

    A 90-minute call and a one-page brief.

  2. Weeks 2–3

    Shape

    Two directions, you pick one.

  3. Weeks 4–7

    Make

    Design in the open, a review every Friday.

  4. Week 8

    Hand Over

    Files, guidelines and 30 days of support.

What I don't do

  • No logo-only jobs in a week.

    A logo without its colours, type and rules is a file, not an identity. The Brand Sprint is the smallest honest version.

  • No unpaid pitches or spec work.

    You can judge me by sixteen finished projects and a call. I will not guess at your brand for free against four other studios.

  • No anonymous white-label work.

    If I design it, you talk to me. I do not disappear behind an agency's account manager.

  • No more than two projects at once.

    That is why there is a waiting list, and why your Friday review always happens on Friday.
