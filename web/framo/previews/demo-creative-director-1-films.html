How a Film Gets Made

Six to ten weeks, brief to final grade.

  1. 01Treatment A written film with references, music and a shot list, in the first week.
  2. 02Casting & location Street or club casting, scouts on two continents if the story needs it.
  3. 03The shoot One to five days with a crew of nine to twenty-five, hired for the brief.
  4. 04Edit & grade I sit in every session, then deliver the master and its cutdowns.
