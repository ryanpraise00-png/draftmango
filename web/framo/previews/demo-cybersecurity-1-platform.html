01 · Endpoint

Ransomware stopped on the first file, then rolled back.

A light agent on every laptop and server watches what processes do, not what they are called. When something starts encrypting, it is stopped and the files come back.

  • Windows, macOS, Linux and cloud workloads
  • One-click isolation from the console
  • Seven days of file rollback

02 · Identity

Stolen logins caught by where, when and how they are used.

Most intrusions start with a real password. Identity learns how each person signs in and holds the session that does not fit, before it reaches mail or money.

  • Impossible travel and new-device checks
  • Session revoke and forced reset in one step
  • Dormant admin accounts flagged weekly

03 · Cloud

Exposed workloads ranked by what an attacker could reach.

Connect each account in a few minutes, read-only. Cloud lists what is open, then ranks it by the data behind it, so the first fix is the one that matters.

  • Agentless scan of every account and region
  • Attack paths, not a list of 4,000 warnings
  • Fixes as tickets or infrastructure code

04 · Email

Phishing and payment fraud pulled before anyone clicks.

Email reads the message the way a careful colleague would: who usually writes, how they write and what they ask for. A lookalike invoice leaves every inbox at once.

  • A report button your people will use
