Before You Book

Four small things, said once.

Passes & Prices

  • Book 14 days ahead

    Classes open two weeks out. Full? Join the waitlist — you move up automatically until two hours before.

  • 12-hour late cancel

    Inside 12 hours the class counts on a pack, or $15 on Unlimited. No-shows the same.

  • Doors close at five past

    Five minutes after the start the room is settling. Your first time, come ten minutes early.

  • Garden Room at 95 °F

    Drink water during the day, eat lightly two hours before, and rest whenever you need.
