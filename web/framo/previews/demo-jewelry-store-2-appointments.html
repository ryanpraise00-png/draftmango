What to Expect

An hour that goes quickly

No pressure and no selling: most clients leave with a shortlist and photos, and decide at home.

  1. 01

    A tray, ready for you

    From your notes we lay out the pieces before you arrive: shapes, golds and sizes to try, and loose stones if it is bridal.

  2. 02

    Try everything on

    Every setting on your own hand, in daylight by the window. We measure your size properly while the tea cools.

  3. 03

    A shortlist by email

    The next morning: photos of what you tried, prices and the bench time. Hold any piece for seven days.
