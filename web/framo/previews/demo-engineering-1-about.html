Sheet 06.1Our Story

We draw it, then we build it.

Framo Engineering began in 1968 as a drawing office of six engineers above a shipyard supplier on the Clyde. Their rule was simple: whoever draws a structure stands on site while it is built.

Fifty-eight years later the rule still holds. Our designers and builders work as one team, from the first sketch to the last inspection, in 22 offices across the UK, Ireland, the Netherlands and the Nordics.

From one office to twenty-two.

Nine moments that shaped the company, from the Clyde and back again.

  1. 1968

    Founded in Glasgow

    Six engineers open a drawing office on the Clyde.

  2. 1979

    First motorway bridge

    A 410 m viaduct over the Calder valley, designed and built in-house.

  3. 1986

    Manchester opens

    Our first office in England, and head office since 2004.

  4. 1994

    Rail division opens

    Station and track engineers join to form Framo Rail.

  5. 2008

    Water & energy

    Treatment works, flood defences and the first wind connections.

  6. 2012

    Across the water

    Offices open in Dublin and Rotterdam.

  7. 2017

    Digital twin practice

    Every new project gets a live model of itself.

  8. 2020

    The Nordics

    Copenhagen, Oslo and Stockholm join the network.

  9. 2026

    Kestrel Crossing opens

    1,240 m over the Clyde, a mile from where we started.
