Since 2022 · Reichenberger Straße 120

A print works, now a place to live.

For eighty-eight years this building printed Berlin’s posters and timetables. Today 36 apartments fill its five floors.

Our Story

We wanted the flat you get when a friend in Berlin lends you theirs, with someone to call when the boiler stops.

Marlene ran guest relations for city hotels for a decade; Theo spent his twenties moving between project jobs and short lets. In 2019 they found the empty print works on Reichenberger Straße and a landlord willing to let them try something between a hotel and a home.

The conversion kept the brick, the steel windows and the iron columns, and added kitchens you can cook in, desks you can work at and walls thick enough to sleep. We opened in the spring of 2022 with 36 apartments and a rule: everything a long stay needs is in the price.

The Building

Five floors, four lives.

  1. 1906

    A print works on the canal side

    Built for a poster and timetable printer, with steel windows for daylight on the presses.

  2. 1951

    Rebuilt after the war

    The roof and top floor were rebuilt; the rotary presses ran on until the nineties.

  3. 1994

    The presses fall silent

    Twenty-five years of studios, a bike workshop and, for a while, nothing at all.

  4. 2022

    Framo Residences opens

    36 apartments, the lounge in the old type room and the courtyard planted again.
