Process & Rates

From a few lines to a finished picture.

How a commission runs, what it costs, how long it takes and what you own at the end. No small print.

Four steps, every time.

  1. 1

    Brief & quote

    You tell me the story, the size and the date. I reply within two working days with ideas and a fixed price.

    2 days

  2. 2

    Three rough sketches

    Pencil roughs of three different ideas. You pick one, we sharpen it together, changes included.

    1 week

  3. 3

    Colour & texture

    The chosen sketch in full colour and grain, with one round of colour changes before it’s final.

    1–2 weeks

  4. 4

    Files, or the wall

    Print-ready files and layered originals, or, for a mural, me on the scaffold with a van of paint.

    On the date

What helps in a brief

  • Who will see it, and where: a shelf, a tin, a page, a wall
  • Sizes, the print method and the date it’s needed
  • Two or three pictures you love, by anyone
  • Your budget, even a rough one

How Long It Takes

A typical diary, week by week.

  1. Week 1

    Read the manuscript, talk to the editor, three roughs.

  2. Week 2

    Sharpen the chosen idea; type placement with the designer.

  3. Weeks 3–4

    Colour, texture, the spine and the back.

  4. Week 5

    One round of colour changes, endpapers, print files.

  1. Week 1

    The range, the shelf and the competition, then three directions.

  2. Weeks 2–3

    The lead design drawn out, tested on a real dieline.

  3. Weeks 4–6

    The rest of the range and the repeat pattern.

  4. Weeks 7–8

    Colour proofs with your printer, final files.

  1. Weeks 1–2

    Site visit, wall survey, a workshop with the people who’ll walk past it.

  2. Weeks 3–4

    The design to scale, colour samples on the actual wall.

  3. Weeks 5–6

    Priming, the chalk grid, painting (about a square metre and a half an hour).

  4. Week 7

    Two coats of varnish, a launch morning, a care sheet.

  1. Day 1, morning

    The article and the slot; three thumbnails by lunch.

  2. Day 1, evening

    The chosen rough, sized to the layout.

  3. Day 2

    Final art in colour, delivered by the agreed hour.

  4. Longer features

    Up to ten days for a full-page opener and spots.

Questions

Asked most weeks.

Book covers and packaging six to eight weeks ahead; murals two to three months, more in summer. Editorial spots often fit in the same week. New work is booking from March 2027.

I’ll work inside your colours, type and format, but the drawing will always be mine. If you need a different hand, I’m happy to recommend friends.

You license it for the use we agree: the product, the territory, the run. I keep the copyright and the originals. Full buy-outs are possible and quoted separately.

Yes, across Ireland and the UK, and further for the right wall. Travel and lodging are added at cost, and I bring my own scaffold within Ireland.
