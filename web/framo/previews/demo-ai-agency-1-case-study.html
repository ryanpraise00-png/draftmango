A three-hour review, done in nine minutes.

How Quillhaven Insurance put a triage agent in front of every new claim, with an adjuster approving each payout.

The brief

Sector
Insurance
Team
420 People
Systems
Claims Platform, Policy System
Timeline
Live in 8 Weeks

The Challenge

Every claim waited in the same queue.

Quillhaven receives about 1,100 new claims a week. Each one sat in a shared inbox until an adjuster opened it, read the attachments and decided where it belonged. A cracked windscreen waited as long as a house fire.

The Approach

Read first, route second, pay only with a person.

We mapped the first review with six adjusters, then built one agent that does it on every claim: it reads the notice and the photos, checks the policy and proposes a route. Anything that pays out waits for an adjuster.
