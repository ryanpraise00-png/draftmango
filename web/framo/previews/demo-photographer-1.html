Kind Words

How It Works

From the first call to the final grade

  1. 01

    A call, not a moodboard

    Thirty minutes on what the pictures are for, who sees them and where they will run.

  2. 02

    A schedule built on light

    I scout by map and weather, then plan the days around sunrise and the last two hours.

  3. 03

    A small crew on the day

    Me and one assistant. Guests and staff barely notice we are there, which is the point.

  4. 04

    Selects in 48 hours

    A first edit two days after the shoot, the final grade within ten. Every frame graded by hand.

About

Born above a bakery in Le Panier

I bought my first camera with summer wages at sixteen, spent eight years assisting in Paris and went freelance in 2013. I shoot digital and film side by side, and I grade every frame myself.
