How We Work

A plan in six weeks. A planner for years.

Four steps from the first call to a written plan, then the same CFP® professional checking in as life changes.

What happens, and when.

You'll always know what's next and what it costs. Nothing is billed until you sign the planning agreement after the intro call.

A written roadmap, presented in one meeting: what to do first, what can wait and why.

You

04 / 04

Quarterly, then twice a year

Implement & Ongoing

The plan only works if it happens. We do the paperwork with you, then keep it current.

You
Message your planner any time; meet quarterly in year one, then twice a year.
We
Open and move accounts, update the plan after big changes and review it every year.

Your Year With Us

Six checkpoints, on the calendar.

Besides the meetings, we watch the dates that cost families money when they're missed.

  1. January

    Tax-document checklist

    What to gather, and the last call for prior-year IRA and HSA contributions.

  2. March

    Return review

    Our Enrolled Agent reads your draft return before it's filed, alongside your CPA.

  3. June

    Mid-year review

    Cash flow, savings rate and goals, checked against the plan, by video or in person.

  4. October

    Open enrollment

    Health plans, HSA, life and disability: the benefits choices for next year.

  5. November

    Year-end tax moves

    Roth conversions, loss harvesting and charitable gifts while there's still time.

  6. December

    Annual plan refresh

    The whole plan updated for the new year, with next year's numbered actions.
