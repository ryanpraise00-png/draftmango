Three ways to breathe.

The fastest way to calm down is already with you. Each pattern takes about a minute.

  • 4 · 7 · 8

    Relaxing Breath

    In for four, hold for seven, out for eight. The long exhale tells your body it is safe to rest.

    For falling asleep

  • 4 · 4 · 4 · 4

    Box Breathing

    Four even sides: in, hold, out, hold. Simple enough to count when your head is full.

    For a busy mind

  • 5 · 5

    Even Breath

    Five seconds in, five out, for a few minutes. A steady rhythm that settles the heart rate.

    For steady focus
