Where to Start

Three paths most clients take.

The Diagnostic tells you which one is yours. These are the sequences we see most often.

  1. A

    Pricing, then Sales

    For teams that win deals but leave money on the table. New packages first, then a playbook so reps hold the price.

    • Typical: 6 months
    • Payback in 1–2 quarters
  2. B

    Retention, then Pricing

    For subscription businesses losing customers in year one. Fix onboarding and renewals before you raise prices.

    • Typical: 8 months
    • Churn down within a year
  3. C

    Go-to-Market, then Marketing

    For companies entering a new segment or region. Size the market and the territories, then build the demand plan.

    • Typical: 5 months
    • First pipeline in 11 weeks
