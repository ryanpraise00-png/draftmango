Process

Four steps, one release people finish.

The same rhythm for a two-week sprint or a six-month build. Only the length of each step changes.

Week 1

Find the real drop-off

Analytics, support tickets and five interviews with people who quit. We agree on one metric and the number that would count as a win.

You get a one-page brief and the success metric.

Weeks 2–3

Map the shortest path

Flows on a wall before pixels on a screen. Every step has to earn its place; most products lose a third of theirs here.

You get the flow map and the screens it needs.

Weeks 3–8

Test it every Thursday

A clickable prototype in front of six people each week. Your team watches live, and Friday's changes come straight from Thursday's sessions.

You get the prototype and a two-minute clip per session.

Until release

Ship it with the engineers

Specs inside the components, reviews of every build and a launch checklist. Three months later we read the metric together.

You get production files and the after-launch read-out.

About

Hi, I'm Ezra. I design for the tired user.

The parent at 11 PM, the owner between two customers, the runner at mile nine. Twelve years in, I still start every project by watching someone like that use what you have today.

I led design at a health-tech company and ran the design system at a fintech scale-up before going independent in 2021. I work from Portland, on Pacific time, with teams across the US and Europe.
