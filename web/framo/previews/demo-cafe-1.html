The Bakehouse

While Brooklyn sleeps, we bake.

Flour, water, salt and a levain we’ve fed every day since 2015. Four hours from the first mix to the first customer.

  1. 3:00 am MixThe dough comes together while the street is dark.
  2. 5:00 am ShapeEvery loaf by hand, then a rest in its basket.
  3. 6:30 am BakeInto the deck oven, out with a crackle.
  4. 7:00 am OpenStill warm on the shelves at all three cafés.
