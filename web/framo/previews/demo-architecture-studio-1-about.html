A practice of twenty-four, since 2009

Architects, interior designers and two model makers in a former print works in Hudson, with a project office in Brooklyn.

01 / How It Started

Two architects, one borrowed table and a barn that needed a roof.

Clara Weiss and Jonah Reyes met at a firm in Copenhagen and came home to the Hudson Valley in 2009 with one commission: a neighbor's barn. They drew it at a table borrowed from a print shop on Warren Street.

Four years later they bought the print works. The table is still there, now one of six, and the studio has grown one project at a time to twenty-four people and 140 buildings.

— Clara Weiss & Jonah Reyes, Partners

02 / How We Work

Three rules we keep on every job

  1. 01

    Begin on the site

    Before we draw a line we walk the land at three times of day, in two seasons if we can.

  2. 02

    Keep what is there

    An old beam, a stone wall, a stand of trees. What already works stays, and we design around it.

  3. 03

    Draw every detail

    Door handles, stair nosings, the joint where oak meets stone. A partner signs off on every sheet.
