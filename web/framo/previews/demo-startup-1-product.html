Product Tour

One loop, from “could you add…” to “it shipped.”

Framo does four things and tries to do them well: it collects every request, ranks it, plans it in public and tells people when it lands.

01 · Collect

Every request lands in one inbox.

Chat threads, support emails, sales call notes and your own portal all feed the same list. Nobody keeps a private spreadsheet any more.

  • Public Portal & WidgetA page and a button where your users post and vote themselves.
  • Chat & Email CaptureForward a thread or react with an emoji. It becomes a post with the quote attached.
  • Import from a SpreadsheetDrop in a CSV, map the columns, keep the backlog you already have.

02 · Prioritize

Know what matters, not who shouted.

Every vote carries the person, their plan and what their account is worth. Sort by any of it and the loudest thread stops winning by default.

  • Impact ScoringWeigh requests by revenue, reach and effort. Your formula, not ours.
  • Segments by PlanFilter by plan, team size or region, then save the view.
  • Merge DuplicatesFive posts, one request. Every vote and comment comes along.

04 · Announce

Close the loop. People remember.

Mark a post shipped and Framo drafts the changelog entry, then thanks every person who asked for it, by name.

  • Release EmailsOne short email to everyone who voted, sent from your domain.
  • In-App WidgetA quiet badge in your product when something new lands.
  • Close the LoopEvery voter sees the status change on the post they backed.
