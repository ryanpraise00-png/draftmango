Our Story

Skincare that explains itself, made in San Diego.

Framo Beauty started at a kitchen table in North Park with one question: why does a good serum need forty ingredients? Five years on, it is twenty formulas, short lists and a small lab by the bay.

Every batch is made and tested in our Little Italy lab

How It Started

Two formulators, one short list.

Maya Ashby spent ten years developing skincare for other people’s labels. Iris Fenwick had the skin those labels never seemed to suit: dry, quick to flush, tired of fragrance. In 2022 they started making the products they wanted to use, with the dose of every active printed on the box.

The rule has not changed since: if an ingredient does not do something you can feel or see, it does not go in. Every formula is tested by 120 people for four weeks before it reaches the shop.

Five Years

From forty jars to twenty formulas.

  1. 2022

    A kitchen table in North Park

    Maya and Iris mix the first Cloudmilk Cleanser in small jars for forty friends with skin like Iris’s.

  2. 2023

    The 120-tester rule

    Four weeks, every skin type, 85% happy or back to the bench. Dewline Serum is the first to pass, at 91%.

  3. 2024

    A lab and a studio in Little Italy

    1840 Kettner Boulevard: the bench behind glass, and a counter at the front to try everything.

  4. 2025

    Refills

    Seven bestsellers come back in refill pouches; glass and aluminium for everything else.

  5. 2026

    Colour, kind to skin

    Lip tints and cheek balms built like skincare, with the same short lists.

  6. Today

    Twenty formulas, no more

    Shipped across the US and Canada, 18,240 reviews in, and a new formula only when an old one retires.

From Bench to Bathroom

How a formula earns its place.

About fourteen months, from the first sketch to the shelf. Most ideas never make it.

  1. 01

    The brief

    One skin problem, one hero active, a target dose and how it should feel.

  2. 02

    The bench

    Thirty to fifty versions in the lab until the texture and the stability are right.

  3. 03

    The trial

    120 people, four weeks, every skin type. Under 85% happy and it goes back to the bench.

  4. 04

    The shelf

    Small batches in San Diego, shipped within a month of being made.
