About Ezra

Twelve years of products for people with no time.

I'm Ezra Ridley, an independent product designer in Portland, Oregon. I design apps and design systems for health, finance and SaaS teams, and I measure the work after it ships.

The Short Version

I started in support. It still shows.

My first job in tech was answering support emails for a scheduling app. Three hundred a week, most of them about the same four screens. I taught myself Sketch to fix them, and never went back.

Since then I have led design at a health-tech company, built the design system at a fintech scale-up and, since 2021, worked independently with teams who want fewer screens and better numbers.

Outside work I run the trails in Forest Park, bake bad bread and mentor junior designers once a month.

Principles

Three things I will not skip.

  1. 01Watch before you drawEvery project starts with real people using what exists today, at the hour they really would.
  2. 02One metric, agreed earlyWe write down the number that counts as a win in week one, and read it again three months after launch.
  3. 03Leave the team strongerFiles, components and habits your team can run without me. The best handoff is not needing one.
