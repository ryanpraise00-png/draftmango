Brand strategy

(01) Service

Positioning, naming and the story behind it: the decisions every later design choice can stand on.

Overview

Before anyone draws a logo, we decide what the brand stands for, who it’s for and how it should sound. Strategy is the brief every later choice answers to.

Duration
3–6 weeksFrom kickoff to the strategy book.
Team
3 senior peopleA strategist, a writer and a design lead.
Outcome
One clear storyWritten down, agreed and ready to brief the design.

What you take away

(06) Deliverables

Everything lands in one shared workspace, written for the people who’ll use it, not filed away after launch.

  1. 01

    Research & Audit

    Interviews, a market scan and an honest look at what you already have.

  2. 02

    Positioning

    Where you play, who you’re for and why you, said in a single line.

  3. 03

    Naming

    Shortlists screened for trademarks, domains and how they sound out loud.

  4. 04

    Messaging & Tone

    Key messages and a voice guide your whole team can actually write in.

  5. 05

    Brand Architecture

    How products, sub-brands and partners sit together, now and later.

  6. 06

    Strategy Book

    One document that holds it all, built to brief designers, writers and hires.

How it runs

(04) Steps over six weeks

One team and a live session every Friday, so you see the thinking while it’s still cheap to change.

  1. Week 1

    Listen

    Stakeholder interviews, customer calls and an audit of every touchpoint.

  2. Week 2

    Frame

    A workshop day to agree the audience, the rivals and the ambition.

  3. Week 3–4

    Shape

    Position, names and messages drafted, tested and refined with you.

  4. Week 5–6

    Hand Over

    The strategy book, a team session and a ready brief for the identity.
