1952 — Where it began

One building on the Herengracht, bought to keep.

Hendrik Vermeer was a surveyor with a simple rule: buy what you understand, look after it, and never sell to make a quarter look good. His first building is still in the group.

The rule outlived the property business. Every company we have bought since — from a machine shop in Eindhoven to a wind developer in Rotterdam — was bought to be owned for decades.

Hendrik Vermeer, founder, 1952

What we hold to.

Three values, written down by the founder's family and still read out at every new board.

  • Patience

    We judge a decision by where it leaves the group in twenty years, not by next quarter.

  • Stewardship

    We hand every business on in better shape than we found it — its people, its assets, its places.

  • Candour

    We report the hard numbers first, to our boards, our people and our shareholders.
