The Arc Studio story

Turn the world down. Keep the music exactly as recorded.

Made for the long stretches: a flight, a commute, an afternoon of deep work. Light enough to forget, quiet enough to hear the detail.

The 7:40 to Dallas, one coffee, nothing but the record.

We started at our Austin bench with one rule: if the noise cancelling changes the sound, it isn't done. So the six microphones measure the room and the inside of each cup, and the tuning stays the same whether the train is loud or the office is quiet.
