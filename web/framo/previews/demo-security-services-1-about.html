Framo Defense began as three security testers who were tired of reports nobody read. Fourteen years on, the people who break in still write every word.

Meet the Team Open Roles

A report should change something.

That was the whole idea in 2012, and it still decides how we hire, what we sell and what we turn down.

We test by hand, we explain what we found to the people who have to fix it, and we come back to check. When clients asked us to keep watching after the test, we built a SOC on the same rule: every alert answered by a person who could have found it.

We're independent, we don't resell software and nobody on the team works on commission.
