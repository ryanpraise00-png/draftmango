Framo Studio — Studio

Fourteen people, one studio.Antwerp

A small, independent studio by the water in Antwerp. We make identities, websites and films for companies that would rather be remembered.

We started with two desks and one rule: the people who win the work do the work. Twelve years on, that rule still runs the studio.

Ilse Maes and Ruben Claes opened Framo in 2014 with two desks on the top floor of a former printworks on the Kattendijkdok. Three years later the studio had the whole floor, and the harbour light that comes with it.

We’re still independent and still fourteen people, on purpose: small enough that the designer in your first workshop is the one at your launch, big enough to make an identity, a website and a film in one go. So far that has meant 60+ clients in 19 countries.

Four things we keep

Not values for a poster: the habits that decide how your project runs, week by week.

  1. 01

    One Team From Brief to Launch

    The people in your first workshop are the people who hand over the files. Nobody passes you on.

  2. 02

    Show the Work Every Thursday

    Every Thursday you see where the work stands, finished or not, so a wrong turn costs a week, never a month.

  3. 03

    Fixed Quotes, No Surprises

    One figure for an agreed scope, written down before we start. If the scope moves, we talk first and price second.

  4. 04

    Make It Usable After We Leave

    Guidelines, templates and a handover session, so your team runs the system without calling us.
