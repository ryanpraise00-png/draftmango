Cream blush, three ways: tapped, swept and stacked.

One pan of Cream Blush, three placements, three faces. Where to start, how much to take on your fingertip and when to stop, from the lesson our artists teach most.

Sofia LundLead Artist, hosting
Jump to the Kit
  1. 01Tapped: a fresh flush high on the cheek
  2. 02Swept: lifted, along the cheekbone
  3. 03Stacked: cream under powder, all day
Prep 01 · Tapped 02 · Swept 03 · Stacked The Kit

Cream blush is the most forgiving thing in a makeup bag, as long as it goes on before powder and on skin that is not still damp from moisturiser. Give your base two minutes, then start.

Everything below uses one shade. On fair to light skin, Rose reads as a cool, fresh pink; on medium to deep skin, Berry gives a warm, rich flush and Peach a sun-warmed one. If you are unsure, start sheer and build.

Way 01Tapped: a fresh flush high on the cheek

Smile, find the top of the apple, then move a finger’s width up and back. Tap the colour there with your ring finger, three light taps, and press outwards towards the ear. Your ring finger is the lightest, so it never takes too much.

Artist’s noteWarm the cream on the back of your hand first. It melts in faster and leaves no edge.

Way 02Swept: lifted, along the cheekbone

Pick up a little colour with the Cloud Powder Brush, then sweep it from the temple down along the top of the cheekbone, stopping level with the outer corner of your eye. It lifts the face and leaves the centre of the cheek fresh.

On the hero lookRose Clay on the lips, Cream Blush in Rose swept high: the same pink family, so the face reads as one.

Too much? Do not wipe it. Pat a clean damp sponge over the edge, or press a little Second Skin on top. It softens in seconds.

Way 03Stacked: cream under powder, all day

For a long day or a warm one, tap the cream on as in Way 01, set your base with Silk Setting Powder, then dust a whisper of powder blush in the same family on top. The cream gives the glow, the powder locks it in.

  • Cream first, always on bare base, never on powder.
  • Powder blush with the brush you used for setting, no new product needed.
  • Finish with two sprays of Dew Setting Mist to melt the layers together.
